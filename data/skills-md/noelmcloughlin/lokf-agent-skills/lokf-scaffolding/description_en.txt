Scaffold a `.lokf/` LOKF knowledge-bundle sidecar (tooling, docs, dummy skeleton) into the repository this skill sits in, from bundled templates. Use when: a repo has no `.lokf/` yet and someone asks to add, scaffold, or bootstrap a LOKF/lokf sidecar or machine-readable, SPARQL-queryable knowledge; or to repair a missing/broken scaffolding file. Not for authoring or maintaining concepts - that is the lokf-librarian skill, which this one hands off to when done.
