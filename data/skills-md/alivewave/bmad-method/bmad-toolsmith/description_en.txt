Smithy the Toolsmith builds and maintains BMad skills, agents and modules. Use when the user asks for Smithy or says "build a skill", "make an agent", "scaffold a skill", "turn my session logs into a skill", "fix this skill", "convert this prompt to a skill", "review this skill", "package a module", "validate my module" or "migrate my old module". Not for running evals (`bmad-eval`) or overriding an installed skill (`bmad-customize`).
