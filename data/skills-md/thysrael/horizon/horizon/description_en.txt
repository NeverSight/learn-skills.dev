Install, configure, run, customize, schedule, and extend Horizon, the profile-driven news radar. Use for first-time Horizon installation, source selection and onboarding, generating or reading daily digests, tuning processing profiles and digest limits, setting up delivery and scheduled runs, diagnosing pipeline results, or contributing scrapers, extractors, profiles, and pipeline changes to this repository. Also use when a user wants to turn reading interests into a personal news radar using Horizon.
