# Premium Art Direction

Work as an art director: find an idea specific to the context, compose the whole section, and use imagery and motion to communicate what matters. Apple's care in execution is a quality reference; each project's brand determines its aesthetic.

## Read and view the relevant references

Read the [art direction guide](references/art-direction.md) for shared principles, the header/hero reference catalog, prompting, motion, and review criteria. For footers, also read the [footer direction guide](references/footer-direction.md), including its image catalog and worked examples.

Choose relevant references and **open the actual images** with the available image viewer. Use [hero references](references/hero-blocks/) for opening and product compositions, and [footer references](references/footer-blocks/) for closing sections, full-width versus inset layouts, artwork extraction, and responsive treatments. Names and captions cannot replace visual inspection; opening every image is unnecessary.

Resolve these paths relative to this `SKILL.md`, independently of the active project's directory.

## Adapt the method to the current project

- Start from the message, audience, product, and user direction. Stay within the requested scope; a direction or documentation request may end with a proposal rather than a page implementation.
- Choose atmosphere, product demonstration, or a combination according to the idea. A scene need not literally depict the service. Photography, illustration, typography, and code-based compositions are all available choices.
- Name what each reference contributes: scale, framing, overlap, narrative, reading space, product presentation, or motion. Choose colors, lighting, subjects, typography, and layout for the current brand.
- Treat the header and hero as an opening, the body as a progression, and the footer as a deliberate conclusion with useful navigation and a clear next step. A footer can have as much compositional care as a hero without repeating its layout.
- When new artwork is needed, write a composition-specific prompt and use the integrated image-generation tool available in the session. If unavailable, disclose the limitation and provide the intended prompt and composition instead of silently substituting generic imagery.
- Build essential copy, navigation, legal information, controls, and interactive demonstrations in code. Separate artwork from usable interface; plan mobile, scrolling, text effects, and dynamic states as part of the composition.
- Evaluate artwork inside the real layout and refine it. For footers, compare a contained rectangle and a full-width treatment when that choice matters, then check a narrow-screen version. Do not generate every variant by default.

## Transfer principles, not a compulsory aesthetic

The Izekai footer examples use manga, fantasy, streetwear, and dramatic scenery because that was their brand context. These subjects, cats, portals, palettes, logos, and fixed reading-zone percentages are **not requirements for other projects**. The reusable method is to coordinate artwork, reading space, navigation, brand presence, and responsive behavior. Adapt it just as readily to an accounting firm, a hospitality site, or a software product.

The bundled screenshots and artwork are study references, not production assets or evidence of the current project's customers, features, or results. Embedded Portuguese text and brand names remain in the original images as visual evidence; the instructions are in English. Do not publish a bundled image unchanged as another project's identity.

Osmo's infinite carousel is a behavior reported by the user. Other static captures establish composition, not unobserved animation. The footer source specimen documents a past implementation; its framework dependencies and content are not a universal template.

Save new prompts, generated images, and implementation files in the active project according to its conventions. This skill holds the reusable guide and reference library without depending on the original Izekai checkout or `.orca-tmp` directory.
