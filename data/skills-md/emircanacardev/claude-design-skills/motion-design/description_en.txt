Designs purposeful UI motion for microinteractions and transitions, covering purpose, duration by distance and size, easing for enter, exit and movement, choreography and stagger, spring versus bezier curves, reduced-motion fallbacks and performance, with CSS and Framer Motion (now Motion) snippets. Use when asked to add animations, transitions, hover effects or microinteractions, or when motion feels slow, janky or excessive. Not for defining loading, empty and error states; use ui-states.
