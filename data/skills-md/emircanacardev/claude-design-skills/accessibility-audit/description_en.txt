Audits a page, component or design against WCAG 2.2 AA, covering contrast, keyboard paths, visible focus, semantics and landmarks, heading order, ARIA usage, form labels and errors, target size, motion and alt text, and reports each issue with its success criterion, severity and fix, followed by corrected code. Use when asked to check accessibility, run an a11y or WCAG audit, or make something work with screen readers. Not for general visual feedback; use design-critique.
