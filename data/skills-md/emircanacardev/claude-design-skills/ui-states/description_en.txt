Designs every state a screen or component can reach beyond the happy path, including first-use, user-cleared and no-results empty states, loading with skeletons, spinners and optimistic updates, inline, page and toast errors with recovery, success, partial, offline and permission-denied, with copy, visuals and code. Use when asked to add loading or empty states, or to handle errors and edge cases. Not for form field validation (use form-design).
