Build a living 3D world of any real place, after studying it. Studies the place first (its trees and where each grows, what grows under them, its birds, the colour of its buildings, what happens there), writes that into a profile, then calls render_world, which builds one HTML file you can walk through, hear and share. Use when someone says "build a living world of <place>", "make a living world", "a world I can fly through", "make it look like a game", "real trees", "the forest in 3D", or wants to stand in a place rather than look down on it.
