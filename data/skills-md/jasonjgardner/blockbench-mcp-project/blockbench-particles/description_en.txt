Add particle effects (smoke, fire, sparks, magic, glow, dust, snow, drips, bubbles, bursts) to Blockbench models with MCP tools. Covers Bedrock/Snowstorm particle JSON, presets and design knobs, locators, particle keyframes on animations, Blockbench's Animate-mode preview, and delivery to Bedrock resource packs (client entity particle_effects) and GeckoLib, plus fallbacks for glTF, Java block and Hytale targets that cannot carry particles. Use when a model needs an emitter, VFX, particle effect, locator, Snowstorm file, particle_effects keyframe, or effect polish in a deliverable.
