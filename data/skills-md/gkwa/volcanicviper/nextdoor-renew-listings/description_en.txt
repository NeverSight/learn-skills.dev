Renew the Nextdoor For Sale & Free listings that are about to expire, using the Claude in Chrome browser tools in the user's signed-in Chrome. Renews only listings labeled Expiring, including items pending pickup, and never marks sold or deletes. Use when asked to renew Nextdoor listings, when a Nextdoor email says a listing expires soon, or when asked to keep the Nextdoor for sale listings from expiring.
