Report the bulk fermentation status of a Zephyr bake as one terse line, by reading the bake log and running chargingcheetah and ferment.py. The line carries elapsed time, expected total time, time remaining and the expected finish time, the initial and target dough volume, the estimated and target rise, and the average dough temperature. Use when asked about any one of those for a Zephyr key, such as how long is left on bulk, when the bulk will be done, how long it has been, what volume or rise to expect, how far along the rise is, what the dough temperature is, or how the bulk ferment is going.
