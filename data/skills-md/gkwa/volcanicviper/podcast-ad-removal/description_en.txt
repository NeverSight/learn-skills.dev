Download a podcast episode, transcribe it, find the sponsor and ad blocks by reading the transcript, cut them out, and copy the ad-free MP3 into the Syncthing podcasts folder, which syncs to the Pixel's Audiobooks folder for playback in the Voice app. Takes the podcast name, and the most recent episode unless the user names a particular one. Use when asked to remove ads from a podcast, clean today's episode of a show, pre-download a podcast without ads, or run the ad cleaning for a named show.
