Turn a short bake utterance such as 'bake two for Dana' into one three-day all-day entry on the Google bake calendar, running from the start day through the bake day so overlapping bakes show as stacked bars. The day named is the handoff day, which is the bake day, as in 'bake two for Dana on Tuesday', and the start day is computed two days earlier. A day after 'starting' sets the start day instead, and with no day the start is today. Also removes a bake, deleting its entry, or both entries of an older two-entry bake, as in 'delete the bake for Dana'. Use when the user says to bake some number of loaves for a person, asks to put a bake on the bake calendar, dictates a bake order with a count and a name, or asks to delete, undo or cancel a bake.
