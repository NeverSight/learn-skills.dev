Add a product to the shopping list for a store by setting that store's front matter boolean to true in the product note in the Obsidian vault, creating the product note first when none exists. With no store named, it goes on the amazon_fresh catch-all list. Use when asked to add something to the shopping list, with or without a store, put an item on the list for a store, or mark a product to buy at Trader Joe's, Costco, PCC, or any other store.
