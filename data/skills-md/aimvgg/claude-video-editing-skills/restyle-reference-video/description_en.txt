Restyles a reference video shot for shot around a new subject, keeping its cuts, timing, camera moves, look and song and swapping the person, text and story. Rebuilds hype videos, music videos, ads, reels and TikToks. Measures the reference first, keeps its audio as the clock, and generates only the shots that have to change. Also replaces a voice or announcer in a clip without losing the music, and cuts podcast clips onto the beat. Use when someone links a video and says 'make this but about X', 'remake this', 'recreate this edit', 'restyle this music video', 'do this shot for shot with my face', 'make a hype video like this one' or 'do this style for our launch', or asks to 'remove the announcer from this clip', 'replace the voiceover with our lines' or 'make these podcast clips hit on the beat'. Not for putting a person into one clip with no edit to rebuild (v2v-video-to-video-swap), finishing shots or adding a film look (clean-up-ai-video), or turning footage into a new art style.
