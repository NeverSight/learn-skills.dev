Looks up how AI music videos and hype videos were made on AIMV (aimv.gg), with the recipes, prompts, models, tools and repos behind each one. Writes the recipe for a finished video as a public repo with RECIPE.md, an eight-section making-of guide and a storyboard page, plus the video entry YAML for github.com/aimvgg/awesome-ai-music-videos, checked against the schema and scrubbed for secrets and private paths. Use when someone asks 'how was this AI music video made', 'find a recipe that uses Wan', 'which model do people use for lip sync', 'write up how we made it', 'make a making-of guide for our hype video', 'publish our recipe' or 'submit this to AIMV'.
