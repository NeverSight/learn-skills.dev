Finds the moment in a YouTube video or podcast where a person says something or a topic comes up, quotes the exact words with a timestamped link, and, when a shell is available, cuts each moment into a clip that starts and ends between words, with its source logged. Searches by speaker through the Arcmira MCP server when it is connected, and works without it. Also cuts a span from a YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or X link, or downloads a short reference video whole. Use for 'find clips of X saying Y', 'where did she talk about Z', a soundbite or quote from a podcast or interview, footage of someone on stage for a hype video, or 'cut 1:02:10 to 1:02:18 from this link'.
