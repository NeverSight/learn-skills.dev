Gets weather and air quality for any place without an API key from Open-Meteo - current conditions, an hourly and daily forecast up to 16 days (temperature, feels-like, rain and its chance, wind and gusts, UV, sunrise and sunset), past weather for any day since 1940, and air quality now and ahead (European and US AQI, PM2.5, PM10, ozone, NO2, pollen) - for a city, address, landmark or coordinates, with the resolved place shown. Use when the user asks about the weather, temperature, rain, snow or wind, a forecast for a place or date, what the weather was on a past date or in a past month, whether to take an umbrella or how to dress, or whether the air is clean or safe to run or go outside, AQI, PM2.5, smog or pollen today ("какая погода в", "будет ли дождь", "какой сейчас воздух"). Not for severe-weather warnings, climate facts or classifications (wiki-lookup), climate projections, marine or aviation forecasts.
