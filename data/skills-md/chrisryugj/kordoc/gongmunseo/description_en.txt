An orchestrator skill that takes content and outputs Korean administrative official documents (drafts, implementation documents, reports, plans, notices, minutes) in the standard HWPX format. Based on the Ministry of the Interior and Safety’s “Administrative Work Operation Manual” and implementation rules, it applies eight‑level item numbering (1. 가. 1) 가) (1) (가) ① ㉮), second‑line indent alignment, official margins (top 20 mm, bottom 10 mm, left/right 20 mm), Myeongjo 15 pt font, line spacing, and date/attachment/end/amount notation rules, converting them into Kordoc’s official‑document mode. Triggers — “Write a report for me,” “Make a draft,” “Export as an official document,” “Write a plan,” “Organize minutes to hwpx,” “In official‑document format,” “Create a notice,” “Official document hwpx,” “Write an administrative document.” Simple markdown‑to‑hwpx conversion (format‑agnostic) uses kordoc generate directly. Filling blanks in existing hwp/hwpx templates uses kordoc fill.
