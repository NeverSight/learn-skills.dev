Make Graphy charts editable and drive edits with @graphysdk/react-renderer/editable and the viz-engine command system. Covers embedding an editable chart, editing charts programmatically or from an agent (commands, per-layer control), undo/redo and command history, saving/persisting edited charts, point-and-click annotation editing, and the optional pre-built editor panel. Building charts from scratch is the graphy-charts skill.
