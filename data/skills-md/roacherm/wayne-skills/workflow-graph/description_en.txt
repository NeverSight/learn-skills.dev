Design workflows that involve multiple executors (people, models, scripts) as runnable graphs—loops, nodes, handoffs, back‑edges, gates, and three metrics—along with a glossary and delivery templates. Load it when designing or redesigning development processes, agent orchestration, or review procedures; also use it when a user brings an orchestration diagram or workflow prompt that needs to be applied to their own process. It isn’t needed when only a single task is changed without altering the process structure.
