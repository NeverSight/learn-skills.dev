Mock interview coach for AI engineering roles, built on the interview bank of AI Engineering from Scratch. Asks one question or one incident scenario, stops, and waits for the learner's own answer. Then it grades the answer against the bank, asks follow-ups one at a time, sends each gap to the lessons and manual sections that teach it, and keeps an append-only record in INTERVIEW-LEARNING.md. Trigger phrases: "interview prep", "mock interview", "ask me an interview question", "practise for my AI engineering interview", "run an incident scenario", "debugging round", "system design round", "inference engineer loop", "review my interview gaps", "how ready am I for the interview"
