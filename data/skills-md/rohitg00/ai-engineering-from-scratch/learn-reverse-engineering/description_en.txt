Focused interactive tutor for the Reverse Engineering path in AI Engineering from Scratch. Start or resume this route when a learner wants to read a compiled program: file formats, disassembly, control flow, lifting, evidence, proof, calling conventions, compiler idioms, linking, types, debugging, bytecode, diffing, symbolic execution, and AI-assisted analysis. Teaches one lesson per invocation and records evidence in REVERSE-ENGINEERING-LEARNING.md.
