Argues against review findings before anyone fixes them. For each must-fix finding it tries to reproduce it, asks who actually hits it and how bad that is, and picks the cheapest answer, which can be cutting the feature or leaving the finding open. It rejects findings whose only fix is a defensive check nothing shows is needed, and sends fixes that need a new rule or special case to the user. Read-only. Use between a review and its fixes, as the autopilot's falsify step, or for "falsify these findings", "do we really need to fix these", or "which of these review comments matter".
