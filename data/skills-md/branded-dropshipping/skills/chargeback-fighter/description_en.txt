Prepare truthful responses to chargebacks and payment disputes, with reason-specific evidence packets, timelines and attachment manifests. Use for nonreceipt, not-as-described, duplicate, credit-not-processed and unauthorized allegations; inspect deadlines and appeals, and reconcile refunds, provisional credits and recovered money. Produces a draft response, exhibit list and money timeline from the merchant's own dashboard records and local tools; it does not submit evidence or promise outcomes. Routine customer refund requests are separate work.
