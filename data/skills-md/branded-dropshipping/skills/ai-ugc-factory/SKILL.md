# Creator-style production

Deliver the requested brief, shot plan, repair, edit or media package, with human footage, synthetic performers or both. A creator video that sounds natural and handles the product cleanly can still fail if the script never gives a reason to buy, so check the selling spine as well as the performance. Start at the unresolved decision; a small caption change does not require a new creative strategy or casting exercise.

Identify what is fixed: message, factual claims, opening, product, selected performer/take, destination and deliverable role. Reuse adequate context. Ask only for missing information that changes the work, such as whether “raw” means separate camera clips or a clean assembled export. A synthetic speaker cannot supply an actual customer experience the user has not established.

Read connected material only when the work crosses its boundary. Keep the reason for each shot visible: what must the viewer hear, see or understand here? A-roll carries the main spoken performance; B-roll supplies a view, action or context that earns its place. More motion is not automatically more useful coverage.

## Produce something usable

For a creator brief, use [the template](references/creator-brief.md) selectively. For a repair, inspect the supplied version and locate the failure in time. For generation, prepare the approved inputs and operation in the available tool. If an integration is missing, return usable local assets, the prepared request and the exact prerequisite.

Preserve originals and accepted versions. Deliver selected sources, the edit plan, subtitles where used and requested exports; retain a native project when supplied or required. For multiple versions, link sources, candidates and review decisions in a short selection note. A small correction needs no ledger.

## Judge the right thing

Check spoken content, performer delivery, physical action, continuity and finishing separately. A stable face does not prove a stable product; a correct final frame does not prove a valid action; good captions cannot fix wrong speech. Listen and view continuously where those judgments require it. Frame samples, transcripts and decode checks have narrower uses; state the actual coverage and leave uninspected consequential issues unresolved.

Carry forward task authorization within its scope. For UI work, see [browser access](references/browser-runtime-and-sessions.md). Login does not authorize private uploads, paid generation, publication or contacting creators.

State what was produced, which version it uses, what was inspected and what remains undone. Supply a [revision note](references/revision-note.md) when new work is needed; do not send it automatically. A finished creator video may itself be the ad, so its script, performance, footage and revisions stay here. Hook selection, product research and campaign allocation are separate tasks. Consume an approved destination unchanged; new commerce sites or partner-referral links belong to the commerce workflow, not media finishing.
