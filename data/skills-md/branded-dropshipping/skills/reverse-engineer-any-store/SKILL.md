# Reverse-engineer any store

A teardown answers a specific question. What is this competitor selling, to whom, with what argument and offer, through which route, and which parts of that could this user actually reproduce? Deliver what the question needs: a concise brief, a scoped ad comparison, a route and offer table, or an original adaptation brief. Reuse supplied artifacts and settled choices; one offer question doesn't need a market-wide dossier.

Public evidence tells you a lot. You can see who is sending traffic, which buying reason each ad argues, the unit counts and renewal intervals, the shipping and refund rules, and sometimes how buyers fared afterward. It doesn't show spend, profit or supplier terms. Ad publication is not spend, spend is not profit, and a policy is not proof of execution. Read the signals as reasons to look harder, and say what each one would need in order to mean more.

## Investigate and deliver

For live inspection, use an available browser; see [browser access](references/browser-runtime-and-sessions.md) if needed. Keep queries generic; check whether a spy tool publishes the businesses you look up.

Identify the Page and ads, preserve the country, filter and date scope, inspect the contrasts that matter, and follow the actual offer. Routine reversible cart inspection implied by the task needs no extra confirmation. Purchases, accounts, sensitive submissions, publishing and commitments are separate actions. When a branch is blocked, say so; never bypass access or invent a post-purchase flow.

Separate what's visible, what someone reported, and what you infer, and keep that uncertainty inside the delivered artifact rather than in a footnote. Cite the public sources actually inspected. The optional [brief template](references/competitor-brief.md) fits most teardowns. For repeated captures, put entity, date, market and filter scope, observed offer and open interpretation side by side, and don't mistake a changed observation for a proven change in spend or performance.

Product selection, sourcing, checkout repair, offer economics and creative production are separate jobs; hand them the specific evidence and the unresolved prerequisite. Whop setup and referrals belong to the payment/build workflow when requested. This investigation needs no Whop account, link generator or platform promotion.
