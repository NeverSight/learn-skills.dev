Looks at a local business's site, or the page its ad opens, the way a customer sees it on a phone, and reports what the first screen is missing (the number with tap-to-call, the promise the ad made, the star rating and other proof, a way to book) plus the site faults that quietly break trust, as a short ranked list with one thing to fix first. Use when the site or the ads aren't making the phone ring, when someone wants a page checked on a phone, or when sizing up a prospect's site before a pitch.
