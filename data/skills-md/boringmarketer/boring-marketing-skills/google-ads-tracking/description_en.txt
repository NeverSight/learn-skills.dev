Audit and repair the conversion signal in a local service business's Google Ads account so it counts the calls, phone taps, form fills and bookings that are real leads, and bids on them. Maps every path a lead takes, reads which conversion actions are primary and which goals actually bid, fixes the tag and proves it with Tag Assistant, then decides the bid strategy and whether more budget would help at all. Use when Google Ads shows zero or strange conversions, when campaigns bid on clicks, before switching to Maximize conversions, before raising a budget, or when taking over an account from someone else.
