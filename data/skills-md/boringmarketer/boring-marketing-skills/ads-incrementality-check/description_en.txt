Test whether an ad campaign makes the phone ring or only takes credit for calls that would come anyway, using a planned pause and the business's own phone log as the denominator. Plans the pause window, cleans the call log into four daily measures, controls for season and day of week, reads before against during against after, and turns the result into a keep, cut or resume decision with a cost per extra call. Use when Performance Max, branded search or Google Business Profile actions claim most of the conversions, when the platform reports leads the business can't find, or before resuming or raising a campaign's budget.
