For agencies and freelancers. Finds the owner of a local business, then writes a short cold email about ONE thing that's broken (with the evidence from their own phone screen or their own ad), offers one scoped fix with a way to show it worked, and plans two follow-ups. Includes a fix menu an agency can sell. Use when you have a prospect and need the first email, want to pitch a local business, or need to find who owns it.
