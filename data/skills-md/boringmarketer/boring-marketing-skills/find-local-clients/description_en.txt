For agencies and freelancers. Picks one trade and one town, gathers the businesses from free sources (search results, license lists, directories, the map pack checked by hand), and ranks who is losing the most customers, with a can-pay tier and the single biggest problem per business backed by evidence anyone can re-check. Use when you need local clients to pitch, want a prospect list for a trade and a town, or ask which businesses in a town need marketing help.
