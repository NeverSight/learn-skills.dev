Independent translation skill training for workplace trainees. Two modes: Coach mode (Filtering → Decapsulation → Logic manifestation → Re‑expression → Cultural adaptation, with coaching guidance at each step) and Fast mode (AI directly produces the translation result and explains step by step what adjustments were made and why). The decapsulation stage adapts to the trainee’s level—higher proficiency requires less decapsulation. Trigger words: translation skill training, translation training, practice translation, translation training.
