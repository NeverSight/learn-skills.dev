Independent translation skill training for workplace learners. Two modes: Coach mode (Filtering → De-shelling → Logical manifestation → Re-expression → Cultural adaptation, with coaching guidance at each step) and Fast mode (AI directly provides translation results and gradually explains what adjustments were made at each step and why). The de-shelling stage adapts to the learner’s proficiency—the higher the level, the less de-shelling is needed. Trigger words: translation skill training, translation training, practice translation, translation training.
