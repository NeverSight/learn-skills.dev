Independent logical thinking training for workplace learners. Two modes: exploration mode (the problem isn’t clear yet, requiring starting from identifying the true issue to constructing a pyramid) and organization mode (the content already exists, just needing help to reorganize it using a pyramid structure). AI acts as a Socratic coach, using probing questions to guide learners to complete their own thinking. Trigger words: logical thinking training, logic training, practice logic, build pyramid, logic training, help me organize.
