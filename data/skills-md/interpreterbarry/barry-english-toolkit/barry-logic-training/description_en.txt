Independent logical thinking training for workplace trainees. Two modes: exploration mode (the thought isn’t clear yet, requiring starting from identifying the real question to building a pyramid) and consolidation mode (the content already exists, just needing help to reorganize it using a pyramid structure). AI acts as a Socratic coach, using probing questions to guide the trainee to complete the thinking themselves. Trigger words: logical thinking training, logic training, practice logic, build a pyramid, logic training, help me sort it out.
