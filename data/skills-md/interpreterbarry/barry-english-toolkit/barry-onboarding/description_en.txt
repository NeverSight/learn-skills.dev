Unified entry point of Barry's workplace English toolbox. Called when a new learner first interacts with the toolbox, displaying a welcome message and guiding them through the complete onboarding three steps (profile → assessment → solution); also called when a returning learner needs to redo onboarding (due to changed circumstances). This is the Skill that should be invoked the first time a learner starts—tying together the entire toolbox usage so the learner doesn’t need to know the names of each atomic Skill. Trigger words: start, launch, start learning English, English training, barry, barry‑onboarding, english onboarding, how do I start, I want to learn english, welcome.
