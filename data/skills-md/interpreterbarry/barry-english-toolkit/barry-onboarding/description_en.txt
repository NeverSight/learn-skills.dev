The unified entry point of Barry’s Workplace English Toolbox. When a new learner first accesses the toolbox, it is invoked to display a welcome message and guide them through the complete onboarding in three steps (profile → assessment → solution); it is also invoked when an existing learner needs to rerun the onboarding due to changes in circumstances. **This is the Skill that should be invoked when a learner launches for the first time**—it strings together the usage of the entire toolbox, so the learner does not need to know the names of each atomic Skill. Trigger words: start, launch, start learning English, English training, barry, barry‑onboarding, english onboarding, how do I start, I want to learn english, welcome, welcome.
