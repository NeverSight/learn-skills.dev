It is used when creating EPUBs or when verifying and correcting them for upload to RIDI Books and other domestic readers. Use it when, after Calibre conversion, the table of contents shows as “work without a table of contents,” or when checking and fixing missing NCX, CSS constraints, title‑size hierarchy, or left‑alignment issues; also when checking for quotation‑mark changes or duplicate entries in the table of contents in Korean EPUBs from Pandoc or Calibre. Use it to restore bold, subheadings, and block‑quote formatting from scanned PDFs, or when bold disappears in Calibre DOCX conversions. In Korean EPUBs and DOCXs, use it to display semantic emphasis (em) as underline, strong emphasis as bold, and to handle publication titles with 《》 and 〈〉.
