Review the changes since a fixed point (commit, branch, tag, or merge-base) along four axes: Standards (documented coding standards), Spec (fidelity to the originating issue/spec), Simplification (less complexity, duplication, or reimplementation), and Optimization (performance and scaling opportunities such as N+1 queries, batching, caching, and overload risks). Runs four reviews in parallel sub-agents and reports them side by side. Use when the user wants to review a branch, a PR, work-in-progress changes, or asks to "review since X".
