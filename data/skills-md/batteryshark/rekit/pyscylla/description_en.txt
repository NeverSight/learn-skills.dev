Dump, realign, and rebuild Import Address Tables of Windows PE binaries through Rekit's Python adapter for the external libscylla engine. Supports process enumeration, live dump, IAT search/parse/fix, XML tree round-trip, and reference scanning. Windows-only; bring your own arch-matched libscylla DLL and comply with its upstream GPL-3.0-only license. DYNAMIC on a live process (ReadProcessMemory) but does not execute the target.
