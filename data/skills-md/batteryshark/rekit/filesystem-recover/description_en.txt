Recover allocated, deleted, or all files from an ext, NTFS, FAT, or other Sleuth Kit-supported filesystem at an explicit image sector offset. Use after disk-image-scan validates a filesystem start. Runs tsk_recover without mounting the source, emits an fls metadata-address/MFT record-to-name map, safely renames unambiguous OrphanFile-record placeholders from surviving metadata, and hashes recovered files.
