Build, integrate, and maintain a local knowledge base, retain original documents, and create traceable indexes. It is used for “enterprise knowledge base,” “company document organization,” “product library, customer library, skill library,” “turning folders into a knowledge base,” “self‑media knowledge base,” and “knowledge base health check.” Supports three modes: enterprise triple‑library, general, and self‑media. In enterprise mode, first understand the company and its documents, categorize by product, client, and team SOP, and deliver the content and document gaps for each library. Compatible with WorkBuddy, Codex, and Claude Code; does not handle cloud RAG, automatic synchronization, or unauthorized file migration.
