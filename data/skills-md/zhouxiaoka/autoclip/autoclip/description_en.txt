Use AutoClip to turn a YouTube/Bilibili link or a local long video (lecture, interview, podcast, course) into finished videos, thumbnails, publishing copy, and a ZIP publishing package for each platform. Use this when a user wants to “produce a video,” “create a thumbnail,” “write publishing copy,” “package and publish,” or “make a short video from this link.” For raw highlight clips, compilations, or when sending old clips to Upload‑Post, do not use this instruction; instead read references/legacy.md.
