Fixes the Korean (Hangul) that coding agents get wrong, in web UI and in documents of any length. Typography for web UI: line breaking (break-all, keep-all, overflow-wrap), letter-spacing (자간), line-height (행간), font stacks, lang, synthetic italic/bold, numbers, truncation. Writing: the style each situation calls for (app screens, sites, product pages, reports, pitch decks, notices, e-mails, press releases, READMEs and docs, job posts), meaning the speech level, how sentences are built and the order things come in, plus the faults that are specific to Korean: spelling and spacing (돼요, 할 수, 회의 중), honorifics on things (커피 나오셨습니다, 결제 가능하세요), translationese (~함에 있어, 필요로 하다, 보여지는), mixed speech levels, punctuation and dates, and notes left in a document. Testable rules from W3C klreq and CSS Text, 「한글 맞춤법」, 「외래어 표기법」 and 국립국어원 guides, KRDS / Apps in Toss / SEED, OmD's measurement of 67 Korean services, and Korean studies. APPLY while writing; AUDIT with two deterministic checkers. Triggers: '한글 타이포', '줄바꿈 이상해', '최악 데이터로 깨뜨려 봐', '/hangul break', 'Pretendard', 'keep-all', 'UX 라이팅', '문구 다듬어줘', '에러 문구', '맞춤법', '띄어쓰기', '번역투', '해요체', '보고서 문구', '피치덱', 'PPT 문구', '공지문', '보도자료', '이메일 문구', '채용 공고', 'Korean typography', 'Korean UX writing', 'Hangul'. Writing in another language → omd-humanize; translation → omd-locale-adapter; motion and contrast → omd-feel. A project's DESIGN.md type tokens, voice or speech level win over these defaults.
