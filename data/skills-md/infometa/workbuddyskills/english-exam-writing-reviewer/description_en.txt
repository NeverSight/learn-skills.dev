Traceable correction skill for mainstream Chinese English exam essays. Covers six exam levels — the national College English Test Band 4/6 (CET‑4 / CET‑6) plus the national postgraduate entrance exam English (Postgrad 1A / 1B / 2A / 2B), where Section A is an application letter and Section B is a short essay. The scoring strictly aligns with each exam’s official rubric using a “grade‑based + overall impression” method (CET grades 14/11/8/5/2; postgraduate grades 5/4/3/2/1). Every score can be traced back to the original grade descriptor plus the specific evidence sentences from the essay (rationale_trace field). Feedback is provided entirely in Chinese in a coaching style, including the grade‑adjustment and point‑deduction rules, grade boundary determinations, upgrade pathways (with concrete lexical replacement suggestions based on a vocabulary bank, up to five per attempt), and a JSON + Chinese HTML correction report (including an SVG radar chart).

Unique features: detection of copied sentences of eight or more words in Postgraduate Section A Directions, simultaneous multi‑level correction across different exams, low‑frequency/ out‑of‑scope item calibration_status to prevent hallucination. The skill is triggered when a user submits an English essay and requests correction/score/grade. Trigger phrases include: “批改四级/六级作文”, “CET 作文评分”, “四六级写作批改”, “考研英语作文批改”, “英一/英二作文打分”, “考研一图画作文”, “考研二图表作文”, “考研 A 节书信批改”, “给我打个分”, “档次是怎么定的”, “怎么升档”, “按官方标准评一下”, “字数不够会不会扣分”, “review CET‑4/CET‑6 writing”, “grade CET essay”, “grade postgraduate English essay”, “score this kaoyan essay”. Whether the user uses the internal ID “Postgrad2B” or natural language like “考研英二大作文”, the skill should be activated.

Strictly do not perform: ① translation; ② IELTS/TOEFL/GAOKAO/GRE/ TEM‑4/8 tasks (use the corresponding independent skill); ③ pure vocabulary drills; ④ sentence‑level grammar polishing (use tools like Grammarly); ⑤ reading‑comprehension/listening/translation Q&A; ⑥ real‑world email polishing (non‑exam essays); ⑦ scoring of creative writing such as poetry or novels.
