Use when the user wants to complete a daily journal or wind down, requests a daily chief-of-staff review or a weekly or quarterly review, asks to set up, list, add, update, or remove chief-of-staff source mappings, approves, resumes, or otherwise decides visible chief-of-staff actions, or another workflow requests current cross-source chief-of-staff context.
