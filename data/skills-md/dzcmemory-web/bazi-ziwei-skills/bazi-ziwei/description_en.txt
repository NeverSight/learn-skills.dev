Bazi + Ziwei Dou Shu AI charting and comprehensive analysis. Used when users provide their birth details (Gregorian/Lunar date, time, gender) and ask questions related to Bazi, Ziwei, destiny, natal chart, yearly luck, and major cycles. Based on two independent open‑source charting libraries, mingpan (Apache‑2.0) and iztro (MIT), plus the enrichBazi completion layer for precise charting (preventing LLMs from generating erroneous charts), it supports on‑demand independent analysis of Bazi, independent analysis of Ziwei, and cross‑validation between the two charts.
