Convert Markdown documents and associated local images into a single-file interactive HTML that preserves content fidelity, looks attractive, and can be read offline. This is used when users want to turn documents such as requirement specifications, operational reports, design proposals, tutorials, etc., into reading pages, or need to use a table of contents, flowcharts, diagrams, and comparative analyses to aid understanding of the documents. The structure is designed based on the content, without forcing a fixed template; it is not intended for building backend‑driven business applications.
