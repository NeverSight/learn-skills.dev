Apply when implementing a VTEX Payment Provider Protocol connector for Point of Sale (POS) payments on VTEX Sales App. Covers Venda Direta Credito and Venda Direta Debito manifest methods, the two-phase Payment App challenge (terminal connector then wait-for-confirmation), null card data on Create Payment, asynchronous undefined status, timeout pairing of secondsWaiting and delayToCancel, and callback payloads with cardBrand, firstDigits, and lastDigits. Use for building or debugging PPP + POS integrations that charge a physical card on a payment terminal.
