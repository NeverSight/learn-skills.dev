# Cluster Feedback Into Jobs

## Contract

Cluster on the progress customers seek in a situation, not on the requested feature or shared nouns. Preserve ambiguous items and the mapping back to raw evidence.

## Workflow

1. Define scope, segments, dates, sources, and the decision this clustering informs.
2. For each item, record the situation, desired progress, blocked outcome, current workaround, urgency trigger, and literal request. Leave fields unknown rather than inventing context.
3. Draft a provisional job for sufficiently rich items. Put thin or ambiguous items in an unresolved queue.
4. Cluster items whose situations and desired progress are materially alike, even when their proposed solutions differ.
5. Split clusters that contain independently shippable jobs or distinct causal barriers. Merge clusters that differ only in wording.
6. Rate each cluster on evidence volume, source diversity, severity, trend, strategic relevance, and confidence; keep these axes separate.
7. Produce a receipt mapping every item to a cluster or unresolved reason.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first, then use signals or structured extractions for the evidence set. Filter direct customer voice explicitly when required, preserve signal and call IDs, and use aggregation only for counts after the clusters are defined.

## Output

Return job clusters, job statements, evidence counts, representative evidence, workarounds, triggers, ratings, unresolved items, and the complete item-to-cluster ledger.

## Quality Gate

Reject labels such as `dashboard requests` or `export features` unless they describe the customer progress. Do not rank roadmap priority from volume alone.
