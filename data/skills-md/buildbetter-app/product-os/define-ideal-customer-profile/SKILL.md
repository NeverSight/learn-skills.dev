# Define Ideal Customer Profile

## Contract

Define the customers most likely to realize and sustain value, not the largest logo list or a demographic stereotype. Separate organization fit, buying context, user roles, and disqualifiers.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the product's value mechanism, strategic goal, and horizon.
2. Segment by job, situation, behavior, environment, or constraint before firmographics.
3. Compare segments on problem intensity, urgency, existing alternatives, ability to adopt, time to value, expansion or retention potential, acquisition efficiency, and strategic learning.
4. Analyze best customers, churned customers, stalled deals, non-users, and outliers. Do not use current customers alone.
5. Define company attributes, trigger events, buyer and user roles, required conditions, disqualifiers, and observable signals.
6. Choose a primary ICP and explicitly defer adjacent segments.
7. Specify evidence confidence and tests for weak assumptions; define review triggers.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use people and account properties, calls, signals, survey responses, project evidence, and customer histories where accessible. Respect access boundaries and do not infer sensitive traits.

## Output

Return primary ICP, job and trigger, firm and environment attributes, roles, value and adoption conditions, disqualifiers, evidence, adjacent segments, messaging implications, and validation plan.

## Quality Gate

Reject an ICP that is only industry plus company size, includes mutually incompatible segments, or lacks disqualifiers and evidence of realized value.
