# Manage Product Sunset

## Contract

Sunset is a customer transition and risk program, not a deletion date. Confirm authority, affected populations, contractual obligations, recoverability, and evidence before irreversible actions.

## Workflow

1. Define the candidate, rationale, decision owner, status, target dates, reversibility, and alternatives to retirement.
2. Inventory usage, revenue, cost, strategic fit, reliability, dependencies, integrations, contracts, data, security, and customer evidence.
3. Segment affected customers and internal teams by impact, migration complexity, entitlement, geography, and support need.
4. Choose deprecation stages and entry or exit criteria: decision, internal freeze, announcement, migration, restricted access, shutdown, retention, and deletion.
5. Design alternatives, migration tooling, validation, data export or retention, billing changes, support escalation, and exception policy.
6. Plan communications by audience and channel with clear dates, impact, action, assistance, and confirmation. Do not send without explicit approval.
7. Define monitoring, readiness reviews, rollback or extension criteria, final verification, and post-sunset cleanup.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use account and people context, calls, signals, surveys, projects, tickets, documents, and knowledge gaps to assess impact. Never change customer access, billing, lifecycle state, or send communications without exact approval and a readback.

## Output

Return evidence and decision brief, affected-customer matrix, dependency inventory, staged timeline, migration and support plan, communications, exceptions, risk register, approval gates, rollback, and verification plan.

## Quality Gate

Reject a sunset with unknown affected users, no viable transition, unresolved contract or data obligations, unowned support, or irreversible action before verified readiness.
