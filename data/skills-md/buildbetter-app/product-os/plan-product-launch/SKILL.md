# Plan Product Launch

## Contract

Match launch intensity to customer impact, reversibility, novelty, audience, and risk. Shipment, availability, announcement, adoption, and outcome are different states. Never activate, publish, message, or enroll an audience without explicit authority.

## Workflow

1. Define target customers, outcome, launch type, exact release artifact, channels, markets, constraints, and success state.
2. Choose rollout and communication tiers: internal, silent, limited, soft, broad, or major. Explain the choice.
3. Build workstreams for product, engineering, design, data, support, success, sales, marketing, legal or compliance, finance, and operations as relevant.
4. Define entry criteria, dependencies, owners, dates, decision gates, test evidence, support materials, and escalation paths.
5. Plan audience eligibility, migration, access, feature control, monitoring, rollback, incident handling, and customer recovery.
6. Define messaging from positioning and proof; distinguish internal enablement, customer notification, public announcement, and lifecycle communication.
7. Define leading adoption, quality, guardrail, business, and qualitative measures with review cadence.
8. Run a readiness review and premortem. Record no-go conditions and the exact approval required to launch.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use customer evidence, surveys, projects, tickets, documents, knowledge gaps, release context, and changelog or portal tools only when available. Publishing or sending requires explicit user approval and a readback of audience and content.

## Output

Return launch brief, tier, audience, workstream plan, readiness checklist, owners, dependencies, rollout and rollback, support plan, communications, measures, no-go criteria, approval state, and post-launch reviews.

## Quality Gate

Reject plans that equate deployment with launch, lack rollback or support ownership, omit affected customers, or hide audience activation inside a setup step.
