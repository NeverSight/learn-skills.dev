# Analyze Competitors

## Contract

Compare how customers solve the job, not only vendors that share a category. Use current primary sources for unstable claims. Distinguish verified facts, customer perceptions, and analyst inference.

## Workflow

1. Define the customer, job, decision, geography, and time horizon.
2. Enumerate alternatives: direct products, adjacent tools, internal workflows, services, spreadsheets, and doing nothing.
3. Collect comparable evidence on target customer, value claim, workflow, switching costs, proof, pricing model, distribution, and constraints.
4. Add customer evidence about why alternatives are chosen, rejected, or replaced.
5. Compare on decision criteria that matter to the target segment, not a maximal feature matrix.
6. Identify defended strengths, credible gaps, market shifts, and assumptions requiring validation.
7. Derive strategic implications: where to compete, where not to compete, and which claim needs proof.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use signals, transcripts, documents, knowledge pages, and survey responses for alternative and switching evidence. Use web or supplied primary sources for current competitor facts; cite both evidence classes separately.

## Output

Return scope, alternative set, decision criteria, evidence matrix, customer switching evidence, strategic gaps, implications, watch items, and sources with dates.

## Quality Gate

Reject unsupported claims, stale pricing or product facts, false precision, and tables whose rows are irrelevant to customer choice. Never infer competitor strategy from one feature.
