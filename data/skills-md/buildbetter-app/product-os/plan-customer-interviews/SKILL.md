# Plan Customer Interviews

## Contract

Seek concrete history before opinions. Ask about the respondent's world, not whether they like the team's idea. A good interview can disconfirm the premise.

## Workflow

1. Write the learning objective and the product decision it may change.
2. Define participant criteria from behavior, situation, or segment; distinguish users, buyers, admins, churned customers, and non-consumers.
3. Draft a short guide: context, last occurrence, sequence of events, current workaround, consequences, alternatives considered, and commitment or next step.
4. Replace leading, hypothetical, compound, and satisfaction-only questions with prompts about specific recent behavior. Ask `what happened next?`, `show me`, and `why then?`.
5. Plan consent, recording, note-taking, timeboxes, interviewer roles, and a neutral closing.
6. Define capture fields before the call: verbatim evidence, observed behavior, inference, follow-up, segment, and source ID.
7. Pilot the guide once. Remove questions that invite speeches but do not affect the decision.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use existing calls, transcripts, signals, people, CRM properties, and survey responses to avoid re-asking known questions and recruit the right evidence gaps. Do not contact or schedule anyone unless the user explicitly authorizes it.

## Output

Return the objective, participant screener, 30-45 minute guide, interviewer notes, capture schema, consent checklist, pilot changes, and synthesis handoff.

## Quality Gate

Flag every question that asks for prediction, praise, feature design, or a numeric rating without behavioral follow-up. The guide must leave room for the premise to be wrong.
