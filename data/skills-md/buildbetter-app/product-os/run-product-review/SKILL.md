# Run Product Review

## Contract

A product review is a decision forum, not a slide-reading ceremony. Separate outcome movement, evidence, shipped artifacts, current work, risks, and asks. Pre-read factual status; spend synchronous time on choices.

## Workflow

1. Define cadence, scope, participants, decision rights, timebox, and standing outcomes.
2. Collect a stable pre-read: outcome and guardrail metrics, customer evidence, experiments, shipped results, current bets, capacity, risks, dependencies, and prior commitments.
3. Label evidence dates and exact states. Distinguish proposed, committed, in progress, shipped, deployed, observed, and validated.
4. Select exceptions and decisions that need discussion; remove routine detail.
5. Run the meeting in order: outcomes, customer reality, learning, bet health, risks, and bounded decisions.
6. Record decisions, dissent, owners, due points, evidence needed, and items explicitly stopped or deferred.
7. Audit whether the review changed decisions or only reported activity; improve the template accordingly.

## BuildBetter Acceleration

If BuildBetter MCP is available, search organization skills first. Use signal and tag aggregates, representative evidence, surveys, projects, triage, linked tickets, documents, knowledge gaps, and agent-session or release evidence when deployed. Keep CI, staging, production, and customer validation states exact.

## Output

Return pre-read, agenda, outcome and evidence summary, decision queue, risk and dependency list, decisions, commitments, dissent, and follow-up ledger.

## Quality Gate

Reject reviews dominated by feature status, undated metrics, screenshots without conclusions, or decisions with no owner and evidence requirement.
