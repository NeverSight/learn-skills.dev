Elevating public issues and diagnosing content topics. Transform a private, vague “I feel something is off” into an observable phenomenon, test whether it has public relevance, challenge superficial explanations, uncover deeper, evidence-based mechanisms, and ultimately craft a discussion-worthy public issue along with a repeatable tagline. Use this when users say “Is this phenomenon worth talking about?” “I feel something is off but can't explain it,” “This is too personal; how can we get more people to care?” “Help me amplify this issue,” “How does an individual phenomenon become a public topic?” “What’s the mechanism behind the phenomenon?” It is not for mere headline polishing, pure emotional venting, fact-checking that lacks depth but demands forced elevation, or restating already clear public events.
