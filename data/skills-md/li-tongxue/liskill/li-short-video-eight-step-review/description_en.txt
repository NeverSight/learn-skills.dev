Use Li’s 8‑step short‑video review process to diagnose already written spoken copy, scripts, or VLOG narration. Apply this when the user says “Help me review the manuscript,” “Can this be posted,” “Review according to the eight‑step process,” “Why isn’t it getting traffic,” or “Check the start, information density, marketing points, or chips.” Only review existing finished drafts; for content created from scratch, first use the corresponding script to generate a skill.
