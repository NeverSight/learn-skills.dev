Manage product functional specifications—function lists, interaction logic, and Flyway‑style versioned additions and change records—producing specification files that can be fed directly to AI design tools such as Claude Design, v0, Figma Make, Lovable, etc. The specifications are placed under products/specs/<product line>/, with a V‑chain (V1__baseline, V2__xxx) documenting evolution; the synthesized CURRENT.md is the single entry point for feeding design tools. It is used when the user says “create product functional specification,” “write functional specs,” “record this product change,” “generate specs for design tools,” “sync CURRENT.md,” “product‑spec,” and also for initializing specifications for a new product line or when spec‑implementation drift requires verification.
