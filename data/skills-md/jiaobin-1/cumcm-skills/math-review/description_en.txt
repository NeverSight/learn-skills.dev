Universal Adversarial Modeling Reviewer. Targeting national competitions (CUMCM), the Mathematical Contest in Modeling/Interdisciplinary Contest in Modeling (MCM/ICM), graduate contests (NPMCM), general competitions, academic papers, and industrial practice, it simulates senior national reviewing experts or technical directors to conduct a stringent final quality review. It checks nine fatal flaws, the three‑dimensional consistency among paper, code, and results, specific rules for target profiles (such as the 25‑page hard limit and Summary Sheet for MCM, absolute anonymity in electronic submissions and a one‑page abstract for the national competition), and outputs a top‑5 high‑reward scoring action guide for the final three hours before submission.
