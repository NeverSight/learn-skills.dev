Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) clear, precise, and concise writing standards. Used when writing, rewriting, reviewing, editing, or translating Traditional Chinese content. Covers technical documents, software documentation, README, design documents, specifications, SOW, engineering reports, error messages, commit messages, and Traditional Chinese content generated by AI agents. Includes a comparison of Taiwanese and Mainland Chinese terminology, mixed Chinese‑English typesetting, punctuation, numeric format, AI tone anti‑patterns, and a final checklist. Based on the writing principles of *The Elements of Style*, it localizes for Traditional Chinese grammar, information architecture, and technical writing.
