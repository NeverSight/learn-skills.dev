Run a heuristic UX audit of a customer journey from screenshots. Use when the user asks for a UX audit, heuristic evaluation, usability review, design teardown, or expert review of screens, mockups, or a flow, whether your own product or a competitor's. Evaluates against named frameworks (Nielsen, Shneiderman, Gerhardt-Powals, Bastien & Scapin, Hick/Fitts/Miller/Jakob/Peak-End, Fogg, Cialdini, Gestalt, Norman, Tognazzini, WCAG 2.1, content design heuristics) and produces severity-rated findings with heuristic citations, annotated screenshots, and a structured markdown report. Do not use for generating new designs or for code-level accessibility scans of a live DOM.
