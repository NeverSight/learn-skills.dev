Pitch your business idea to a Shark Tank-style panel of 5 Claude sharks who only want to say no. Each investor grills it with their three hardest questions, a founder Claude answers only from what you told it, then each investor decides IN or OUT with a practice offer and what would change their mind. Use when the user wants honest feedback on a business, product or side-hustle idea, says Claude thinks every idea is great, asks "would investors fund this", wants to practice a pitch, or says "pitch" or "shark tank".
