PSX Ticket. Prints a plain-text paper ticket for the current psx/order.json so you can enter the order by hand in your PSX broker's platform (AKD Trade, JS Global TradeCast, Alfalah Trade, Arif Habib CTS, MIT, etc. - PSX has no public retail-broker API), records it in psx/state/broker.json, lets you log the actual fill price and quantity after the trade with `fill`, and can list open orders or cancel one. Paper-trading-first so you see how the plan survives real slippage before risking money. Use when the user says "print the ticket", "write the ticket for HUBC", "log the fill", "mark that filled at X", "list my open orders", "cancel order", or right after /psx-risk passes.
