PSX Time Machine. Takes an entry + stop + target (from psx/plan.json) and replays the stop-and-target rule against that symbol's last ~60 trading sessions of EOD bars, plus a 10,000-draw Monte Carlo of resampled daily returns. Prints the share of runs that hit target vs stop, median final outcome, and an ASCII fan chart. Verdicts: COIN_FLIP (within 5 % either way), STRONG (lopsided > 15 %), MANY_NEITHER (> 40 % time out without hitting either). Use when the user says "run the time machine", "backtest this plan", "has this trade worked before", "what did 10,000 runs look like", or right after /psx-exit and before /psx-budget.
