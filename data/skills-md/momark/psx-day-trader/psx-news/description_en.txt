PSX News. Pulls the latest corporate announcements from the PSX Data Portal's /announcements/companies page (dividends, book closures, financial-result disclosures, directors' interest, material information, general meetings) and flags items for the symbols on your psx-scanner watchlist. Keeps a rolling 30-day log at psx/state/news.json with dedupe by document id, so you only see new announcements since the last run. Use when the user says "scan PSX news", "what announcements came out", "any material info on OGDC", "news for my watchlist", "dividend announcements", "book closures", or right after /psx-scanner before /psx-entry.
