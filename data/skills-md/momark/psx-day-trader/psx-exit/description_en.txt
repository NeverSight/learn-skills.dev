PSX Exit. Takes psx/entry.json and the opening-range it was drawn against, and writes psx/plan.json with an explicit stop price, a reward/risk target, a stop-rule label (or-mid, or-low, or-high, price:X, pct:X) and the detail you need to defend it later. One clean exit plan per trade. Use when the user says "set my stop", "plan the exit on OGDC", "where do I cut this", "what's my target", "stop-and-target", "write the plan", or right after /psx-entry fires.
