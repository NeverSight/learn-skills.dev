PSX Chart Scanner. Reads the Pakistan Stock Exchange symbol universe from dps.psx.com.pk, filters to the Regular Market common stocks (optionally to KSE-100, KMI-30 or the Shariah-screened KMI-ALL list), runs a pre-open scan of daily closes (20-day closing-high breakout, bull flag, reclaim 20-day MA, oversold, oversold bounce) and an intraday opening-range breakout scan on 5-minute bars bucketed from PSX intraday ticks, then writes psx/watchlist.json for psx-news and psx-entry. Free PSX Data Portal data, bar by bar, Asia/Karachi time. Use when the user says "scan the PSX", "find me PSX setups", "KSE-100 breakouts", "KMI-30 Shariah setups", "scan for bull flags on PSX", "oversold PSX stocks", "pre-open PSX scan", "build my PSX watchlist", "what should I look at today on the exchange", or at the start of a PSX trading day before /psx-news.
