PSX Alerts. Keeps a small list of price-and-time alerts on symbols (above PRICE, below PRICE, pct_move >= X %, bar at HH:MM, opening bell), checks them against the PSX Data Portal's latest intraday tick, and marks them triggered with the exact tick that fired. Reads/writes psx/state/alerts.json. Use when the user says "alert me on OGDC above 320", "ping me when HUBC breaks 210", "alert if LUCK drops 2 %", "wake me at opening bell", "check my alerts", or wants something to watch the market while /psx-entry is running.
