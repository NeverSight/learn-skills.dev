PSX Entry. Takes a symbol from the psx-scanner watchlist and watches intraday 5-minute bars (bucketed from PSX Data Portal ticks) for a precise entry: the first bar to close above the opening-range high (or below the opening-range low for a short), with a configurable confirm rule. Writes psx/entry.json with the entry price, time, and the exact bar that triggered it, so psx-exit, psx-budget and psx-risk have one clean price to size and plan against. Use when the user says "watch OGDC for an entry", "ORB entry on HUBC", "wait for a break on LUCK", "pullback entry on ENGROH", "when should I buy", "find me an entry point", or right after /psx-scanner orb.
