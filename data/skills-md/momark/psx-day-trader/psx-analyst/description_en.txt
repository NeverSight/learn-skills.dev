PSX Analyst. For one symbol, lists every quarterly, half-year and annual financial report PSX Data Portal has links for (going back to 2013-14 for mature issuers), scrapes recent dividend/payout history, and flags the latest report's recency so you know if the company's numbers are current or a year stale. Does not read PDFs - it hands you the direct financials.psx.com.pk URL so you can open them in your browser. Use when the user says "fundamentals for OGDC", "when was HUBC's last report", "OGDC dividend history", "latest annual for LUCK", "has ENGROH posted Q3", or wants a sanity check before swinging a position.
