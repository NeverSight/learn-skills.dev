PSX Budget / Sizing. Reads psx/plan.json and psx/config.json (editable cost table in PKR) and sizes the position against four limits - max_loss_per_trade, max_position_pct, max_order, cash_available. Computes every PSX cost on both sides of the round trip (broker commission, PSX trading fee, PSX regulatory fee, SECP levy, NCCPL trade fee, CDC transaction fee, provincial sales tax on brokerage, CGT on the gain), then writes psx/order.json so psx-risk can pre-flight-check it and psx-ticket can print the paper ticket. Use when the user says "size this trade", "how many shares", "position size for HUBC", "what's it cost round-trip", "what's my net R/R after taxes", "budget the trade", or right after /psx-exit.
