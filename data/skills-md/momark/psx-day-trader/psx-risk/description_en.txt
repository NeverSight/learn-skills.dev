PSX Risk. Pre-flight-checks psx/order.json against a dozen named rules (order shape, cash on hand, position-pct cap, PSX scrip circuit breaker, stop on the correct side, minimum net R/R after costs, daily loss budget, trading-window open, PSX holiday, short-selling flagged, duplicate order via fingerprint, Shariah tag mismatch) and prints a pass/warn/fail per check with the exact numbers. Writes psx/state/risk.json.  Use when the user says "check the risk", "is this trade safe", "pre-flight the order", "risk check on OGDC", "am I over my daily limit", "any red flags", or right after /psx-budget and before /psx-ticket.
