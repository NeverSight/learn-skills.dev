Convert an AI video prompt from the writing style of a source model (e.g., Sora 2) to the best‑practice style of a target model (e.g., Kling 3.0, Wan 2.7, Veo 3.1, etc.). This is based on 110 entries covering 10 scenarios × 11 models from the benchmark data (prompts/data/cross-model-matrix.json), and the rewrite should not rely on intuition but on a lookup‑style in‑context learning. The 10 scenarios are: product, two‑person dialogue, physical action, image‑to‑video, multi‑person meeting, horror, natural timelapse, abstract, wuxia, cute pets. It is used for trigger scenarios such as “Sora is EOL, help me convert this prompt to Veo,” “I have a Kling prompt and want to run it on Wan,” “cross‑model A/B testing,” “optimize an English prompt into a Kling Chinese version,” etc.
