Risk Manager for the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX). Checks every order against your limits and the PSX rules, before psx-broker shows it. Day trades: a stop is needed, and it checks the loss at the stop (costs included), reward:risk, the day's total risk, trades per day and entry times (Karachi time). Swing trades add your open positions, the open risk across days and the loss if the next day opens at the lower price limit. PSX rules: the REG board, the Rs 0.01 tick, today's price limits, ordinary shares only and no blank sales. It also checks order size, position cap, cash buffer, no margin and duplicates. Getting out is never blocked by a day or swing limit. Prints PASS or BLOCK with the reason and records it, so psx-broker shows only an order that passed. Use when the user says "check my order", "is this safe", "risk check", "does this break my rules", "can I take this trade", "how much have I risked today", "can I add another swing trade", or before /psx-broker, always.
