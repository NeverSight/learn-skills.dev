Time Machine for the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX). Replays a planned trade before you risk a rupee: 10,000 simulated runs, plus the same rule on past days. Takes the plan from psx-exit (entry, stop, target). A day plan replays the rest of the session. With 20 or more past sessions of the stock's own 5-minute bars it replays those bars (bars mode). Without them it uses a simpler model from the last 60 daily bars (daily mode), and says so. A swing plan replays the next 20 sessions, one daily bar at a time, gaps at the open included (swing mode). Prints the odds of target first, stop first or neither, where the price may end, the average R with PSX costs counted, and one honest verdict. Use when the user says "run the time machine", "replay this trade", "what are the odds", "simulate this trade", "monte carlo", "backtest this setup", "how often does this hit the target", "is this a coin flip", or after /psx-exit writes a plan, before /psx-budget.
