Order tickets for the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX). Paper trading by default, never without your yes. No PSX broker has an order API for retail clients, so it never places an order. Each order is a plain ticket: the order, stop and target, the max loss at the stop (costs included), why, and the one line to type into your broker app. It records your yes word for word, keeps a paper ledger with costs, builds the market sell that gets you flat by your flat-by time, and logs each trade's max loss for psx-risk. In swing style it keeps each plan, makes the stop-limit sell ticket (protect), moves a stop, and records a buy that did not fill, bonus shares and a cash fix. A paper fill never goes into a ledger of real shares. Use when the user says "place the order", "buy it", "execute", "send it to my broker", "close my position", "flatten", "get me out", "set up paper trading", "protect my position", "it did not fill", "record my bonus shares", "move my stop up", or after /psx-risk passes.
