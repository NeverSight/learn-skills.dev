Budget Manager for the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX). Sizes a day trade or a swing trade to your budget and your max loss per trade in rupees, with the broker's costs included (commission, sales tax and fees). Reads the plan from psx-exit (entry, stop, target), finds the whole shares that fit four limits (max loss per trade, position cap, max order, cash after your buffer; a swing trade adds a fifth, the gap limit), says which limit decided it, and writes ONE order to psx/orders.json: a limit order at the plan's entry, with the stop and the target in it. A stop too far away is a plain refusal, not a smaller order. Nothing is placed. Use when the user says "how many shares", "size this trade", "how much should I buy", "I have Rs 500,000", "I only want to risk Rs 5,000 a trade", "set my rules", "keep 10% in cash", "size my swing trade", or after /psx-exit writes a plan.
