Exit Planner for the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX). Sets your stop and target before you buy, so you know when to get out. Reads the entry from psx-entry (or a price you give). The stop goes at the opening-range midpoint by default (or the range low, a price, a percent or an ATR multiple), rounded down to the Rs 0.01 tick. The target is 2R (or 1R, or a price). Checks the day's price limits and costs. Refuses a stop at or above the entry, a target at or below it or over the upper price limit, or a stop too tight to survive the spread, and says why. In swing style it plans a limit buy for the next session: a stop from the daily ATR, a target, an exit-by day, a trend rule and the gap risk. Writes psx/plan.json. Long side only. Use when the user says "where's my stop", "set my stop and target", "plan the exit", "when do I get out", "stop loss", "what's my R", "risk reward", "plan a swing trade", or after /psx-entry (day style) or /psx-scanner (swing style), before /psx-timemachine.
