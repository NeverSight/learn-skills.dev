Chart Scanner for the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX). Builds the list of ordinary shares from the PSX closing file, scans daily closes before the open (20-day closing-high breakout, bull flag, back over the 20-day average, oversold, oversold bounce), records the market tape from the PSX screener in the session (only with "psx_site": true), and scans that tape for opening-range breakouts on 5-minute bars. Leaves out the shares on your blocked list, and says which. In swing style, run the daily scan after the close; orb and tape are day tools. Writes psx/watchlist.json for psx-news, psx-entry and psx-exit. Use when the user says "scan the market", "scan PSX", "find me setups", "find swing setups", "what is breaking out", "opening range breakouts", "oversold stocks", "pre-market scan", "build my watchlist", "what should I look at today", or at the start of a trading day before /psx-news.
