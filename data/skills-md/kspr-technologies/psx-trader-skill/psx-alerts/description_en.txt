Price Alerts on the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) clock. Rings when a price, a stop, a target, a daily price limit, a KSE30 move or the opening bell hits, once each, so you are not spammed. Turns the trade plan into three alerts in one go: the stop, the target and "flat by close". Knows PSX hours (the Friday Juma break, Ramadan) and the 2026 and 2027 holidays, in Karachi time. Reads PSX quotes (about 5 minutes late) from your saved pages, the tape or the PSX site (only if you turn it on), and writes psx/prices.json for sizing and the risk check. For a swing trade it watches the position until it is sold: the stop, the target, a trend break and the time stop, on every closing file, even after the Mac slept. Use when the user says "alert me when", "tell me if HUBC hits 170", "watch my stop", "watch my trade", "watch my swing trade", "remind me to close by 3:20", "is PSX open", "when does the market open", "get me a price", or right after /psx-exit writes a plan.
