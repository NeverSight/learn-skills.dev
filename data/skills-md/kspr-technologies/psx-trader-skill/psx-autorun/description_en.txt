Auto-run for the PSX pack, on a Mac. After a one-time install, the Mac runs the pack's own tools on a timer, with no Claude window open: a morning check before the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) opens, the alert checks every 5 minutes while PSX is open, the scan and the long-term signals after the close, and a week report on Saturday. It tells you with a Mac message and a short report. Every time comes from the pack's PSX clock, so Fridays, Ramadan and holidays count. It never places an order and never runs psx-broker, psx-budget or psx-risk.
