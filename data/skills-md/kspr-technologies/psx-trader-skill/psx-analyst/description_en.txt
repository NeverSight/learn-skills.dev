The Results Analyst for the Pakistan Stock Exchange. Reads a company's results from its PSX company page (saved with your browser, or the PSX site when you allow it): sales (for a bank, mark-up earned and total income), profit after tax, EPS and margins, quarter by quarter, each against the same quarter a year back, and the last full year. Flags a quarter label that looks wrong, derived quarters and one-off items. Finds the newest results notice and the next board meeting. Use when the user says "how did OGDC's results look", "what changed this quarter", "is profit growing", "compare margins", "when is the next board meeting", "are results due this week", or before trading a stock around its results.
