News Monitor for the Pakistan Stock Exchange. Reads the PSX notices of every stock you watch (financial results, board meetings, material information, dividends, insider trades) and Pakistani business news (Business Recorder, Dawn, Express Tribune, ProPakistani), and shows how far each stock is from its last close, with a flag at the daily price limit. Shows only what is new since the last check, newest first, in Karachi time. Tags each item (results, dividend, board, material, insider, deal, halt, macro...) and never repeats one. Use when the user says "any news on OGDC", "why is this moving", "what's the news on my watchlist", "morning briefing", "check the PSX notices", "any results today", "KSE-100 today", "why is the KSE-100 down", "did anything break", or after /psx-scanner finds setups.
