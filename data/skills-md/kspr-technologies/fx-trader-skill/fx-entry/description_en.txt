Entry Timer for currency pairs and commodities at a MetaTrader broker, on both sides. Watches one setup and names the moment its rule is met: a finished daily bar that closes beyond the scanner's level (swing), or the first finished 5-minute bar that closes above or below the London opening range (day). Knows the FX day, the sessions, the CME break, holidays, early closes and the 9:00 PM PKT entry cut-off. Says waiting and how far, triggered with the time and the price, expired, late or closed, with the data age and news quiet times, and writes fx/entry.json for /fx-exit. It never places an order. Use when the user says "tell me when to get in", "watch gold", "has EURUSD broken out", "did it break the range", "entry signal", "time my entry", "check my watchlist", or after /fx-scanner and /fx-news, before /fx-exit.
