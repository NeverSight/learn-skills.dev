MetaTrader order tickets for forex, gold, silver, oil and gas. Paper trading by default. Never places an order: it shows each order as a plain ticket (Buy or Sell, lots, stop loss, take profit, max loss with costs, how old the price is), waits for your explicit yes, then gives the one line to type into your own MetaTrader. Keeps a paper ledger, builds close orders and moves stops. Use when the user says "place the order", "buy it", "sell it", "execute", "send it to MetaTrader", "close my trade", "flatten", "get me out", "move my stop", "set up paper trading", or after /fx-risk passes.
