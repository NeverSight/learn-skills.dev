Budget Manager for currency pairs, gold, silver, oil, gas and copper at a MetaTrader 4/5 broker. Sizes one trade in lots to your max loss per trade in US dollars (1% of your balance by default), with the spread, the commission and the swap counted. Reads the plan from fx-exit (entry, stop, target), works for buys and sells, swing and day trades, checks the margin, says which limit decided the size, and writes ONE order to fx/orders.json. If even the smallest lot loses too much, it says no and never moves the stop. Nothing is placed. Use when the user says "how many lots", "size this trade", "what lot size", "how much should I risk", "I have $1,000", "I only want to risk $20 a trade", "set my rules", "copy my broker's specs", or after /fx-exit writes a plan.
