# Research Synthesis

## Purpose
Consolidate raw research material into themes and insights that trace to evidence, with confidence and limitations stated, so later work can rely on them without rereading the source.

## When to use
- Use when: notes, transcripts, survey responses, analytics, or support themes exist and the team needs what they collectively show.
- Do not use when: no source material exists; return the research questions and a plan for gathering it instead. Do not use to draft personas; hand the synthesis to persona drafting.

## Inputs
- Required: the source material and the questions the research was meant to answer.
- Optional: participant details, how and when the material was collected, prior findings, segments of interest.
- Assumptions: unstated sampling or recruitment is recorded as unknown, not assumed representative.

## Process
1. Inventory the sources: how many, from whom, how collected, and when.
2. Extract observations: specific things people said, did, or experienced. Keep each tied to its source and keep the participant's words where they matter.
3. Group related observations into candidate themes by what they show, not by who said them or which question prompted them.
4. For each theme, count how many sources support it and note any that contradict it.
5. State each insight as an interpretation of a theme: what it implies for the product, the user's goal, and the decision it informs.
6. Rate confidence per insight from the number, diversity, and directness of its evidence.
7. List contradictions, outliers worth keeping, and questions the material cannot answer.

## Rules and heuristics
- Required: separate observation, interpretation, and recommendation, and label which each statement is.
- Required: every insight links to the observations that support it. Remove any that cannot be traced.
- Required: report contradicting evidence; do not smooth it away.
- Recommended: prefer behavior over stated opinion when they conflict, and say so.
- Recommended: report a theme's support as counts of sources, not percentages, when the sample is small.

## Checks
- Every insight traces to at least one source observation.
- Source counts match the inventory.
- No insight claims more than its evidence supports.
- Gaps and unanswered research questions are listed.

## Output
Return the source inventory, themes with supporting and contradicting evidence, insights with confidence, unresolved contradictions, research gaps, and suggested next research steps. Use the [insight table](templates/insight-table.md).

## Completion criteria
- Each research question is answered, partly answered with the gap stated, or marked unanswerable from the material.
- Every insight is traceable, rated, and distinguished from observation.
