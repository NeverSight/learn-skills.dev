Use when the user asks to run a task with two Herdr agents splitting planning/review from execution — “你负责规划和审核，让 X 执行”, “协同两个 agent”, “把活派给 herdr 里的 X，你把关”, “让它把结论返回给你”, “你拆一下任务再派给 X”. Roles are agent-type agnostic — either side may be claude, opencode, codex, droid, cursor, hermes, omp, kimi, pi, grok or anything else on the roster. Covers role contracts, cutting the work into verifiable rounds, self-contained handoff prompts, the 131,071-byte prompt limit and “argument list too long” recovery, artifact-based verification with and without git, auditing an executor’s explanations and not just its numbers, the concurrent-writer hazard, and the quality kill-switch. Not for one-shot relays — use herdr-handoff for those.
