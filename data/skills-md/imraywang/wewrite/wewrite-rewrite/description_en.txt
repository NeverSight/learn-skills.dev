WeWrite multi‑platform rewriting module: transform a public account article (or a specified source manuscript) at the content level into versions for other platforms. Currently supports Xiaohongshu (image‑text notes) and Douyin (voice‑over script), checking editing quality and similarity to the source manuscript. Trigger keywords: rewrite for Xiaohongshu, Xiaohongshu version, Douyin version, voice‑over script, multi‑platform distribution, one manuscript multiple releases, platform rewriting. It should not be triggered by “translate”, “abbreviate”, or “change the title” — those are single‑platform editing actions.
