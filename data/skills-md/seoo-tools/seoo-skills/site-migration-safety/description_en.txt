Protect search performance during any change that alters URLs or how pages are served: domain move, HTTPS/www/subdomain change, replatform or framework change, URL restructure, template or rendering rewrite, CMS change, hreflang/international restructure, or merging/deleting pages. Use when a user says "I'm moving my site," "we're changing our URL structure," "I'm merging these pages," or "did my migration break SEO?" Not for diagnosing an unexplained traffic drop (traffic-drop-diagnosis) or a general technical audit.
