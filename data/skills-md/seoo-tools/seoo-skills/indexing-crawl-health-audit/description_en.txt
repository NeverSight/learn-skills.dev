Use when a user asks "why isn't my page indexed," "check my robots/sitemap/noindex/canonical setup," or what "Crawled - currently not indexed" means. NOT for traffic-drop investigation (traffic-drop-diagnosis), on-page tags (technical-seo-audit) or content quality (page-quality-audit).
