Determine how much SEO tinkering is actually appropriate for a site right now, before recommending any optimization action — a small/growing site and a mature one need opposite advice. Use at the start of any SEO engagement, before running any other audit skill in this pack, or when a user asks "should I be optimizing more" or "why isn't more tinkering helping." This is a gating skill: its answer changes what the other audit skills should even recommend.
