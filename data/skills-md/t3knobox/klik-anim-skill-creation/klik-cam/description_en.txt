The camera-movement craft layer for Remotion motion graphics — the companion to klik-anim (which governs everything moving WITHIN the shot; this skill governs the shot itself). Read and obey this WHENEVER a Remotion piece moves, or considers moving, its camera: pushes, dollies, orbits, pans, parallax and 2.5D depth moves, "camera shake", momentum handoffs between shots, or any request mentioning camera work, camera energy, or a moving/locked camera. It owns the Camera Motion Dial (C0, 1–5) that sets a video's camera energy as a branding decision, and the camera grammar: anti-AI-camera move pairing, monotone-spline pose interpolation, shutter blur at velocity peaks, camera signature variation scene to scene, focus discipline (the focal plane sits on the words), and 3D-space hygiene. klik-anim's base laws (determinism, thin-peak easing, pacing dial C0.5) still bind every camera parameter. No camera move ships without passing the CAM checklist at the end.
