Data-story videos where an animated chart carries the argument - lines that draw as time passes, bars that grow, a big counter that ticks, axes that rescale when the data surprises, annotation pins on the key moments - rendered to MP4 at 9:16 or 16:9 with HyperFrames, SVG and GSAP. Use whenever someone wants to animate statistics, show a trend, growth or cost over time, compare numbers, break down a figure (salary, budget, benchmark), or turn a dataset or table into a short narrated video. Not for architecture or request flows (use system-diagram-animation) or text-only videos (use kinetic-typography).
