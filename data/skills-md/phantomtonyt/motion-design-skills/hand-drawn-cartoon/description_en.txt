Hand-drawn storybook cartoon videos - wobbly inked outlines that boil, paper grain and crayon hatching, flat muted colours, simple round characters with big expressive acting - built in code (SVG + GSAP in HyperFrames) and rendered to MP4 at 9:16 or 16:9. Use for cute explainers, character stories, mascot shorts, fables and any request for a handmade, doodly, storybook or less-corporate look. Not for clean flat vector (use flat-vector-explainer), 3D characters (use 3d-toy-character) or text-only videos (use kinetic-typography).
