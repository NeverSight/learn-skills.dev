Main entry skill for Chinese undergraduate thesis workflows. Use first when the user asks for thesis topic selection, literature retrieval, literature review, proposal drafting, thesis outline and word budget, project material analysis, body chapter writing (正文), figures and tables (图表), thesis QC or mock blind review, Word conversion, "完整流程", "从选题到综述/开题/成稿", "写完整篇论文", or any task that may involve thesis-related modules. This is the single public workflow entrypoint; it routes internally to modules/undergraduate-thesis-topic, modules/cnki-cssci-search, modules/workbuddy-cnki-webbridge-search, modules/literature-review-cn, modules/thesis-proposal-cn, modules/thesis-outline-cn, modules/thesis-materials-cn, modules/thesis-body-cn, modules/thesis-figures-cn, modules/thesis-qc-cn, and modules/docx-editor-cn, asks whether to continue after each completed stage, and defaults to generating Word documents for literature reviews, proposal reports, and the full thesis.
