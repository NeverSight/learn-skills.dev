Draft a single social post for one platform — LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Instagram, Facebook, Threads, Bluesky, or Mastodon — built for how that platform's feed actually reads: a hook that survives the fold, one idea, white-space rhythm, a specific call to action, and platform-correct links and hashtags. Use when the user says "write a post", "draft a LinkedIn post", "write an X post", "post this on Instagram", or asks for "a post about" something. Produces two hook variants and self-checks the draft against the user's voice rules. For the same message adapted across several platforms at once use social-crosspost; for a multi-post sequence use social-thread. Reads social-context.md for voice, audience, pillars, positioning, goals, and red lines so the post sounds like the user.
