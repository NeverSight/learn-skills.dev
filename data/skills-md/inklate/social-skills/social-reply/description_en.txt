Triage a pasted batch of comments, mentions, and DMs from LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, or Threads, and draft on-voice replies where they're warranted. Use when the user says "help me reply", asks you to "draft responses to these comments", says "triage my mentions", or asks you to "answer these DMs". Each item gets bucketed as question, praise, objection, lead signal, or troll/spam, assigned a recommended action — reply, react-only, DM, or ignore — and, where a reply is warranted, a draft written in the user's voice. It reads social-context.md for brand voice, product facts, and escalation boundaries so replies sound like the user and never invent answers. Lead signals get flagged prominently with a suggested next step; legally or PR-sensitive items get escalated with a reason, not drafted.
