Academic Paper Review Assistant. This skill is triggered when a user uploads a paper file (PDF/Word) and wants to receive review comments, paper evaluation, or revision suggestions. It also applies to scenarios where the user says “help me look at this paper,” “review this article,” “give modification suggestions,” etc. The skill first refines the user’s review requirements, then reads the paper, automatically categorizes issues into “structural issues” and “open-ended expansion issues,” and determines whether the paper has reached a point where further revisions can be terminated.
