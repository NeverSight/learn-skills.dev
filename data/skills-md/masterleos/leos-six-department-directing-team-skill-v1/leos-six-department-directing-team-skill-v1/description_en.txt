AI feature films, AI series, and narrative videos employ a six‑role directing team skill. By collaborating as chief director, performance director, in‑camera execution director, cinematographer, prompt director, and script supervisor/continuity supervisor, they convert scripts, assets, storyboards, prompts, video or timeline into scene designs, shooting plans, structured pure‑text video prompts under 5,000 characters, role‑by‑role and shot‑by‑shot reviews, continuity records, minimal rework, and project‑style consolidation. Users need to consult by role, generate video prompts after clear authorization, review shots and visual language, or use them when updating methods from generated records. It does not handle real‑world production aspects such as personnel division, scheduling, delivery cadence, or project risk management.
