It corrects and proofreads Korean text’s spelling, spacing, standard language, foreign‑word notation, punctuation, and obvious malformed sentences according to linguistic norms. It fixes only objective errors—such as the use of 되/돼, 안/않, -로서/-로써, spacing of dependent nouns, nonstandard forms like 바램→바람, foreign‑word spellings like 메세지→메시지, predicate‑subject agreement mismatches, and meaning duplication (e.g., before reversal)—while leaving style, meaning, and formality unchanged. Each correction is presented with the regulatory basis and details. Use it for spelling and spacing checks, correction/proofreading, typo or malformed‑sentence fixing, and requests to verify standard language or foreign‑word notation. If the purpose is to refine style, translation tone, or AI “fingerprint,” use the “humanize” skill; for translation‑style correction, use “translate‑polish”; for style or formality transformation, use the “restyle” skill. Adding, deleting, or rewriting content is excluded.
