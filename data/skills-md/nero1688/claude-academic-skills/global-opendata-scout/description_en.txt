Cross‑national public statistical data scout. When research requires cross‑country comparison, or when the user is not in Taiwan, determine which international database, which country’s statistical agency, and whether the data can be programmatically retrieved. Built‑in are tested, key‑free endpoints (World Bank, Eurostat, ILOSTAT, IMF, UN Data) and retrieval scripts, plus a five‑step methodology for “how to find any country’s official statistical agency” without memorizing a list of countries. The core value lies in warning about pitfalls of cross‑national data: three different country‑code systems must not be mixed, choices of currency, PPP, and base year, differences in fiscal year, industry classifications (ISIC/NACE/NAICS) cannot be directly mapped, coverage gaps are non‑random and cause selection bias, aggregate statistics may be retrospectively revised so the retrieval date must be recorded. When to use: for cross‑national studies, when international comparative data are needed, to find out whether a country has official statistics, and for non‑Taiwan users to employ this skill family. Trigger words: cross‑national, international comparison, cross‑country, World Bank, OECD, IMF, Eurostat, ILOSTAT, International Labour Organization, UN Data, FRED, national statistics, national statistical bureaus, SDMX, cross‑national data, international databases, foreign data, foreign statistics, PPP, purchasing power parity. Boundary with Taiwan official statistical sources: there is a dedicated skill that focuses on Taiwan’s official statistics in depth; this skill handles everything outside Taiwan and cross‑national; **Taiwan data should always be routed to that skill**. Boundary with multi‑source‑data‑integrator: when data from multiple countries and sources are fetched and need to be merged and integrated, hand them over to that tool.
