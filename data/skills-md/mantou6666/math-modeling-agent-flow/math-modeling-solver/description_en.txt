General collaborative solving and result certification Skill for mathematical modeling competitions. Used for new problem understanding, Problem Contract, independent modeling divergence, Web Research integration, model/algorithm selection, formal evaluator, complex optimization search space auditing, program solving, result verification and Result Certificate. Particularly suitable for issues that can change scientific outcomes such as “how to model,” “why can’t a better result be found,” “whether the evaluator is correct,” “whether the code truly matches the problem intent.” Paper wording, citations, and DOCX formatting are handed to math-modeling-paper; code humanization after results are frozen and final delivery are handed to math-modeling-finalizer.
