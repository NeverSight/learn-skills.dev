Writes, debugs, and art-directs procedural GLSL fragment shaders (Shadertoy mainImage, WebGL2 / GLSL ES 3.00): SDF raymarching, procedural buildings/cities, 2D distance-field graphics, noise / fBm / domain warping, terrain, ocean/coastal water optics, clouds and other volumes, PBR lighting, color and tonemapping. Ships a verified GLSL building-block library with a bundler and checker. Use when the user asks for a shader, Shadertoy, GLSL/.frag/.glsl code, a raymarcher, an SDF scene, a procedural texture or generative visual, or wants a shader fixed, optimized, ported or explained. Not for HLSL/Unity ShaderLab or non-graphics GPU compute.
