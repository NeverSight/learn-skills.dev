Turns one image of any subject (an AI image, a render, a photo of a figurine or product, a drawing, or an idea turned into an image first) into a watertight 3D sculpt that is measured against the image and recorded step by step in Blender: front maps (mask, metric depth and focal length, normals), generated views of the unseen sides (registered over the whole circle and screened), a visual hull, then many recorded fit passes against every normal map, silhouette and the photo's depth, with symmetry, focus regions, gap cuts and smoothing. Ships a CLI (`scripts/i2s.py`) for case scaffolding, fal calls with a budget, registration, carving, fitting, map checks, Blender replay, timelapse and turntable films, exports (GLB, OBJ, STL) and a report. Use when the user wants a 3D model or sculpt "from this image", a figurine or character turned into a mesh, a sculpting timelapse, or a check of a 3D model against a reference image. Not for scenes or rooms, text-to-3D without any image, rigging or animation, PBR texturing, or CAD-accurate parts.
