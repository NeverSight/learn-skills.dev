Sends MMS multimedia messages (images, video, audio, PDFs, vCards) via the MMS channel of the Sinch Conversation API. Available in the United States, Canada, and Australia only. Covers supported media types, file size limits, MMS channel properties, and transcoding behavior. Use when sending an MMS or picture message, sending media to US/CA/AU numbers over MMS, or debugging MMS media rejection and size issues.
