Analyze and report Traigent optimization results from the terminal — without opening the portal's tabs. Use when a user asks to analyze a run, 'how did my run do?', 'analyze my latest run in project X', what the winner is, or to read result fields, reports, leaderboards, Pareto trade-offs, correlations, or parameter/example insights. Decision questions route to `traigent-analyze-guidance` (Mode B for portal-tracked runs, Mode C for offline/local). Also covers the local OptimizationResult object: reading results.best_config, comparing trials, checking stop_reason, automatic best-config application, candidate export, accessing total_cost or total_tokens, or understanding why optimization stopped.
