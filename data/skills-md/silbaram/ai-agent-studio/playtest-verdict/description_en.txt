Records prototype playtest sessions as data, has a read-only judge propose a verdict (holds, refuted, unknown) for each concept hypothesis from those records, promotes measured numbers from assumed to measured with a checkable source, and calibrates assumptions from recorded stage statistics: compares assumed levels, durations, kills, and income with the records, compares simulated stage clear rates with measured ones, and suggests survival curve values. Use after playing a prototype, when deciding whether a concept's hypotheses survived, before turning an assumed number into a measured one, or when checking balance assumptions against real runs.
