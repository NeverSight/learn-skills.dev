Models item enhancement (cost, success rate, and keep/downgrade/destroy on failure per level) and synthesis (fixed recipes and random merges that combine matching items into one of a matching set), computes the expected cost and attempts to reach each level, the value ratio of each recipe against stat budgets, and the expected crafts to get one specific result, and finds item-multiplication loops and items no drop, recipe, or declared source can produce. Use when designing or reviewing upgrade systems, crafting, merging, salvage, or item acquisition paths in any genre and engine.
