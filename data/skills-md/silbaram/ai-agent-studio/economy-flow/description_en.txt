Models currency income per stage (kills times enemy rewards, expected sell value of drops, other income) and an ordered purchase plan whose prices come from item prices, expected enhancement costs, crafting costs, permanent upgrade costs, or fixed amounts, then computes when each purchase becomes affordable within a run, which run a carried meta currency affords it, and how much currency is left over. Use when tuning rewards, prices, shop or upgrade pacing, or checking for currency piling up or running short, in any genre and engine.
