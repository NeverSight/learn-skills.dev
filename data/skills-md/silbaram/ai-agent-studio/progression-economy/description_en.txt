Keeps items, permanent upgrades, and enemies in single-source JSON/CSV data, shows them in a read-only local viewer, validates them with deterministic scripts (tier inversions, dominated items, pity expectations, upgrade cost chains, enemy threat progression, cross-file drop-table references), and simulates roguelike meta-progression either with an explicit abstract model or from the combat data (permanent upgrade stats and in-run growth added to the combat calculation per stage, with income and purchase order read from the economy plan). Use when designing, adding, or reviewing items, loot, shops, permanent upgrades, enemies, or progression pacing for action roguelikes and other run-based games in any engine.
