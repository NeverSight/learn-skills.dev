Read GMGN call-outs (喊单) — public token calls that wallets publish on GMGN, with call price, call market cap, multiplier since the call, thesis text and the caller's current holding. Use when user asks who is calling a token, what a wallet has called, a wallet's call-out track record (hit rate, best multiplier), or wants the latest call-out feed (KOL-only, paid declarations, safe tokens). Read-only; publishing a call-out is not supported.
