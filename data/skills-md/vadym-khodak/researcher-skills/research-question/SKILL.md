# Formulate a Research Question

## Overview

Helps develop a focused, researchable question or hypothesis from a broad topic. A good research question is specific, feasible, original, and answerable with available methods and data.

## Workflow

1. **Start from the topic** — ask the user to state their broad area of interest
2. **Narrow the scope** — ask about population, context, time frame, and key variables
3. **Identify the question type** — descriptive, comparative, relational, or causal (see below)
4. **Draft 2–3 candidate questions** — present options at different levels of scope
5. **Test against FINER** — evaluate each draft (see criteria)
6. **Refine** — iterate until the question is sharp and researchable

## Question Types

| Type | Asks | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| Descriptive | What is the current state? | "What digital marketing strategies do Ukrainian SMEs use?" |
| Comparative | How do two groups/cases differ? | "How do marketing strategies of SMEs differ between Ukraine and Poland?" |
| Relational | Is there a relationship between X and Y? | "What is the relationship between social media spend and brand awareness in SMEs?" |
| Causal | Does X cause Y? | "Does influencer marketing increase purchase intent among Gen Z consumers?" |

Causal questions require experimental or quasi-experimental design — flag this to the user.

## FINER Criteria

| Criterion | Check |
|-----------|-------|
| **F**easible | Can this be answered with available data, time, and resources? |
| **I**nteresting | Does it matter to the field or to practice? |
| **N**ovel | Does it add something beyond existing research? |
| **E**thical | Can it be studied without harm to participants or data misuse? |
| **R**elevant | Does it connect to the user's field, program, or practical goals? |

## From Topic to Question

```
Broad topic:     "Social media and students"
↓ add population
Narrowed:        "Social media use among Ukrainian university students"
↓ add outcome
Focused:         "How does social media use affect academic performance among Ukrainian university students?"
↓ add direction/context
Sharp:           "What is the relationship between daily social media screen time and GPA among first-year students at Ukrainian universities?"
```

## Ukrainian-Specific Notes

| Element | Convention |
|---------|-----------|
| Мета дослідження | Ukrainian theses require a formal statement of мета (goal) and завдання (objectives) in the вступ — the research question underpins both |
| Об'єкт і предмет | Object = the broad domain; Subject = the specific aspect being studied. The research question should reflect the предмет |
| Гіпотеза | Quantitative Ukrainian theses often require an explicit hypothesis; qualitative ones may not — check your supervisor's requirements |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Too broad ("How does marketing work?") | Add population, context, and a specific outcome variable |
| Two questions in one | Split into separate questions or make one the sub-question |
| Answerable with yes/no | Rephrase as "How…", "What…", "To what extent…" |
| Already answered in literature | Run a quick search before finalising — originality is required |
