Use the catbus CLI to read or interact with the 11 platforms—Xiaohongshu (RedNote), Douyin, TikTok, Bilibili (B), Kuaishou, Weibo, Xianyu, Taobao, JD.com, X (Twitter), and 12306 railway—supporting search, details, comments, users, recommendation feeds, downloads, posting, likes/follows, direct messages, live‑stream bullet comments, and train ticket availability and pricing, and outputting a unified JSON. It is used when users need to query, collect, export, monitor, or operate content and accounts on these platforms.
