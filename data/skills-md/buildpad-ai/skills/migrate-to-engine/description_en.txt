Engine projects only: finish moving a Buildpad project from Supabase to the Buildpad Engine. Rewrites the hooks, cron jobs and custom services the Engine could not run as they were (engine-plan/automation-to-review.json) for the Engine's runtime, and ports the app's code: environment, sign-in, data calls, files, administrator code, schema and access rules, database functions. Use after a project was moved, when .buildpad/project.json says the backend is engine, or when asked to port an app or automation to the Engine. On a Supabase project it explains the move and stops.
