Use when writing or reviewing tests for code that handles money, payments, ledgers, schedules or reconciliation. Covers property tests for sum preservation and round trips, frozen test vectors reviewed by humans, golden schedules, ledger invariants after random operations, mutation testing as the measure of a suite, synthetic data with valid check digits and edge amounts, an injected clock for month ends and leap days, idempotency and concurrency tests with fault injection, recorded processor fixtures, and CI gates that count killed mutants rather than coverage. Triggers on a new money module, a test suite that passes but misses bugs, a flaky payment test, a coverage target. Not a replacement for the domain skills; it tells you how to prove them. Use it also when asked to delete, skip or quarantine a failing money test.
