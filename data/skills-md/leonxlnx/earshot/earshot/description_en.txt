Listen with earshot whenever you must judge, choose, trim, verify or compare sound you cannot hear - picking a sound effect, music or ambience from candidates, finding the cleanest moment in a recording, checking whether audio reads as a real recording or as code-synthesized, reviewing a soundtrack or a video's audio (what happens when, sync to picture, loudness, mix), A/B-ing a mix change, or making a sound in code and improving it by listening (earshot forge). Use it before telling anyone how audio sounds.
