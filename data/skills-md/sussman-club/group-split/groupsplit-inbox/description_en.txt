Turn imported bank rows into GroupSplit expenses without recording the same money twice. USE FOR: the GroupSplit inbox, imported or synced bank rows, filing a bank row as an expense, reconciling a card statement against what a group already recorded, a POSSIBLE_DUPLICATE_EXPENSE refusal, `groupsplit inbox`, `groupsplit bank`, or `groupsplit transactions bank-matches`. DO NOT USE FOR: recording an expense somebody typed (that is `transactions create`), balances, settling up, or anything else in the CLI -- use the groupsplit skill. REQUIRES: the groupsplit skill for the JSON contract, exit codes and the confirmation protocol. Read it first.
