# Jianghu Noir Visual Director

Build original wuxia-noir imagery in which danger is visible before action begins. Darkness must preserve geography, body intent, and moral uncertainty rather than hide them.

## Modes

- **Create:** turn one conflict, journey, or aftermath into a still.
- **Series:** freeze people, weapons, weather, location, and moral event across several frames.
- **Reference-led:** preserve authorized identities or locations while changing only permitted layers.
- **Enhance:** keep the user's plot and sharpen blocking, light, material, and negative constraints.
- **Diagnose:** repair unreadable darkness, duplicate weapons, action-poster drift, magic effects, or costume-ad styling.
- **Direct image:** generate only when explicitly requested.

## Select a route

Read [references/visual-system.md](references/visual-system.md) before writing.

- `threshold-suspicion`: doorway, inn, gate, corridor, or table-based mistrust before violence.
- `rain-pursuit`: alley, roof, riverbank, forest path, horse route, or urban chase.
- `legible-ambush`: hidden attackers, terrain geometry, exits, and one readable rupture.
- `aftermath-return`: wound, abandoned weapon, empty room, burned paper, or return after consequence.

Subject intent chooses the route. A duel in rain is still an ambush/confrontation route, with rain as modifier.

## Lock before lighting

- exact adult subject count and role;
- relationship: hunter, target, ally, witness, betrayer, or uncertain;
- exact weapon count, owner, containment, hand, and direction;
- location map: entrances, exits, obstacles, elevation, and safe standing surfaces;
- action beat: before, rupture, consequence, or aftermath;
- era, wardrobe, weather, and injury level;
- visible violence limit;
- ratio and whether faces must remain readable.

## Direction workflow

1. State the moral question and one visible decision.
2. Map arena, exits, body spacing, sight lines, and weapon ownership.
3. Choose one dominant silhouette relationship.
4. Assign motivated light to doorway, lantern, moon, fire, window, or reflected water.
5. Preserve enough shadow detail to read feet, hands, exits, and terrain.
6. Couple rain, wind, cloth, dust, water, and blood to the action beat.
7. Apply one restrained color chapter from the reference.
8. Compile prompt and nearby negative constraints.
9. Audit geography, weapon continuity, violence level, identity, and noir tone.

## Default output

1. `冲突与路线`.
2. `场地和人物锁定`.
3. `ImageGen 中文提示词`.
4. `负面约束`.
5. `连续镜头方向` only when requested.

Read [references/reference-and-output.md](references/reference-and-output.md) for reference images, series, model-specific output, and drift repair.

## Boundaries

- Do not use darkness to conceal incoherent bodies, weapons, or terrain.
- Do not add glowing qi, spell circles, flying-sword energy, monsters, or superhero launches unless explicitly requested as fantasy.
- Do not glamorize graphic injury; keep violence at the user's requested level.
- Do not turn every woman into a seductive assassin or every man into a stoic killer.
- Do not rely on a filmmaker name, title, or generic `film noir` tail.
- Do not recreate an identifiable movie frame without an authorized reference.
- Generate images only on explicit request.
