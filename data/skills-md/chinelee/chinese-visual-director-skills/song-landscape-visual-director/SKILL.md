# Song Landscape Visual Director

Turn a landscape premise into an original image governed by classical Chinese spatial logic. Work from mountain-water relationships, paths, void, scale, brush structure, and material medium rather than a generic ink-wash filter.

## Modes

- **Create:** build one landscape from a place, season, journey, or emotional premise.
- **Derive:** preserve spatial DNA while changing season, route, color, or format.
- **Series:** create a scroll passage, album sequence, or four related scenes.
- **Reference-led:** inspect authorized/public-domain references and transfer only named principles.
- **Diagnose:** repair flat depth, decorative mist, pasted figures, photographic composition, or fantasy drift.
- **Direct image:** generate only when explicitly requested.

## Select a route

Read [references/visual-system.md](references/visual-system.md) for every task.

- `northern-monumental` for towering central mountains, dense structure, travelers, and deep routes.
- `southern-corner` for partial views, asymmetry, intimate banks, weather, and concentrated emptiness.
- `mist-river-journey` for water routes, layered banks, boats, villages, and atmospheric distance.
- `blue-green-realm` for mineral color, ceremonial clarity, terraces, and luminous but controlled ideal landscapes.

Use one primary route. Do not mix Northern monumental density and Southern corner emptiness without a clear hierarchy.

## Lock the picture

- format: hanging scroll, handscroll passage, album leaf, fan, horizontal screen, or modern crop;
- aspect ratio and reading direction;
- viewpoint system: high distance, deep distance, level distance, or an explicit combination with one dominant system;
- mountain/water proportion, path, architecture, boat, bridge, and exact human count;
- season, weather, time, and medium;
- monochrome ink, light color, or mineral color strategy;
- whether the result should remain painterly or translate into a photographic/3D medium.

## Direction workflow

1. Choose the dominant distance system and reading path.
2. Establish host mountain or primary water movement.
3. Place paths, streams, bridges, trees, roofs, and figures as scale and circulation evidence.
4. Treat blank space as cloud, water, sky, or separation with a structural job.
5. Build near, middle, far, and unreachably distant layers without Western one-point perspective by default.
6. Select brush families for rock, tree, water, cloud, and architecture.
7. Apply one medium and color chapter consistently.
8. Compile a prompt that describes visible relationships before historical labels.
9. Audit scale, route, void, brush logic, material, and fantasy drift.

## Default output

1. `画幅与路由`: format, distance system, route, medium, and color strategy.
2. `山水骨架`: host/guest masses, water, path, void, and human narrative.
3. `ImageGen 中文提示词`.
4. `负面约束`.
5. `衍生方向`: only when requested.

Read [references/reference-and-output.md](references/reference-and-output.md) for reference art, series, platform compilation, and drift repair.

## Boundaries

- Do not claim exact reconstruction of a museum work without a verified high-resolution source.
- Do not paste calligraphy, seals, colophons, signatures, or museum marks unless supplied and authorized.
- Do not turn structural blank space into uniform fog.
- Do not add floating islands, glowing palaces, giant moons, or game-concept spectacle unless the user explicitly requests fantasy.
- Do not render mountains as smooth 3D cones or photographic drone views when painterly Song logic is requested.
- Keep people tiny and purposeful; do not convert travelers into costume portraits.
- Generate images only on explicit request.
