# Analog Memory Photo Director

Build images that feel remembered because time, behavior, camera limitations, and physical print evidence agree. Nostalgia comes from ordinary relationships and imperfect capture, not from a sepia overlay.

## Modes

- **Create:** turn one memory fragment into a plausible snapshot.
- **Series:** freeze people, era, camera family, color process, and relationship across an album sequence.
- **Reference-led:** preserve authorized identities, clothing, location, or print damage.
- **Restore-direction:** describe a respectful cleanup while preserving original evidence.
- **Diagnose:** repair fake grain, random light leaks, period errors, over-staging, or identity drift.
- **Direct image/edit:** generate or edit only when explicitly requested.

## Select a route

Read [references/visual-system.md](references/visual-system.md) before writing.

- `family-daylight`: home, courtyard, school gate, picnic, birthday, or ordinary daytime gathering.
- `summer-travel`: road, lake, mountain, station, bus, beach, or holiday pause.
- `indoor-everyday`: kitchen, living room, shop, dormitory, restaurant, or domestic work.
- `night-direct-flash`: party, street food, karaoke, doorway, festival, or late-night candid moment.

Choose one capture condition and one aging condition. Do not add every defect.

## Lock evidence

- approximate era and region; mark uncertain details as assumptions;
- exact people and relationship, with authorization for real identities;
- ordinary action and who is looking at whom or the camera;
- camera distance, crop, focus error, flash/no flash, and likely exposure;
- film family: consumer color negative, black-and-white, slide-like, instant print, or unspecified analog;
- print state: fresh lab print, aged album print, faded scan, scratched negative, or clean archival scan;
- exact defects allowed and defects prohibited;
- whether visible date text is supplied, verified, or forbidden.

## Direction workflow

1. Build one ordinary social action before choosing defects.
2. Place people with plausible amateur timing and incomplete framing.
3. Choose capture physics: daylight, mixed indoor light, underexposure, direct flash, or motion.
4. Choose one film/color response and one print/scan state.
5. Add no more than two dominant imperfections.
6. Keep era evidence in clothing, objects, architecture, and behavior, not fake captions.
7. Compile prompt and nearest negative constraints.
8. Audit identity, era, action, exposure, defect causality, and sentimentality.

## Default output

1. `记忆设定与假设`.
2. `拍摄和冲印条件`.
3. `ImageGen 中文提示词`.
4. `负面约束`.
5. `相册序列` only when requested.

Read [references/reference-and-output.md](references/reference-and-output.md) for reference photos, restoration boundaries, series, and platform compilation.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent a real person's age, kinship, date, location, or life event.
- Do not add fake handwritten notes, timestamps, logos, or camera dates unless the exact text is supplied.
- Do not combine heavy grain, dust, scratches, burn, leak, blur, fading, vignette, and color shift indiscriminately.
- Do not make ordinary families look impoverished, exotic, comic, or tragic without user intent.
- Do not turn candid memory into polished fashion editorial.
- Preserve real faces before applying color or print aging.
- Generate or edit only on explicit request.
