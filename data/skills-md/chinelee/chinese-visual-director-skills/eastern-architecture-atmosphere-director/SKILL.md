# Eastern Architecture Atmosphere Director

Direct architectural imagery as evidence of structure, climate, movement, and inhabitation. Atmosphere must reveal space rather than cover mistakes.

## Modes

- **Create:** build an original but structurally credible architectural scene.
- **Reference-led:** preserve an existing building's geometry, material sequence, condition, and terrain.
- **Weather study:** change only time, season, rain, snow, fog, or light while locking architecture.
- **Adaptive-use study:** show old and new interventions without erasing either.
- **Series:** create exterior, threshold, circulation, interior, and detail views with shared truth.
- **Diagnose:** repair fantasy drift, impossible roofs, scale failure, flat light, decorative fog, or showroom styling.
- **Direct image/edit:** generate or edit only when explicitly requested.

## Select a route

Read [references/visual-system.md](references/visual-system.md) for every task.

- `monument-and-axis`: gate, hall, tower, temple, ceremonial path, or dominant public structure.
- `courtyard-and-threshold`: house, academy, inn, garden, corridor, doorway, or nested court.
- `inhabited-adaptive-use`: restored old building, cultural venue, retreat, shop, or mixed old/new program.
- `weather-and-material`: rain, snow, mist, dawn, night, seasonal vegetation, or aging study.

Use one route as the spatial organizer. Weather is not permission to change structure.

## Lock architecture

- verified vs inferred building type, region, date, and current condition;
- site plan, terrain, orientation, approach, and visible circulation;
- bay rhythm, columns/walls, beams, roof form, eaves, openings, platforms, stairs, and supports;
- drainage, ground contact, retaining walls, and weather response;
- material sequence, repair patches, patina, vegetation, and maintenance state;
- human scale, exact people, activity, and access;
- protected elements and permitted interventions;
- ratio, camera height, viewing distance, tilt, and depth requirement.

## Direction workflow

1. Identify the load-bearing and circulation story.
2. Choose one dominant spatial reading: axis, threshold sequence, courtyard nesting, diagonal circulation, or framed view.
3. Establish at least three scale references.
4. Describe roof, support, platform, opening, ground, and drainage relationships.
5. Place weather where it physically collects, reflects, freezes, dries, or scatters light.
6. Preserve surface age and repairs without making the building abandoned by default.
7. Add people only to explain use and scale.
8. Compile prompt, negative constraints, and uncertainty notes.
9. Audit geometry, material, climate, use, camera, and factual claims.

## Default output

1. `建筑事实与假设`.
2. `空间路线和机位`.
3. `ImageGen 中文提示词`.
4. `负面约束`.
5. `系列镜头` only when requested.

Read [references/reference-and-output.md](references/reference-and-output.md) for source images, weather edits, series, platform compilation, and repair.

## Boundaries

- Do not alter a referenced building's roofline, openings, terrain, or structural rhythm without permission.
- Do not invent heritage status, dynasty, architect, ritual meaning, restoration date, or public access.
- Do not make all Chinese architecture red-and-gold imperial palaces.
- Do not use fog, darkness, foliage, or shallow focus to hide structural incoherence.
- Do not add railings, stairs, bridges, doors, or windows solely for decoration when they break circulation or period logic.
- Do not remove weathering to create a real-estate rendering unless requested.
- Generate or edit only on explicit request.
