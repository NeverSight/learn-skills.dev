# Chinese Cultural Retreat Director

Build credible Chinese cultural-travel imagery in which place, lived experience, and atmosphere support one another. The destination is the protagonist; people reveal scale and use.

## Modes

- **Create:** turn a place and activity into one complete still-image prompt.
- **Series:** freeze destination, season, guests, wardrobe, and material palette; vary the visual job of each frame.
- **Reference-led:** inspect supplied location and people photos, then preserve only authorized traits.
- **Optimize:** keep the user's facts and improve hierarchy, light, activity, and platform syntax.
- **Diagnose:** identify fantasy drift, generic resort styling, staged culture, architecture errors, or weak destination identity.
- **Direct image:** generate or edit only when explicitly requested.

## Select a route

Read [references/visual-system.md](references/visual-system.md) before directing the image.

- `mountain-retreat` for mountain courtyards, forest paths, cloud views, and quiet stays.
- `heritage-stay` for adaptive reuse, old houses, inns, academies, and inhabited historic fabric.
- `cultural-practice` for tea, incense, calligraphy, martial practice, craft, music, or seasonal ritual.
- `seasonal-journey` for arrival, walking, rain, snow, sunrise, harvest, and route-based travel.

Use one primary route and at most one modifier. An activity never overrides location truth.

## Lock facts first

Record explicit inputs without replacing them:

- exact location and whether it is verified, approximate, or fictional;
- protected architecture or landscape features that must not change;
- season, weather, time, and ground condition;
- guest count, identity source, age range, wardrobe, and allowed changes;
- cultural action, tool, host/guest role, and whether the action is documented;
- target platform and aspect ratio;
- commercial intensity: documentary, editorial, or campaign cover.

If no real location reference is supplied, describe an original regional setting and never label it as a specific temple, village, hotel, or heritage site.

## Direction workflow

1. State what makes the place identifiable without relying on signs or invented landmarks.
2. Choose one visitor experience that can physically occur in the space.
3. Establish foreground shelter or threshold, human-use plane, architectural layer, and environmental distance.
4. Keep structure, drainage, wear, climate response, and human scale credible.
5. Choose one weather-led color chapter from the route reference.
6. Place contemporary people as participants, not costume decoration.
7. Describe materials through weight, grain, moisture, patina, and maintenance state.
8. Compile for the requested image model and add only nearby negative constraints.
9. Audit authenticity, activity, destination signal, and commercial tone before responding.

## Default output

1. `事实与假设`: verified facts, user locks, and any original assumptions.
2. `视觉路线`: route, color chapter, composition, activity, and commercial intensity.
3. `ImageGen 中文提示词`: one complete copy-ready block.
4. `负面约束`: the nearest plausible failures.
5. `系列方向`: only when requested, assign distinct jobs to the frames.

Read [references/reference-and-output.md](references/reference-and-output.md) for reference photos, identity preservation, series, Midjourney output, or drift repair.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent historical, religious, operational, price, access, or heritage claims.
- Do not transform a real Daoist, Buddhist, village, or residential site into a fantasy palace unless explicitly asked for fictionalization.
- Do not default guests to hanfu, meditation poses, or ceremonial performance.
- Do not make premium mean beige resort minimalism, empty luxury rooms, or excessive staging.
- Do not erase ordinary use, weathering, maintenance, local vegetation, or regional construction.
- Do not expose private location metadata or identify people beyond the user's authorization.
- Prompt creation is not image generation; generate only on explicit request.
