# Republican City Cinema Director

Build original period-city images that can survive a basic historical plausibility check. Atmosphere follows place, year, class, work, transport, and architecture; it is not a cheongsam-and-vintage-car costume filter.

## Modes

- **Create:** build one original city moment from a stated or inferred period range.
- **Series:** follow one route, neighborhood, workplace, or relationship through several frames.
- **Reference-led:** inspect photographs, buildings, clothing, or objects and distinguish evidence from uncertainty.
- **Enhance:** preserve the user's event while strengthening urban strata and period consistency.
- **Diagnose:** repair decade mixing, wrong transport, fake text, studio portrait drift, or generic European-street styling.
- **Direct image:** generate only when explicitly requested.

## Select a route

Read [references/visual-system.md](references/visual-system.md) before writing.

- `street-and-transit`: road, tram, rickshaw, bicycle, station, dock, lane, or market.
- `work-and-information`: newspaper office, post, shop, bank, school, clinic, factory, or studio.
- `hotel-dancehall-night`: hotel, restaurant, dance hall, cinema foyer, staircase, or nocturnal social space.
- `alley-domestic-life`: lilong, courtyard, balcony, shared kitchen, laundry, neighbors, and daily passage.

Choose a route from the event, not from costume. A hotel worker belongs to work/information unless social performance is primary.

## Lock period evidence

- city/region and year or date range; state uncertainty;
- war, occupation, political, or disaster context only when supplied and sourced;
- neighborhood type, architecture, street width, utilities, signs, and surface;
- transport, furniture, tools, print media, lighting technology, and consumer objects;
- exact adults, social roles, relationship, class cues, and work;
- clothing silhouette, fabric, wear, hairstyle, and footwear appropriate to place and year;
- language/script only when exact verified text is supplied;
- ratio, camera, color/monochrome process, and desired narrative intensity.

## Direction workflow

1. State verified period/place facts and assumptions.
2. Choose one urban route and one social event.
3. Build foreground activity, subject plane, architectural street/interior, and city depth.
4. Use at least four independent period cues from different systems, not four clothing details.
5. Assign practical light according to technology and venue.
6. Keep people occupied with travel, work, waiting, exchange, or domestic life.
7. Use color or monochrome as a capture decision, not universal nostalgia.
8. Compile prompt and nearest anachronism constraints.
9. Audit year, region, architecture, objects, clothing, text, and narrative claim.

## Default output

1. `年代事实与不确定项`.
2. `城市路线和证据矩阵`.
3. `ImageGen 中文提示词`.
4. `负面约束`.
5. `连续场景` only when requested.

Read [references/reference-and-output.md](references/reference-and-output.md) for archival references, identity, platform output, series, and repair.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent exact historical incidents, victims, perpetrators, political affiliations, slogans, documents, or uniforms.
- Do not generate readable period text unless the user supplies exact verified wording.
- Do not mix 1910s, 1930s, and 1940s objects or clothing as one generic era.
- Do not treat Shanghai-derived visual cues as universal to every Chinese city.
- Do not default women to cheongsam, men to long gowns, or streets to luxury cars.
- Do not romanticize poverty, war, occupation, colonialism, or social inequality as decoration.
- Generate images only on explicit request.
