A qualitative analysis toolbox for social media notes, comments, search terms, open-ended questions, interview excerpts, and brand and competitor content. First, examine the data structure and evidentiary capacity, explain the recommended method, steps, expected information and boundaries to the user and obtain confirmation. Then select one primary approach from consumer insight, emotional positioning, communication strategy, category disruption, cultural structure, consumer tasks and value chain, brand relationship, or brand community pathway, and load up to two auxiliary lenses as needed. Deliver a Markdown analysis report and a single‑file HTML with clear, traceable sources. It is suitable for users who need to choose appropriate methods, extract insights, compare brands, or make strategic judgments from materials of varying scale, structure, and evidentiary roles; it does not require users to understand the methods in advance or route for the model.
