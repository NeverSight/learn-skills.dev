Compare two SQL Server workload captures — a baseline and a current period, a fast day and a slow day, before and after an upgrade or configuration change, or production against test — to find what regressed and why. Applies 14 checks (WC1–WC14) covering capture comparability, per-query regressions and improvements matched by query_hash, new and disappeared heavy queries, systemic versus isolated regression, regression class (CPU-, reads- or wait-driven), execution-volume shifts, sp_configure, server property, database option and database-scoped configuration differences, and wait-profile shifts. Use this skill when someone says "it was fast last week", "it got slow after the upgrade", "compare these two traces", "what changed between Monday and Tuesday", or pastes two Query Store, trace or DMV captures and asks what is different. Trigger whenever the question is a comparison between two periods or two servers rather than a review of one capture.
