Writing principles for prompts, instructions, system prompts, delegation statements, and skill or instruction files given to AI agents, as well as for drafting, revising, and reviewing LLM judge prompts and rubric text. These principles are used even when not explicitly stated during the design of agent services and skill instruction texts. They address high‑level descriptions, naming of already learned concepts, switching between processes and mental models, and descriptions that take advantage of model evolution.
