Used when the agent is told they do not understand the immediate previous response they wrote themselves, or the words or explanations within it. Prompts such as “make it easy to understand,” “in other words?,” “concise,” “what does it mean?,” “rephrase it?,” “in plain terms?,” “so that even an elementary school student can understand,” as well as questions about a word that appeared in the response (“what is ~?”) or about items listed in the response (“what is the difference between ~ and ~?”) apply. It also applies when asked for the same meaning in another language. Rephrase in language that makes sense to someone who does not know the background of the work, and based on the requested wording, choose either to summarize briefly or to add premises. General questions unrelated to the response (e.g., “What is REST?”) are answered as usual. If asked about the progress of the session, use “session-status.”
