From documents (e.g., README, specifications, design docs, proposals, procedures, rules, SKILL.md, prompts, etc.), locate and correct meta descriptions (sentences that talk about the document itself or how it was created) and rewrite them as sentences that describe the actual subject. Use this when told to “exclude meta descriptions” or “check the meta descriptions.” Even if not asked, apply it before delivering a document that will be read on its own. Do not use it for replies to users or work reports. In documents handed to people, apply it before the “review‑for‑readers” stage.
