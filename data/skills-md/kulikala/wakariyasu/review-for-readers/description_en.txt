Review documents meant to be handed over, sent, or published (such as READMEs, specifications, procedures, proposals, regulations, SKILL.md, etc.) from the perspective of a reader who doesn’t know the background in which they were written, and fix any problematic points. Use this when you’re told to “review it from the reader’s viewpoint,” “look at it before publishing,” or “check whether it’s clear to someone reading it for the first time.” Even if no one mentions it, apply this before finalizing a document that will be given to others. Have another agent—who isn’t provided with the background—read only the document, correct the issues that surface, and review it again.
