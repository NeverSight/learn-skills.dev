Make small hand-drawn stick-figure scenes for landing pages and sites, where a stick figure works a machine or prop that shows what a product does (pull a lever, pin a note, stamp a page). Each one is an interactive canvas widget, `<stick-scene>`, that idles quietly and plays one short action on click, hover or scroll into view. Use when the user asks for a stick figure animation, a "worker" or "crew" scene, an animated explainer or doodle widget for a landing page, something "like the stick figures on my story page", or wants a product's feature shown as a little animated scene.
