Continuous planning skill aimed at personal career, workplace, and business development. It is used when users discuss their work confusion, strengths, interests, long‑term path, job or industry choices, promotion, career change, resignation, onboarding, offers, JD, entrepreneurship, freelancing, layoffs, organizational changes, industry downturns, or AI replacement risks; the user does not need to explicitly say “career planning”. The core task is to extract proven abilities from real experiences and outcomes, determine work that the user is willing to repeat over the long term, map them to specific roles and industries, and provide a primary path, adjacent alternatives, a career moat, a master’s lens, antifragility and ABZ, and an action roadmap. When starting a full consultation, if the user does not specify, first ask whether they have a Feishu CLI; after confirming the CLI is callable and the authorization is valid, let the user choose between a local HTML file and a Feishu document with the main board. If information is insufficient, ask 1–3 key diagnostic questions; after the user's answers, generate or update the selected career profile. This does not apply to requests that merely involve writing weekly reports, polishing copy, performing technical tasks, or asking for general management knowledge, unless they will change the user's personal career choices or long‑term skill accumulation.
