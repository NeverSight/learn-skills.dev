Decide whether a failure belongs to native Termux or a proot distro and which side should own the package, interpreter, and project path. Use when something works in one environment but not the other, when Ubuntu or Debian runs under Termux, when a glibc-only binary is needed, or before installing, backing up, or resetting a proot distro. Do NOT use for ordinary native Termux package errors, and never use proot-distro reset or remove as a generic repair.
