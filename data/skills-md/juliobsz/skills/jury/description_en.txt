Score any website the way an Awwwards jury would, from evidence alone. Screenshots every page type on desktop, a throttled phone and reduced motion, measures LCP, CLS, FPS, axe, keyboard focus and hover feedback, audits SEO and metadata, then a fresh critic that never sees the code scores Design, Usability, Creativity, Content and the six developer areas, with a ranked fix list. Use when someone asks to review, critique, score, rate or roast a website, portfolio or landing page, asks whether a site is good enough for Awwwards or Site of the Day, or wants design QA of a live URL or a local build. Not for building or redesigning a site; use /awwwards for that.
