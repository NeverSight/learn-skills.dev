Investigates kinship ties and family history using personal data and documents with a focus on the former USSR, the USSR, and the Russian Empire. To be used for genealogical searching, matching of namesakes, archival, metric, military, migration and repression records, constructing evidence chains and final reports. Not to be used for stalking living people, searching for their current contacts or addresses, accessing closed data, bypassing protection, and asserting biological kinship without independent verification.
