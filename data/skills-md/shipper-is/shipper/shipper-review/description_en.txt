Orchestrate a QUIPS code review (Quality, Uptime, Intent, Performance, Security) of a PR or branch. Deterministic scripts build the risk floor and compute the verdict; tiered lane and persona subagents find issues; blockers must carry proof and survive a cross-family fact-check; a tech-lead judge writes the scorecard. Produces one report headed by a verdict and per-lane grades, posted to the PR and persisted in `<reviews>/`. Use before requesting human review on a PR, or whenever the user asks for a thorough review of changes.
