Makes a Minecraft-style blocky voxel animation for a reel. A cottage builds itself block by block on a grassy hill, about 165 blocks dropping in with a bounce, then the chimney slams in with a puff of dust, the windows light up warm, smoke rises and a big pixel-font word lands. Procedural 16x16 pixel textures, blocky trees and flat blocky clouds, rendered with three.js. Use when the user runs /minecraft or asks for a Minecraft-style, voxel or blocky animation, or a build-it-block-by-block visual.
