PRD review / requirement review: Use this when the user already has a PRD draft, handoff, requirement document, or product plan and needs to identify gaps, conflicts, unimplementable points, and untestable points from the perspectives of PM, development, and testing. It can be invoked in Chinese with phrases such as “Help me review the PRD,” “Find issues from a development and testing perspective,” “Can this requirement document be handed off to development,” “Help me draft revisions to the PRD,” “Check whether PRD diagrams are missing or non‑editable.” It is not for generating a PRD draft from scratch, writing code directly, or conducting a Q&A stress test on the mature solution behind the PRD; for solution stress testing, use grill‑me.
