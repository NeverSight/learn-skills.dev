Collaborative Calibration / Cognitive Calibration / Problem Brainstorming: Use when the user has a vague feeling and can’t clearly describe the problem, the solution keeps getting more complicated, they want to challenge assumptions, redefine the problem, or learn what a mature field calls this issue. It can be triggered in Chinese with phrases like “帮我想想” (“help me think”), “先聊一下” (“let’s talk first”), “一起脑暴” (“brainstorm together”), “我感觉有问题但不清楚是什么” (“I sense there’s a problem but I’m not sure what it is”), “先别执行，帮我看清问题” (“don’t act yet, help me see the problem”), “挑战我的假设” (“challenge my assumptions”), “这个方案是不是想错了” (“is this solution mistaken?”), “帮我做认知校准” (“help me do cognitive calibration”), “双向钢人” (“bidirectional steel‑man”), “钢人论证一下” (“steel‑man the argument”), “正反最强论证，给我明确判断” (“strongest pro‑ and contra‑arguments, give me a clear judgment”). It also applies when the user directly says “帮我做 X 功能” (“help me build feature X”) or “接入 Y 平台” (“integrate Y platform”) but the background is complex, or early conversation hints that the direction may be off; the AI should proactively recognize this and suggest entering brainstorming mode. Here, “challenge assumptions” mainly refers to assumptions about problem definition, goals, constraints, and domain positioning. If the problem is already confirmed and the user has a concrete solution, plan, architecture, or decision that needs stress‑testing, switch to the “grill‑me” mode. This is not for translation, summarization, formatting, minor single‑file edits, or other simple execution tasks.
