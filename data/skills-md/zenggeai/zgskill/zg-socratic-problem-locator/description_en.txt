By using expert hypotheses, Socratic testing, and key evidence, help users quickly pinpoint the truly worth‑solving issues from vague confusion, abnormal results, hasty attributions, or self‑labeling. Suitable for scenarios such as “Help me find the real problem,” “Don’t give solutions yet, ask me step by step,” “Why did I act but get no result,” “I think maybe I’m not capable,” “Please check if my judgment is valid,” and also for cases where users provide chat logs, data, screenshots, or files and want the AI to locate the breakpoint based on the material. The default is natural short dialogue; it is not intended for pure factual queries, simple task execution, or when the user has already presented a clear, verifiable question and explicitly requests a direct solution.
