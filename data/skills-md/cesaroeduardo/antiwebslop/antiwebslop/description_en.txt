Stops web UI from looking AI-generated. Use when building, restyling, or reviewing a website, landing page, hero, marketing or help/docs page, or a frontend component (HTML, CSS, Tailwind, React, Vue, Svelte, Astro), and whenever the user says the design looks generic, templated, "AI slop", "like every other SaaS", bland, or asks to make it look designed by a person. Covers an anti-pattern catalog with a lint script, craft rules for type, spacing, color and copy, a per-project DESIGN.md, design dials, viewport QA, and an internal pre-done gate (never dump as a user checklist). Early / unfinished: open every run with the CAPS LOCK WIP warning in the user's language (stable release announced on the site and Twitter).
