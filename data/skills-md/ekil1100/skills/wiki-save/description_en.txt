Use the Obsidian CLI to save conversation conclusions, research summaries, plans, or decisions to a Wiki. Apply this when the user requests “save to wiki,” “store the recent discussion in the knowledge base,” “record this knowledge point,” “record this plan,” “record this decision,” or when supplementing an existing Wiki page; if no other storage location is specified, such requests are handled as saving to the Wiki without needing to mention the Wiki explicitly. It handles duplicate detection, evidence and privacy checks, targeted writing, and verification. Regular Q&A is not saved automatically; full or incremental source ingestion and full‑library linting follow the processes for the target repository.
