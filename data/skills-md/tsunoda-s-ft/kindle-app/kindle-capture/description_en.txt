Capture screenshots from Kindle Web Reader or the Kindle macOS app and generate PDFs. Automatically convert Kindle books to PDFs by specifying the book title or ASIN. Supports Kindle library search, automated page retrieval with Playwright, PNG‑to‑PDF conversion, layout settings (single/double), range selection, quality adjustment, and resizing. If title extraction fails, the cover image is recognized by AI and used for naming.
