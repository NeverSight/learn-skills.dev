Shape a feature as a product before anyone plans how to build it — the sentence, the ladder of use cases smallest to largest, what stays hidden, and the open product questions. Use when the user invokes /draft-experience <feature>, asks what a feature should be, asks whether something is simple enough, or wants the experience document that a technical design, roadmap or plan is then written from. Also use to check an existing feature, bug or proposal against the product shape it claims to serve. Writes docs/<feature>/EXPERIENCE.rev1.md, which is reviewed on its own before any technical planning starts.
