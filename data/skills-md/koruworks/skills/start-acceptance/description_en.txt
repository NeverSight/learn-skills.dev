Open an acceptance-testing session — the user is about to try finished work by hand. Points them at the feature's ACCEPTANCE.md, or writes one from what actually changed when there is none, then takes their notes, screenshots and corrections one at a time, recording each verdict beside its check and fixing or carrying what it finds. Anything that looks like a pattern rather than an incident — a missed requirement, a recurring bug, a design error — is written as a candidate to docs/acceptances/ATTENTION.<feature>.md for end-acceptance to promote, with the user, into docs/acceptances/ATTENTION.md. Runs no test suite during the session, because it is too slow while a person waits; end-acceptance runs it once. Use when the user says they are starting acceptance, about to test or try the feature, or invokes /start-acceptance <feature>.
