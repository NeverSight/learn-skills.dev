Close out a finished roadmap or feature execution by retiring its planning docs into canonical documentation and carrying what is still owed into the next roadmap — you are the archivist and the architect of the next round, not the coder. Use when the user invokes /close-roadmap <feature>, or asks to clean up a feature's docs, retire a roadmap, prepare for the next roadmap, or tidy docs/<feature>/ after execute-roadmap or build-feature has finished. Reviews ACCEPTANCE.md check by check, triages every item of ROADMAP.final.md or PLAN.final.md as landed, pending, deferred or dropped; moves pending work into ROADMAP-V2.rev1.md; turns landed work into decision records, architecture, data-model and product docs; writes the folder's README.md; deletes revs and execution artifacts the tree no longer needs; and fixes every inbound link. Nothing disappears unaccounted for; the user settles anything that would be dropped. For a small plan whose landed work folds into pages that already exist, use close-plan instead.
