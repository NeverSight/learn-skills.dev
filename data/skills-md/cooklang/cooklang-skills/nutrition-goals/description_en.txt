Use when the user wants to change AN EXISTING Cooklang recipe (.cook) or meal plan (.menu) so it hits numeric nutrition targets ("make this 600 kcal per serving", "get my week to 150 g protein a day", "more protein, same calories"). Measures with the Cook nutrition service, edits, and verifies. Not for evaluation-only questions (nutrition-reports), and not for picking or screening recipes that already meet a number (nutrition-reports screening, then meal-planning).
