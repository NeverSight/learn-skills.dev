Use when the user wants a nutrition or dietitian-style evaluation of a Cooklang recipe (.cook) or meal plan (.menu) - calories, macros, micronutrients, passed/failed checks against targets, exclusions/allergens, a plan's per-day nutrition - or wants to SCREEN recipes that already meet a number ("which of my recipes are over 35% protein?"). Not for changing a recipe or plan to hit targets - that is nutrition-goals.
