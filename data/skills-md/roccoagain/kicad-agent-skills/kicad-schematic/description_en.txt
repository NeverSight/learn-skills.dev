Edits or draws a KiCad 10 schematic with sch.py, a small Python library in this skill's folder: adds symbols (including multi-unit parts), wires, labels, power symbols and no-connects by pin, changes values and fields, then checks with ERC and carries the change to the board. Use when making any schematic change, such as adding a part, connecting nets or changing a value, and when drawing a new schematic. To create a symbol, use kicad-library.
