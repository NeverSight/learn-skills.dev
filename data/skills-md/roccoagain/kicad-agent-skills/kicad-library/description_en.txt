Creates or fixes KiCad 10 symbols and footprints for parts that aren't in KiCad's libraries: land patterns from the datasheet or IPC-7351, pin 1, courtyard and silk, 3D models, pin stacks, and symbol pins matched to footprint pads. Also sets up project library tables. Use when a part needs a new symbol or footprint, or a footprint might be wrong. To place an existing symbol, use kicad-schematic.
