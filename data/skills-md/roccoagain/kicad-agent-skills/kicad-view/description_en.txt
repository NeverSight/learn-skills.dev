Shows a KiCad 10 schematic or PCB with view.py (this skill's folder): KiCad's own plots cropped to named parts, each pin's net from KiCad's netlist, resistance between nets, checks of stated connections and decoupling, a 3D render, and a comparison of two git revisions. Use whenever a design needs looking at, before and after any edit, to check a connection, to see what a commit changed, or when the user asks to see or screenshot a board or schematic.
