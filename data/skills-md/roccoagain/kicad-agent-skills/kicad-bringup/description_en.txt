Writes or updates a board's bring-up and test plan (TEST_PLAN.md) from its KiCad schematic: visual checks, unpowered resistance readings computed from the netlist, current-limited first power, block-by-block function tests, and a per-board log. Use when a board is about to be ordered or assembled, when the user asks how to test or bring up a board, or after a schematic change that moves a reading.
