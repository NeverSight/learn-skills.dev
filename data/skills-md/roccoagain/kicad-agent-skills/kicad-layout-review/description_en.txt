Reviews a finished KiCad 10 PCB layout against its datasheets and its circuit: datasheet layout sections, decoupling distances, regulator and crystal loops, return paths under fast signals, differential pairs, track widths for current, thermal copper, ground pours, ESD parts at connectors, connectors and mounting against the enclosure, silkscreen polarity marks, assembly and test access. Reports findings worst first with board locations and proposed fixes; makes no edits. Use when a layout is finished or changed, before ordering, or when the user asks whether a board's layout is good. For the schematic's electrical design, use kicad-schematic-review; for DRC and manufacturing limits, kicad-check.
