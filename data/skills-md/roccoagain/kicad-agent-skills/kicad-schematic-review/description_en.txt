Reviews a KiCad schematic's electrical design (the circuit, not the board) against datasheets: what each connector can see (power states, misuse, ESD), absolute maximum ratings, clamp current, resistor and capacitor ratings and derating, floating inputs, interfaces between boards. Use before ordering, after a schematic change, when adding a part, or when the user asks whether an input can take a voltage or whether the design survives a fault. For the board layout, use kicad-layout-review; for ERC, DRC and manufacturing limits, kicad-check.
