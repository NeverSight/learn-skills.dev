Exports Gerbers, drill files and the JLCPCB fab zip from a KiCad 10 board, after a DRC check, and verifies the files. Also exports STEP and DXF files for enclosures. Use when the user asks for Gerbers, a fab zip, files to order a PCB, or a STEP/DXF, or after a board change in a project that keeps its fab zip. For the BOM and CPL files JLC assembly needs, use kicad-assembly-export.
