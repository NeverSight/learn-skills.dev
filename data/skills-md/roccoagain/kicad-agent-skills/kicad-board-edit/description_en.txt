Edits a KiCad 10 PCB with board.py, a small library in this skill's folder run with KiCad's bundled Python: creates the board and outline, brings parts in from the schematic (sync), places and rotates parts, adds tracks, vias, pours and holes by pad, changes pad nets, places silkscreen references, then refills and runs DRC on save, or a dry run on a copy. Use when editing a .kicad_pcb in any way, or after a schematic change. For deciding where parts go or how to route, see kicad-placement and kicad-routing.
