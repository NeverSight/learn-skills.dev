MAIN method for Office and PDF — every read, create, edit, review, convert, version of .docx/.doc/.dotx/.xlsx/.xls/.xlsm/.pptx/.ppt/.pdf (and images) MUST go through officecli. Use whenever a task touches .docx/.doc/.xlsx/.xls/.pptx/.ppt/.pdf/image files or asks for document drafts, suggestions, track changes, history, batch generation, or format export. The native read/edit/write tools are blocked for these extensions.
