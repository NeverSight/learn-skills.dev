Build in-store card payments with Surfboard Payments: create an order against a terminal, initiate a CARD/SWISH/KLARNA payment, poll for status, and issue a receipt. Covers SurfTouch, SurfPad, SurfPrint, SurfMini, CheckoutX SoftPOS, inter-app integration, tips, and partial payments. Use for POS, till, kiosk, or any integration driving a physical payment terminal.
