Nüwa creates humans: input a name, topic, or even just a vague request, automatically conduct deep research → refine a thinking framework → generate a runnable character skill.  
Two entry points: (1) specific name → direct distillation; (2) vague request → diagnostic recommendation → then distillation.  
Trigger words: “create skill”, “distill XX”, “Nüwa”, “create human”, “XX’s way of thinking”, “take an XX perspective”, “update XX’s skill”.  
Vague requests also trigger: “I want to improve decision quality”, “Is there a way of thinking that can help me…”, “I need a thinking consultant”.
