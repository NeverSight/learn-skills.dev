Nüwa creates humans: input a name/topic/even just a vague request, automatically conduct deep research → extract a thinking framework → generate a runnable character skill. Two entry points: (1) explicit name → direct distillation (2) vague request → diagnostic recommendation → further distillation. Trigger words: "create skill", "distill XX", "Nüwa", "create human", "XX's way of thinking", "take an XX perspective", "update XX's skill". Vague requests also trigger: "I want to improve decision quality", "Is there a way of thinking that can help me…", "I need a thinking consultant".
