Upgrade a PostgreSQL app from Prisma ORM 6 or 7 straight to Prisma 8 in one pass, keeping the existing schema.prisma, database and data. Use when a Postgres project on Prisma 6 or 7 wants Prisma 8, including before deploying it on Prisma Compute, which runs only Prisma 8. Triggers on "upgrade prisma 6 to 8", "upgrade prisma 7 to 8", "upgrade to prisma 8 postgres", "prisma7Schema", "migrations/app", "move off @prisma/client", "Console says move to Prisma 8".
