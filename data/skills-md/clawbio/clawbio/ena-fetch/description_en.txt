Query metadata and download sequencing data from the European Nucleotide Archive (ENA) via the Portal and Browser APIs. Works with ENA/SRA accessions (PRJEB/PRJNA studies, ERR/SRR/DRR runs, ERX/SRX experiments, SAMEA/SAMN samples), listing runs and FASTQ links, building custom file reports, running advanced metadata searches, and emitting a standardised metadata.tsv plus a pipeline-ready nf-core/rnaseq or nf-core/scrnaseq samplesheet.csv.
