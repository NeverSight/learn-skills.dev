Query metadata and download data from the NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO). Works with GEO accessions (GSE series, GSM samples, GPL platforms, GDS datasets) to search GEO DataSets, fetch series and sample metadata, list and download series matrix / SOFT / MINiML / supplementary files, fetch the SRA Run Selector files, and emit a standardised metadata.tsv plus a pipeline-ready nf-core/rnaseq or nf-core/scrnaseq samplesheet.csv.
