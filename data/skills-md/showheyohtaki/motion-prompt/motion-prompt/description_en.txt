Motion Prompt (showheyohtaki.github.io/motion-prompt) is a numbered library of names for motion-graphics moves, camera moves and transitions, UI components, landing-page and web effects, and photo looks and lighting, each with a moving sample, a spec of its timings and easing, and dependency-free Canvas 2D code. The main use: open the gallery for the user with the pick command, let them tick what they want, receive their picks, then rebuild exactly those in the project (Canvas, Remotion, CSS/SVG/Lottie, After Effects) or turn them into prompts for video or image models. Use when the user wants to see or choose from the library, e.g. "open the motion-prompt library", "show me the list", "what moves are there?", "I don't know the name, let me pick", "motion-prompt のライブラリ出して", "一覧見せて", "どんな演出がある？", "分からないから選びたい"; when they paste picks copied from the gallery; or when they give element numbers, e.g. "use #012 and #045", "今のタイトルを #005 に差し替えて".
