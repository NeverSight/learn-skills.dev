The unified audio‑video transcription entry for Yichen's own use routes between StepFun Step ASR and Volcano Engine Doubao ASR according to output requirements, safety boundaries, and availability status. It is used for pure text transcription, timestamps, SRT subtitles, rough voice‑over editing, and pre‑transcription health checks of local audio or video; when the user explicitly specifies a provider, silent switching is not allowed. Use when a local audio or video file needs transcription and the correct ASR backend is unclear, or when the user explicitly invokes $yichen-asr.
