The official WeCom CLI entry point for Enterprise WeChat. It is used to create regular or smart documents from local Markdown, generate smart documents containing local images after the user configures an image upload helper, read or overwrite WeCom documents, create and manage tasks, and, when corporate permissions are enabled, schedule, query, update, or cancel meetings and events. It is used when users mention “WeCom document,” “smart document,” “upload Markdown,” “schedule meeting,” “WeCom meeting,” “WeCom schedule,” or “WeCom task”; it is not used for message sending or Enterprise WeChat client operations.
