WeChat Local Digital Asset Vault: Full and incremental parsing for Mac 4.x, and an experimental Windows decrypted offline snapshot query. Windows snapshots must be accessed via the `snapshot` subcommand and do not trigger Mac incremental refresh. The Mac mode is used for extracting keys for local WeChat chat records, contacts, group chats, Moments, favorites, voice/attachment indexes; performing full decryption, incremental refresh, exporting specified contacts/group chats, relationship review, customer follow‑up, and content consolidation. Trigger words: WeChat parsing, WeChat full, WeChat incremental, chat records, export chat, Moments parsing, favorites parsing, customer follow‑up, yichen-wechat-local-vault.
