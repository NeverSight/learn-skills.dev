Content Packaging Workshop — the final step of the writing series. Handles the presentation layer after the content draft is finalized: title polishing, opening hook, layout optimization, punctuation cleaning (integrated with zan-punct-fix), image suggestions, platform adaptation. Does not modify the main text content. The six modules can be triggered independently.

Triggers: help me polish the title, this title isn’t good enough, how to write the opening, help me check the layout, paragraphs are too dense, punctuation check, help me review the images, which platform is this article suitable for, ready to publish—help me go through the packaging, help me see any presentation issues, the article’s content is fine but feels tiring to read, help me do a pre‑publish check.

EN: “polish the title”, “fix the opening hook”, “check layout”, “fix punctuation”, “review images”, “platform formatting”, “pre‑publish checklist”.

Not applicable: rewriting the main text content (→ plainspoken‑writing / editor‑revisor), content quality diagnostics (→ chinese‑write‑checker), voice calibration (→ voice‑dissolver).
