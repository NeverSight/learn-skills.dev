Herramienta de aceptación de calidad de contenido chino, la entrada principal (puerta) de la serie de escritura. Desde cuatro perspectivas—editor, lector, autor y plataforma—realiza una comprobación integral de salud (16 ítems) de un manuscrito chino, generando una puntuación, una lista de problemas y recomendaciones de enrutamiento. No hay reescritura, solo aceptación y enrutamiento.

Desencadenantes: “Help me see how this is written,” “Is there any problem with this,” “Check this article,” “Is this manuscript qualified,” “Help me review it,” “Article quality acceptance,” “Chinese writing diagnosis,” “Can this be published,” “What needs to be changed in the manuscript,” “How to change it.”

EN: “review my Chinese article,” “check this article quality,” “Chinese writing diagnosis,” “proofread this piece,” “is this article good enough.”
