Chinese content quality acceptance tool, the overall entry point (gateway) for the writing series. It conducts a comprehensive health check (16 items) of a Chinese manuscript from four perspectives—editor, reader, author, and platform—producing a score, a list of issues, and routing recommendations. No editing is performed; only acceptance and routing.

Triggers: “Help me see how this is written,” “Does this have any problems,” “Check this article,” “Is this manuscript qualified,” “Help me review,” “Article quality acceptance,” “Chinese writing diagnosis,” “Can this be published,” “What needs to be changed in the manuscript,” “How to change it.”

EN: “review my Chinese article,” “check this article quality,” “Chinese writing diagnosis,” “proofread this piece,” “is this article good enough.”

Not applicable: direct rewriting (→ plainspoken‑writing / editor‑revisor), only change title/layout/punctuation (→ packaging‑workshop), cannot find one’s own voice (→ voice‑dissolver), verification of facts/data/quote authenticity (→ L0 fact‑review).
