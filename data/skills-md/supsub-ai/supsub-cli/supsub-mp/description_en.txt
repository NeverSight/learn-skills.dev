SupSub WeChat Official Account Discovery — used only when the user wants to search for or discover a WeChat Official Account itself (usually to obtain its mpId and then subscribe). Matches phrases such as “search official account X”, “find the X official account”, “look up X official account”, “want to subscribe to X official account but don’t know the ID”, “discover / find a WeChat MP account”. ⚠️ Do not use it to search article body/content/keyword‑related articles — that intent (e.g., “search content related to X”, “find articles related to X”) must go through supsub‑search. It also includes canceling an ongoing official‑account search task.
