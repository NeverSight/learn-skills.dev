SupSub subscription group management — create / rename / delete groups, view subscription sources within a group (with unread counts), add / remove subscription sources to/from groups. Match phrases like “my groups”, “new / create a group”, “add XX to YY group”, “remove XX from group”, “what subscriptions are in YY group”, “how many unread items are in this group”, “rename group”, “delete group”, “organize subscriptions / categorize subscriptions”. ⚠️ Disambiguation: **group** is a “folder” that collects subscribed sources, organized by sourceId + sourceType; **focus** is an AI‑generated content stream aggregated by topic (“my focus / what I follow” → supsub‑focus); subscription/unsubscription of the source itself is handled via supsub‑sub. Deleting a group is irreversible (the subscription sources inside the group remain unaffected).
