Use when a founder wants a hard business decision debated by a council of world-class operators, says "roast room", "put this in the Roast Room", "founder council", "convene the council", "take this to the board", "what would Hormozi, Bezos, or Buffett say", "should I do X", or can't make up their mind on pricing, hiring, raising money, a new product, a partnership, a pivot, an event, or spending. Seven sub-agents, each built from one expert's public work (Bezos, Paul Graham, Hormozi, Gary Vaynerchuk, Dan Martell, Gino Wickman, Buffett), argue it over three rounds, then a Chair returns a scored verdict, the reasoning, the dissent, and next steps. Also does a one-seat consult ("ask the Hormozi seat"). Not /roast (generic personas, idea validation).
