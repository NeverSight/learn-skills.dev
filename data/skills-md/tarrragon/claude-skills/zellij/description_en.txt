Zellij Terminal Multiplexing Management Guide. Use for: (1) Starting/stopping services in a Zellij pane, (2) Reading the screen output of other panes, (3) Managing multi‑pane layouts, (4) Remotely controlling other panes from a Claude pane. Use when: you need to run front‑end and back‑end services simultaneously across multiple terminals, need to read log output from other panes, and need to manage Zellij sessions and panes.
