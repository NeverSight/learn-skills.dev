Analysis procedure when taking over or collaborating on someone else’s project: declare at the entry that it is not in this framework’s project mode, inventory compilation, features, testing, and observability, and consolidate them into a work‑item list for delivery. Trigger words: take over project, project analysis, inventory someone else’s repo, collaborative project, external folder, the other party has no ticket system. Not used for projects that will be incorporated into this framework for continued development.
