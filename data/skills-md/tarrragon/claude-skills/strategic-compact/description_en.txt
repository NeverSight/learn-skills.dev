Strategic Context Compression Tool – Recommend manual /compact at logical boundaries to avoid auto‑compaction interrupting the workflow at inappropriate times. Use for: (1) when the exploration phase is complete and you’re about to move into implementation, (2) after completing a milestone and starting the next phase, (3) after extended debugging to resume normal development, (4) when the context becomes crowded after numerous tool calls. Use when: the user asks when to /compact, is worried the context will be truncated, or wants to clear irrelevant context after finishing an important milestone.
