Requirement completeness quality gate. Use for: (1) Initialize the functional specification skeleton at the start of Phase 1 (/spec init), (2) Validate the requirement completeness of the functional specification (/spec validate), (3) Determine whether the requirements are clear enough to proceed to implementation. Use when: When the Phase 1 functional design agent is performing functional design, used as an internal tool. Not a process entry point—/tdd manages process orchestration, /spec manages deliverable quality.
