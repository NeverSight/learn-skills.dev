WRAP decision framework — cognitive bias protection, option expansion, and data sufficiency gate. Used to guard against autonomous driving, false options, and hasty decisions made with insufficient evidence. Includes a complete premortem process (parallel deep dive into failure causes + comprehensive report), suitable for high‑cost decisions such as version planning, proposal evaluation, pre‑release, etc. Use when: stuck or experiencing two or more consecutive failures, about to announce a restrictive conclusion, deviating from core goals, conducting root‑cause analysis, attributing agent failure, evaluating proposals, making major architectural or policy decisions, before personalized recommendations (health/medical/financial/legal), when the context is heavy, or when a pre‑project risk simulation is needed. Triggers: stuck, blocked, loop, no progress, analysis, debug, restrictive solution, personalized recommendation, concrete recommendation, premortem, pre‑mortem autopsy, pre‑assumption failure, stress‑test plan, how this plan could die, uncover blind spots.
