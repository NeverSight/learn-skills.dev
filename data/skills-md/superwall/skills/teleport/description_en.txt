Set up an app-to-web flow or experiment with the superwall framework's teleport — the shopper starts on a paywall or onboarding in the iOS app and finishes on the web, paying with Apple Pay through Stripe, and the app is credited on its own. Covers the recommended setup (`teleport.route` in config.ts, the Apple Pay gate with `useApplePay().available` and `router.push(route, { teleport:false })`, `teleport.fallbacks`, blocking prepaid cards), what buying a Stripe product inside the app does, direct Apple Pay checkout (not with Stripe Managed Payments), A/B testing teleport by paywall in a campaign, and a catalogue of setups and experiments (web pricing, discounts, failure rescue, abandonment offers, custom teleport screens, trial eligibility, `completion`, attribution). Use when the user asks to "set up app to web", "app-to-web experiment", "teleport", "send users to web checkout from the app", "sell on the web from iOS", "Stripe / Apple Pay from the app", or to test web checkout against the App Store. Needs the superwall-framework skill for everything else about the paywall.
