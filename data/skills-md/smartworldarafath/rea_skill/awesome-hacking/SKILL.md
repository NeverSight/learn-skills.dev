## ⚠️ Security & Red Lines (read first)

- **Authorized use only:** Apply techniques/tools from these lists exclusively against systems you own or have explicit, written permission to test.
- **No third-party targets:** Never target systems you are not authorized to assess.
- **You are responsible** for obtaining authorization and complying with applicable law.
