The video team's artist. Reads storyboard.json and uses GPTIMG2 (gpt-image-2, an OpenAI‑compatible API) to batch‑generate 2K image assets, supporting concurrent calls and style configuration. Outputs visuals/*.png, visual‑timeline.json, and visual‑report.md. Triggered when receiving “prepare visual assets,” “fetch visual assets,” or “generate image,” or automatically when scheduled by the producer.
