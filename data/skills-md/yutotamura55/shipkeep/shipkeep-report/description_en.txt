Use after an inspection (shipkeep-publish-check, shipkeep-quality-tools, shipkeep-refactor, or another tool's findings converted to the shipkeep result format) to turn the failed items into GitHub Issues the user approves, without duplicates, and to keep the inspection history and the items the user decided not to fix in the repository's .shipkeep/ directory. Also lists checks that keep failing, as candidates for a machine-checked rule.
