Before designing test cases, first identify high‑risk business paths and the required verification assertions (oracle). Analyze requirement documents, code diffs, existing test cases, and context to pinpoint high‑danger areas such as funds, permissions, state machines, concurrency, asynchrony, and data consistency. Output for each risk the businessPath, requiredAssertions, suggestedTestLayers, and the coverage gap. Do not write business test cases before the risks are clarified. Not applicable: writing business test cases, writing test code, modifying product code.
