Before designing test cases, first identify high‑risk business paths and the required verification assertions (oracle). Analyze the requirements documents, code diffs, existing test cases, and context to locate high‑danger areas such as finance, permissions, state machines, concurrency, asynchronous behavior, and data consistency. Output for each risk its businessPath, requiredAssertions, suggestedTestLayers, and the coverage gap. Do not write business test cases before the risks are clarified. Not applicable: writing business test cases, writing test code, modifying product code.
