QA upstream stage — building a factual profile and environmental evidence for the repository. Triggered when you need to collect a module’s code context, index existing test cases and tests, check front‑end and back‑end service reachability, and prepare environment snapshots. Only gathers facts; no risk analysis, no test‑case writing, no quality assessment. Typically executed as the first stage under the scheduling of the quality‑assurance‑agent. Not applicable: risk analysis, test‑case design, test execution, quality determination (handled by downstream stages).
