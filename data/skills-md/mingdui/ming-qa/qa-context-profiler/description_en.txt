QA upstream stage — building a factual profile and environmental evidence for the repository. Triggered when you need to collect a module’s code context, index existing use cases and tests, check front‑ and back‑end service reachability, and prepare environment snapshots. Only collects facts; no risk analysis, no writing of use cases, no quality judgment. Typically executed as the first stage under the scheduling of the quality‑assurance‑agent. Not applicable: risk analysis, use‑case design, test execution, quality assessment (handled by downstream stages).
