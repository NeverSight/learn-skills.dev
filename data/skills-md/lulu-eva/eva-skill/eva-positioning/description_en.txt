Eva Positioning 2.5.0 account-stage positioning entry point. It is used when users explicitly invoke /eva-positioning, or explicitly request media account positioning, track positioning, positioning review, repositioning, determining the account’s main line, or when organizing the homepage around account positioning, to identify worthwhile investigation directions from real experiences and then use the platform reality provided by the user to create a currently actionable positioning. It supports rapid positioning and deep positioning; when there is no qualified platform evidence, only personal material direction and customized evidence‑gathering tasks are delivered, and no positioning candidates are generated. Only when the user explicitly places candidate topics into the business question “what to do first in the current account positioning or account stage,” or arranges the execution order based on an existing work‑positioning schedule, will account‑topic narrow bridging be performed. Bare “building a personal brand / account review,” general social profile edits, ordinary thinking, broad topics, standard content calendars, single‑piece content creation, low‑traffic account content diagnosis, persona material collection, product and service collection, commercial brief, or third‑party account analysis must not be captured by this entry point.
