Video and podcast transcription: after downloading the audio or video, call the Quark cloud drive Skill to retrieve the manuscript from the cloud, then perform light proofreading with a model. Supports single and batch processing, already uploaded cloud drive files, persistent mapping relationships, and interruption recovery. Used for “verbatim transcripts, text conversion, copy extraction” or extraction requests that include audio/video links. By default, does not run local ASR; does not guarantee speaker separation, sentence‑level timestamps, or SRT.
