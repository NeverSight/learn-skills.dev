Measure how many hours a person really works per day, per client or project, and at what clock times, from the digital exhaust they already leave — Claude Code transcripts, Slack, WhatsApp, meeting recordings, laptop screen time, browser history and chess games — with leisure subtracted and the idle threshold calibrated against their own stated breaks. Use it whenever someone asks "how many hours do I actually work", "how much time do I spend on client X vs Y", "what are my working hours / routine", "am I overworking", "how much of my day is chess / YouTube", or wants a timesheet reconstructed after the fact. Trigger it even when the word "hours" never appears — "how long are my days", "split my time between customers", "when do I usually start and stop" are this skill.
