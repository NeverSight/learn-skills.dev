Reverse-lookup glossary for data visualization. It turns a loose description of a chart, chart part or chart behavior into its exact term and the Bklit UI component or prop that provides it. Examples: "the line that follows my mouse" → Crosshair (`ChartTooltip showCrosshair`); "the stick with a dot on top instead of a bar" → Lollipop (`BarLollipop`); "the shaded range around the line" → Range band (`Band`); "bars on top of each other" → Stacked bar (`BarChart stacked`). Use when someone asks "what's it called when…", describes a chart without knowing its name, or wants the right words to prompt an AI with. Also use whenever a chart request, including a request to build a Bklit chart, is phrased in another library's vocabulary: Nivo (`groupMode`, `enableSlices`, `useMesh`, `offsetType`, `ResponsiveLine`, `layers`), Recharts (`stackId`, `ResponsiveContainer`, `layout="vertical"`, `barCategoryGap`, `LabelList`), TanStack Charts (`elementType`, `primaryAxis`, `lineY`, `barY`) or d3 (`d3.stack`, `scaleBand`, `curveStep`, `d3.arc`). Translate those terms to Bklit components and props first, then hand off to bklit-ui to build and bklit-ui-design to style.
