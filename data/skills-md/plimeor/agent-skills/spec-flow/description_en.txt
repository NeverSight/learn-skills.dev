Manage a repo's docs for plans, specs, structure, and decisions. Use when setting up the docs layout; planning a feature or change; reviewing a plan's skeleton or spec change files; checking finished work against its plan; archiving a shipped plan; recording, superseding, or consolidating a decision record; tidying existing specs or decisions; or building or checking docs/architecture.md against the code. Near miss: a principle-based judgment of a design outside a plan is code-design-review; a review of a diff with no plan is code-review.
