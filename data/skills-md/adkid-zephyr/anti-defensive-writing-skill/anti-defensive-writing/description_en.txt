Prevent defensive writing throughout the entire process of paper writing, revision, compression, and experiment organization. Treat the paper as an academic presentation, not as a project summary, experiment log, or self‑review report: identify the most publishable value of the work and build the most advantageous, complete, and persuasive narrative around it. Avoid uneven presentation, do not voluntarily show weakness, do not write a dry experimental log, and do not let reviewers attack you.
