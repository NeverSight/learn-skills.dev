We turn a paper (arXiv ID/URL, DOI, PDF, title) into an explanatory post in the style of Korean community info‑articles (dry tone, short breaths, no profanity). Like a storyteller we unpack it without leaps, attaching cited images of the paper’s figures, tables, and text excerpts, and we include the lineage of previous work → this paper → subsequent work along with author and institution context. All facts are verified by a fact‑checking manager. The result is extracted as part images resembling phone captures, white‑background posts, and 4:5 images for X/thread use. It is used for requests such as “paper explanation,” “paper story,” “explain in community style,” “FEMCO‑style info post,” “field lineage summary.”
