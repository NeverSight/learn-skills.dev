Turn a screen recording of a UI (page load, navigation, redirect/auth flow, app startup) into an interactive paint timeline page. You get a full-window video, a timeline of each visual paint with timestamps and durations, and on-video flashes of what was painted (pink) versus what only moved (blue). Use when the user shares a screen recording (.mp4/.mov) and asks for a timeline of paints, frames, or visual changes, a filmstrip, load-performance breakdown, "what painted when", or how long each screen was shown.
