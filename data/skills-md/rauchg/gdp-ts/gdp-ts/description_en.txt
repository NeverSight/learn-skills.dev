Authorization and other preconditions as compile-time proofs in TypeScript, with the gdp-ts library (Ghosts of Departed Proofs). Use when adding, changing or reviewing authorization, entitlement or ownership checks in a TypeScript codebase that uses gdp-ts (imports of `name`, `Named`, `Proof` or `defineProof`), when adopting the pattern in a codebase, or when fixing type errors that mention `Named<...>` or proof types.
