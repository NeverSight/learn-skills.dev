Create, read and manage KubeSense service level objectives (SLOs) over the REST API — by_count, time_slice and alert-uptime SLIs, error budgets, burn-rate alerting and grouping — and translate Datadog SLOs into equivalent KubeSense ones. Covers the exact POST /api/slo payload, the good/total query config shape it shares with alert rules, and the traps that make an SLO store cleanly and then evaluate nothing.
