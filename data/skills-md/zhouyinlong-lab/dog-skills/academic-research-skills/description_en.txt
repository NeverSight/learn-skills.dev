Academic Research Skills (ARS) — an AI assistant for the entire academic research workflow, an open‑source project with 38.7 k⭐. Four capabilities cover everything from literature investigation to paper publication: Deep Research (13 agents for literature investigation/systematic reviews), Academic Paper (12 agents for writing pipeline/LaTeX PDF output), Academic Paper Reviewer (7 agents for multi‑perspective reviewing/anti‑flattery protocol), Academic Pipeline (10‑stage orchestrator/citation verification/integrity gate). Core philosophy: AI is a co‑pilot, not the pilot—helping you search literature, manage citations, format documents, while research judgments remain yours.
