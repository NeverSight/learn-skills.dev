The general‑supply subscription points for private housing (a total of 84 points calculated from the period of being without a house, the number of dependents, and the subscription account holding period) and the deposit standards for subscription by region and exclusive floor‑area are queried using the values in Appendix 1 and Appendix 2 of the “Regulations on Housing Supply.” These are used for questions about subscription points, subscription deposits, private housing, period without a house, and dependents.
