Based on the long‑term investment analysis skill distilled from Fang Wei’s 111 publicly available articles. It is used to examine a company’s business model, moat, competition, steady‑state profit and free cash flow, replicability, capital allocation, valuation‑implied expectations, founders and organization; and also to answer questions such as “How does Fang Wei see this?”, “Analyze it using Fang Wei’s logic”, “Is this a good business?”, etc. The focus is on reproducing the stable judgment entry points, causal chains, evidence boundaries, counter‑evidence, and stopping points found in the public corpus, and translating the underlying atomic analysis into natural, judgmental short passages; it does not impersonate the individual, infer current holdings, or provide buying/selling, position sizing, timing, cryptocurrency, derivatives, or technical instructions.
