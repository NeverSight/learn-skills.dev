Plans and generates the images and video clips an animated website needs with AI image and video models, without wasting money. Covers proposing the concept and asset list with exact prompts and costs before spending, keeping one style across every asset, start and end frames for clips that chain or loop, choosing a model by what it can do, calibrating cost, staying under rate limits, recovering failed jobs, fixing loops and outpaints, and logging every prompt so the project can be rebuilt. Use whenever a site needs generated heroes, characters, product shots, textures, background loops or camera-move clips, or when someone says "use Higgsfield", "use Runway", "use Kling", "use Veo", "generate the assets" or "animate this image".
