Builds real-time 3D websites in three.js from code alone, with no model files. Covers buildings and landmarks, vehicles, machines, landscapes and product studies made of procedural geometry (profile sweeps, lathes, instancing), canvas textures, shader patches, a physically based sky and sun, soft shadows, ambient occlusion and bloom, light presets, camera vantages and flights, quality tiers, and a starter that runs at 60 fps. Use when someone asks for a 3D website, a three.js scene, a WebGL hero, an interactive 3D model of a real place or object, a "3D version" of something, or when an existing three.js page looks flat, plasticky, aliased, washed out or slow.
