Verifies a 3D or animated web page in real headless browsers before anyone else sees it. Shoots every view, light state and scroll position at desktop, tablet and phone widths in Chromium and WebKit (Safari), measures frame time, draw calls and triangles, catches page errors, failed requests, blank WebGL canvases, horizontal overflow and reduced-motion behaviour, and builds one contact sheet. Use after building or changing any three.js, WebGL, canvas, scroll-driven or animated page, before publishing or recording it, or when someone asks "does it work", "check it on mobile", "why is it slow" or "test it in Safari".
