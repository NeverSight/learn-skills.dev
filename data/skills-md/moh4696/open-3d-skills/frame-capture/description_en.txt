Records any web page (three.js, WebGL, canvas, GSAP, CSS animation, scroll-driven video) to a perfectly smooth 60 fps MP4 by running the page on a virtual clock, one exact frame at a time, and puts two recordings against each other as a labelled stacked or side-by-side comparison. Use when someone wants a video of a site, a demo clip for X or YouTube, a scroll-through, a showcase reel of a 3D scene, or a "model A vs model B" comparison video. Also use when a screen recording of an animated page stutters or drops frames.
