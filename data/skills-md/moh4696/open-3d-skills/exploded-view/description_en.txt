Builds interactive 3D explainers of how a machine or product works, in three.js, with a staged exploded view driven by a slider or scroll, x-ray shells, a section cut through the casing, labels pinned to parts with leader lines that never cover the model, and hover or click to pick parts. Works on code-built models and loaded GLB files. Use for "show how it works", teardown or cutaway pages, product anatomy, engine, motor, gearbox, camera, phone or vehicle internals, technical education pages, or turning a 2D cutaway drawing into an interactive 3D one.
