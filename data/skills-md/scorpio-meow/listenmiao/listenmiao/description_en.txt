Use this skill when the user wants a transcript, subtitles or meeting minutes from an audio or video file and has ListenMiao installed or running, the local transcription app for Taiwanese Mandarin, Mandarin–English code-switching and Taiwanese Hokkien built on MediaTek Research's Breeze ASR 25/26 models. It covers transcribing with speaker labels and hotwords, exporting SRT/VTT/ASS/TXT/Markdown/DOCX/JSON, burning subtitles into a video, writing minutes from a transcript, editing the glossary and voice library, and starting or troubleshooting the service. Use it even when the user never says "ListenMiao" or "Breeze", for requests like "轉逐字稿", "聽打這段錄音", "幫這支影片上字幕", "把會議錄音整理成會議紀錄", "台語錄音轉文字", "transcribe this interview" or "make an SRT for this lecture", and whenever a recording has to stay on the user's machine. Not for cloud speech APIs or for training ASR models.
