# Weekly Review

Ten minutes that make the next week calmer. Reflection, then a short plan.

## Look back (read-only)

- **Calendar** — what actually happened this week; meetings that ate the day.
- **Tasks** — what got done, what's still open, what's been open too long.
- **Notes** — anything captured this week that needs a next step.

## Reflect

Answer three questions, briefly and honestly:

1. What moved forward this week that actually mattered?
2. What slipped — and is it dead, or just delayed?
3. What kept interrupting the important work?

## Plan next week

Pick **three** priorities. Not ten. If everything's a priority, nothing is.
For each, name the first concrete action. Then glance at next week's calendar and
say where those three will realistically fit — or warn that they won't.

## Deliver

```
THIS WEEK
  Shipped: the newsletter, the client proposal
  Slipped: the tax return (delayed, not dead) — do it Monday
  Drag:    back-to-back meetings Tue/Wed killed deep work

NEXT WEEK — top 3
  1. Finish the tax return        → first action: gather receipts
  2. Record the tutorial          → needs a clear morning; Thursday's open
  3. Reply to the three warm leads → 30 min, Monday

Heads-up: Wednesday's fully booked. Don't plan real work there.
```

## Rules

- Be honest, not harsh. Name what slipped without a lecture.
- Three priorities, maximum. Push back if the user tries to add a fourth.
- Don't reschedule the calendar or close tasks yourself — propose, the user
  decides.
