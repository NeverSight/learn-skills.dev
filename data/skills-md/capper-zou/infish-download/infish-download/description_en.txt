Universal online video and music download, media extraction skill. When a user expresses a download intent such as “download this” or “save this” and includes an HTTPS URL, this skill detects and downloads even if they don’t mention “video” or “music.” It fully integrates adapters for WeChat Channels and Spotify single tracks, and supports yt‑dlp extractors for YouTube, Bilibili, X/Twitter, Douyin/TikTok, Xiaohongshu, Instagram, Facebook, Vimeo, Twitch, Reddit, Weibo, AcFun, etc.; it also handles MP3, subtitles, media information, and yt‑dlp updates. It will not automatically download when a URL is pasted alone without a download intent. It never uses UI automation to operate WeChat, Xiaohongshu, or risk‑control verification pages.
