Also for writing or fixing 3D TSX (Camera3d, Mesh3d, lights, materials, glTF models, post effects, an ECS world), a 3D render or play that looks wrong, extending raea's Bevy SDK, rendered video, captions and motion graphics from code, and frames rendered headlessly.
