Tracing theoretical origins and reinterpreting cases. Review empirical judgments in PPTs, courses, copy, or methodologies; first determine whether the proposition holds, then match and verify credible theories, using those theories to reinterpret cases and delineate boundaries. This is suitable for finding support for self‑created theories, adding theoretical sources to PPTs, judging whether a statement can be elevated to a theory, and checking for forced appropriation of famous figures; it focuses on a single theoretical anchoring without assuming an expanded historical isomorphism study.
