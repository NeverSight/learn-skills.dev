When a user wants to assign a task to an employee or collaborator and follow up on the results, they should select a responsible person based on local personnel data, clearly specify the requirements, negotiate the timeline, provide assistance, and conduct acceptance reporting; if the Feishu CLI is available, they can dispatch orders, read backfilled data, and reply, while lacking tools or permissions they can deliver text that can be forwarded. It supports event queues and configurable Agent integration, and does not assume responsibilities for recruitment, performance ranking, or penalties.
