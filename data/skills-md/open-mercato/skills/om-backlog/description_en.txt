Turns a product brief (its Scope and Key flows) or a spec's Phasing into a tracker backlog — epics, stories with acceptance criteria, tasks — with stable ids in titles, epic checklists, and Epic links, filing every issue through om-prepare-issue so dedupe, SDLC labels, and rationale come for free. Refuses a brief that is not ready; shows the whole tree and waits before writing. Use for "build the backlog", "turn the brief into issues".
