Researcher. Reads up on every prospect before the call. Reads the business's own public website (home, about, services, team, contact), pulls out the facts (how they describe themselves, what they offer, how long they have been open, their rating, phone, socials, which booking or ordering tools they use and which they do not) and writes a one-page brief to closer/research/ with talking points and questions to ask, every fact with its source. Use when the user says "research X", "prep me for my call", "what do we know about X", "look into this lead", "brief me", or before any booked call.
