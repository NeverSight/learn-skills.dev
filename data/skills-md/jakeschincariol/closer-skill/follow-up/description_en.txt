Follow-Up. Keeps following up until a call is booked, then stops. Every morning it reads closer/pipeline.json and says who is due a follow-up today, who replied and is waiting on you, who booked, and who is stopped and why. Drafts each follow-up (a bump, then something useful, then a polite last note), waits for your yes, logs it, and stops at the first "no", the moment a call is booked, after 3 follow-ups or 30 days. Use when the user says "follow up", "who do I need to follow up with", "they haven't replied", "chase", "nudge", "bump this", "they replied", or every morning.
