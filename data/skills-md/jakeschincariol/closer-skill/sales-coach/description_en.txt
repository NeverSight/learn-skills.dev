Sales Coach. Listens to your sales call and tells you exactly what to say next. Reads the call's live captions (Google Meet's own captions, read off the page by Claude in Chrome, or any captions file), and the moment the other side raises an objection ("it's too expensive", "let me think about it", "I need to ask my partner", "we already have someone") it shows you, and only you, the answer to say next, filled from your real offer. Also flags buying signals and questions you still owe an answer, and reviews the call afterwards (talk time, questions asked, next step). Use when the user says "coach me on this call", "listen to my call", "I have a sales call", "what do I say if they say it's too expensive", "objection", "handle objections", or "review my call".
