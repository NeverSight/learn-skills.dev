Contract Writer. Creates the contract and sends it over for you, after your yes. Turns what was agreed on the call into a plain-English services agreement (services, fee, term, launch, payment, ownership, liability, signature lines), checks the fee against what was actually said on the call or in the proposal, writes a print-ready PDF, drafts the email to send it, and marks the deal won when the signed copy comes back. Use when the user says "write the contract", "send the agreement", "draft a services agreement", "send the paperwork", "they said yes", or "they signed".
