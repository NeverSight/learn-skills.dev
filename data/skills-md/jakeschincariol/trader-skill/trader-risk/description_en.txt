Risk Manager. Checks every proposed order against your limits before it can reach the broker and blocks anything over them: blocked tickers, max order size, minimum order, max position percent after the fill, the cash buffer, margin, short sales, options and crypto (off unless allowed), malformed orders, duplicates and a daily order limit. Prints PASS or BLOCK with the reason for each order and records the verdicts so trader-broker only shows an order that passed. Use when the user says "check my orders", "is this safe", "risk check", "does this break my rules", "can I place this", or before /trader-broker, always.
