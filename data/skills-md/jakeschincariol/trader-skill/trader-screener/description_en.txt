The Stock Screener. Checks a list of companies against the user's own rules (revenue growth, margins, EPS, P/E, earnings yield, sector, anything in the metrics file), says exactly which rule each company passes or fails and by how much, and ranks the ones that pass into a shortlist. Reads the metrics /trader-analyst writes from SEC filings, or any CSV. Use when the user says "screen for stocks", "find me companies that", "filter my watchlist", "which of these pass my rules", "shortlist candidates", "rank these stocks", or "growing companies under 30 times earnings".
