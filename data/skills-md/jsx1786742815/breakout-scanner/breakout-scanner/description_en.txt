Multi‑strategy scanning toolbox (extensible). Currently built‑in five strategies:

1. **Breakout strategy (volume‑price confirmation)** – triggered by “breakout strategy scan”, “volume breakout”, “scan breakout tickets”; filters stocks that break out with volume on platforms/boxes/previous highs.

2. **Low‑buy strategy (low‑volume pullback)** – triggered by “low‑buy strategy”, “low‑volume pullback”, “pullback low‑buy”, “strong stock pullback”; filters strong stocks that pull back with low volume to the 5‑day/10‑day moving average line.

3. **Board‑hitting / relay strategy** – triggered by “board‑hitting”, “relay”, “board‑hitting strategy”, “space board”, “leader first board”; outputs candidates for highest‑cap / leader / first‑second board (order‑book volume, turnover rate).

4. **Theme / sentiment cycle (leader strategy)** – triggered by “sentiment cycle”, “market sentiment”, “ice‑point repair fermentation peak ebb”, “leader strategy”; determines the market sentiment cycle and gives trading advice (stay flat during ebb periods).

5. **75A strategy (arc‑bottom just turning up)** – triggered by “75A strategy”, “arc bottom”, “just turning up”, “pot bottom”; screens low‑level arc‑bottom stocks that are just turning up on the right side, with a space of ≥10 % above the neckline and MA5 ≥ MA10 (capital‑chase play, single ticket ≤10 %).

6. **Gap‑fill rally strategy (capital gap + rally within main line, real‑trade validation on 2026‑08‑31 for Zhiguang Co. +8.53 %)** – triggered by “gap‑fill rally”, “capital gap”, “main‑line rally”, “buy second not first”; first identifies the day’s main line by sector‑level capital clustering (≥3 stocks in the same sector among the top net inflow), then selects within that line the smallest rise that hugs MA20 (script automatically filters out rises ≥5 % to avoid chasing highs; disabled in weak markets).

Each invocation first pulls the latest market data (Dongcai source, no caching), then filters according to the chosen strategy and outputs the target stocks plus operational notes (entry point / stop‑loss / exit). Adding a new strategy = add a rule file + scanning script.
