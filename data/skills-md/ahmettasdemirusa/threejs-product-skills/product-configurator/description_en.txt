Build a 3D product configurator with three.js, where shoppers pick colors, materials, finishes or parts and see them on the 3D model with the price updating. Use when the user wants a color picker for a 3D product, a "customize your product" page, material or finish swatches, variant selection, a furniture, cabinet, shoe or car configurator, KHR_materials_variants, or wants options stored in the URL or sent to the cart. Includes a tested starter template.
