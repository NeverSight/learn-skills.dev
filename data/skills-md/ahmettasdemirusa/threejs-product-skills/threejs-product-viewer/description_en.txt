Build an interactive 3D product viewer for a website with three.js. The viewer loads a glTF/GLB model, frames it automatically, lights it like a studio and lets shoppers orbit and zoom. Use when the user wants a 3D product view, "show our product in 3D", a GLB/glTF viewer, a three.js product page, an e-commerce 3D model, or a rotatable model of furniture, shoes, bottles, electronics, cabinets or any physical product. Includes a tested starter template. For color/material options see product-configurator; for a no-code embed with AR see model-viewer-ar.
