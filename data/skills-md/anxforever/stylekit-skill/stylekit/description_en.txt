Apply a specific, consistent visual style to frontend UI you are generating. Use when building or styling web UI (pages, components, dashboards, landing pages) and you want a named aesthetic — Glassmorphism, Neo-Brutalist, Cyberpunk, Bauhaus, Apple, Stripe, Linear, and many more — instead of generic AI defaults. StyleKit gives you design tokens, component recipes, and AI rules for each style, with themes installable through the shadcn registry.
