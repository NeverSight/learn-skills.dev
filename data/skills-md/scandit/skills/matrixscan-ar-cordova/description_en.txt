Cordova MatrixScan AR (Barcode AR, BarcodeAr) via the scandit-cordova-datacapture-* plugins — scanning multiple barcodes at once with AR highlights and annotations (info annotations, popovers, status icons) on tracked barcodes. Use for integration, symbology configuration, highlight and annotation providers, BarcodeArView customization, migration from BarcodeBatch/BarcodeTracking, or troubleshooting.
