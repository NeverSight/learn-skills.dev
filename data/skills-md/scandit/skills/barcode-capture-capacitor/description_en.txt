Capacitor — Scandit Barcode Capture (`BarcodeCapture`) in Capacitor (Ionic) hybrid apps via the Scandit Capacitor plugins (`ScanditCaptureCorePlugin`), the low-level, full-control single-barcode scanning mode (BarcodeCapture + DataCaptureView + BarcodeCaptureOverlay) without the pre-built SparkScan UI, not the browser-only web SDK. Use for integration, symbology settings, result handling, viewfinder and feedback customization, SDK version migration, or troubleshooting.
