Capacitor MatrixScan AR (Barcode AR, BarcodeAr) — scanning multiple barcodes at once with AR highlights and annotations, BarcodeArView attached to a DOM element, in Capacitor iOS/Android apps (not the plain-web sibling). Use for integration, symbology configuration, highlight and annotation providers, session handling, migration from BarcodeBatch/BarcodeTracking, or troubleshooting.
