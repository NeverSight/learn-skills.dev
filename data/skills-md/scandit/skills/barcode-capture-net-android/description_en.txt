Scandit BarcodeCapture in .NET for Android projects (`net*-android` target framework, `Scandit.DataCapture.Barcode` NuGet, non-MAUI — for MAUI apps use barcode-capture-net-maui) — the low-level, full-control barcode scanning mode without the pre-built SparkScan UI. Use for integration, scan settings, listener and event wiring, overlay customization, camera lifecycle, SDK version migration (v6→v7→v8), replacing ZXing.Net or ML Kit bindings, or troubleshooting.
