Scandit Barcode Capture (`BarcodeCapture`) in Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP) and Compose Multiplatform projects using Scandit's KMP SDK (`com.kmp.datacapture.*` imports) — the low-level, full-control single-barcode scanning mode without the pre-built SparkScan UI. Use for integration, scan settings, shared-code result handling, overlay and viewfinder customization, or troubleshooting.
