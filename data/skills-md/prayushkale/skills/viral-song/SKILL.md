# Viral song

## Overview
A viral song is **built for a use**: people dance to it, edit to it, or lip-sync one line of it.
Pick the use first, then write a 10–20 s **clip unit** that does that use, then build the song
around the clip. The numbers come from 340 hits (2010+); they are what hits *share*, not a
formula that makes a hit. Say so to the user.

## Workflow
1. **Pick a lane** from the brief (details in `references/lanes.md`):
   - **A. Dance challenge**, the default and first choice: launches, debuts, group acts, party themes.
   - **B. Edit sound**: moody, cinematic, transition/montage use.
   - **C. Lip-sync line**: one confessional or quotable line; the least certain lane.
   - Don't aim for "streaming megahit" (needs label/radio muscle) or "meme sound" (someone else
     invents the meme). If the brief asks for those, choose A, B or C and say why.
2. **Write the clip unit before anything else.** Name the *use* in one sentence (the move,
   the cut, the line). Write the hook: 2 short lines, the **title in line 1**, plus **one
   repeated chant word or syllable** a viewer can do the move on. It must make sense with
   nothing before it.
3. **Place the clip early.** It goes in the first 15 s *and* at the first chorus. Don't open
   with a verse. For songs **under 90 s**, the order is: hook/chant 0–8 s → short verse → chorus
   by about 25 s → post-chorus chant → end on the hook so the file loops. No bridge, no fade, no
   long intro.
4. **Fill the base spec** (lane values override): sung melodic hook, steady high energy (or a
   build into a drop for lane B), 100–128 BPM written as a number, pop or dance-pop, one clear
   lead voice or unison group, clean lyrics, a love/party/confidence theme. Keep the brief's
   language; if it isn't English, put the title and chant in words anyone can sing.
5. **Translate to the generator** with `references/generators.md`. Never name real artists or
   imitate a real voice.
6. **Run `references/checklist.md`.** Fix every "no" before you spend credits.
7. **Generate, then deliver**: the audio, the exact prompt, the lane, the clip-unit timestamps,
   and a one-line "how to use it on day one" (the dance, the edit, the line). For lane B, also
   make sped-up (about 1.25×) and slowed (about 0.85×) versions.

## Common mistakes
| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Verse → pre-chorus → chorus in a 60 s song (hook at about 20 s) | Hook first; the chorus comes back by about 25 s |
| 15+ genre tags of "flavour" | 6–10 tags: genre, BPM, vocal, energy, 2–3 signature sounds, "catchy chant hook" |
| A clever hook nobody can do anything with | Name the use; the chant word is where the move lands |
| Ending on a fragment or a fade | End on the full hook, on the beat, so it loops |
| Promising virality | "This matches what hits share; release and measure it" |
