Reconciles leadership's top-down target against the driver-based bottom-up build line by line via a bundled calculator, flagging every gap over 10% instead of quietly averaging the two into a number nobody actually believes. Use whenever the user is building the annual operating plan, needs to reconcile a leadership target with a bottoms-up model, or has a plan where the top-down ask and the bottom-up build were never actually compared line by line.
