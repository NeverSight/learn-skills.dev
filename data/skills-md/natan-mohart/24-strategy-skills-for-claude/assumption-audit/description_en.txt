Surfaces every unstated belief a strategy or business case depends on, grades the evidence behind each one, and stress-tests the ones that would break the plan if wrong — using a load-bearing-assumption framework, not a generic risk brainstorm. Use before any strategy, business case, or major decision is finalized or presented, whenever the user asks "what could go wrong," "are we sure about this," or wants a pre-mortem, or when a plan has gone unchallenged for a while.
