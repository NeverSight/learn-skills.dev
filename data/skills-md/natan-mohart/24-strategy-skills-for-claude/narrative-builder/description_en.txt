Structures a strategy or recommendation into the Pyramid Principle (answer-first, MECE-supported) and a Situation-Complication-Resolution arc, so a senior audience gets the conclusion before the reasoning instead of a chronological walk through the analysis. Use whenever the user needs to turn analysis into an executive-ready narrative, deck, or presentation, or has a draft that walks through the work chronologically ("first we looked at X, then we looked at Y") instead of leading with the answer.
