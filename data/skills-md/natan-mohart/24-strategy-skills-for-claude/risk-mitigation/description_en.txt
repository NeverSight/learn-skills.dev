Scores strategic and execution risks on likelihood, impact, AND velocity (how fast a risk could hit) via a bundled calculator — catching fast-moving risks a standard 2-factor heat map underranks — then forces a named owner and mitigation for every high-priority item. Use whenever the user wants a risk register, risk assessment, or mitigation plan for a strategy or initiative, or has a risk list with no prioritization, owners, or mitigation actions attached.
