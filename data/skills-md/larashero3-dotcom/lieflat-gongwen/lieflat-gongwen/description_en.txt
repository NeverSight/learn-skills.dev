A document‑writing skill based on comprehensive statistics of real official documents. It is divided into two families—the official‑document family and the party‑building family—and covers six types of document styles: research reports, leader speeches, work opinions, experience materials, work plans, and experience summaries. In addition, it includes a large‑scale (thousands of) category of party‑building and grassroots experience materials. It provides verifiable numerical parameters, scaffolding formulas, title techniques, and automated self‑check scripts. Trigger scenarios: “Write a research report,” “Draft a leader’s speech,” “Write a work opinion,” “Write a party‑building experience material,” “Turn the material into an experience summary,” “Make this manuscript sound more like an official document,” “Check whether the parameters of this document are correct.”
