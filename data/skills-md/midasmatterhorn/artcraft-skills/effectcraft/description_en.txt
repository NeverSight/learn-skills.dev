EffectCraft is an open-source, pure-Rust motion-graphics, compositing and VFX app for macOS, Windows, Linux and the web — a clean-room, After Effects-shaped studio with compositions, layers, keyframes, 308 effects, expressions, Classic/Advanced 3D, tracking, roto, After Effects project interchange (`.aep`/`.aepx` in, `.aep` out — a verified subset that reports what it cannot carry), a render queue, Lottie, replayable repro scripts, and a plug-in surface (WebAssembly ABI 1/2 effect plug-ins, the Plug-in Library, out-of-process external helpers), driven by a CLI, an MCP server, a localhost JSON control channel and JavaScript scripting. Trigger this skill whenever the user mentions EffectCraft, `.ecproj`/`.ecprojx`/`.ectemplate`/ `.ecpreset`, `effectcraft-cli`, its MCP tools or control channel; asks what it can or cannot do, which formats or shortcuts it has, or how to script, batch, render, verify, replay, repair or troubleshoot it; or describes the job without naming the app ("make a title card", "animate this logo", "key this green screen", "render a ProRes", "export Lottie", "open this .aep"). Also use it when choosing between EffectCraft and FilmCraft, or preparing and verifying assets for it. `.aep` interchange is a documented subset, not parity: expect a report of what did not come across, and never promise an After Effects-identical render.
