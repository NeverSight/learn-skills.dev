MailCraft is an open-source, clean-room Outlook-shaped mail, calendar, contacts and tasks program written in Rust (public beta, v0.13.0): IMAP/SMTP accounts with IDLE new-mail push, Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com through Graph with OAuth, Exchange Web Services accounts (Exchange 2010 and newer), JMAP in the browser build (read, and since v0.13.0 send, flag, move and delete), a native .mailcraft mailbox, import/export of .eml, .mbox, .ics and .vcf, Outlook .pst import, and an automation surface of 343 engine commands, 89 window commands, a 20-tool MCP server, a loopback JSON control channel with 20 method names and a 14-subcommand CLI (mailcraft-cli). Trigger this skill whenever the user mentions MailCraft, a .mailcraft mailbox, mailcraft-cli, MailCraft's MCP tools, its control channel or its control-key file, or asks to read, search, file, flag, snooze or send mail, work a calendar, contacts or to-dos from a terminal or an agent, import an Outlook .pst archive or .mbox/.eml mail, drive the running app over localhost, or asks what MailCraft can or cannot do, which formats it reads, how to script, automate, verify, repair or troubleshoot it — even if the app is not named but the job is desktop mail-client work ("organise this mailbox", "import my Outlook archive", "check my calendar", "send this from a script"). Not for Microsoft Outlook itself, not for mail-server administration, and not installed by the ArtCraft launcher on macOS.
