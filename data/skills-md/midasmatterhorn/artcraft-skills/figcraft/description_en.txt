FigCraft is an open-source, pure-Rust, offline interface-design, prototyping and whiteboard app (a clean-room reimplementation of the Figma Design workflow): frames, auto layout, components, styles, variables, vector networks, prototyping, Dev Mode and board files, on macOS, Windows, Linux, FreeBSD and the web. Trigger it whenever the user mentions FigCraft or a `.figcraft` file, asks what it can or cannot do, which formats it opens or writes, how to script, automate, batch or headlessly drive it (figcraft-cli, MCP, the JSON control channel), wants assets prepared for a design file, needs output verified or repaired, or asks to troubleshoot it — and when the app is not named but the job is one of its jobs: "make a mockup", "design a screen / landing page / dashboard", "build a design system", "export these frames to PNG/PDF/SVG", "open this .fig or Figma file JSON", "convert this Sketch file", "prototype this flow", "hand this off to a developer", "make a retro board". Do not guess capabilities from Figma knowledge — FigCraft is not Figma and runs no Figma plugins.
