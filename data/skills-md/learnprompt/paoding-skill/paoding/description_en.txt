Paoding — evidence‑driven blogger strategy distillation. It takes any blogger’s (including the user’s own) hit strategy, deconstructs it from real samples into a four‑layer structure (topic selection/structure/expression/cognition), and finally distills it into a screenshot‑able “Strategy Sheet” and an installable content‑coach skill.

Defaults to zero API, zero key: users can feed pasted text / exported files / screenshots / links, or use yt‑dlp + local Whisper to fetch a public piece of content. When the user explicitly requests it or a locally configured key is available, it can enter “blade” mode: using TikHub to collect public metadata, text, comments, and video streams from Xiaohongshu/Douyin, then transcribe with local Whisper; TikHub imposes no hard caps on manual requests or fees, but retains balance checks, platform rate limits, limited retries, checkpointing, and sample‑quality gates.

Trigger phrases include but are not limited to: “deconstruct this blogger”, “distill blogger XX”, “analyze why he went viral”, “load this blogger’s strategy into AI”, “learn from benchmark bloggers”, “help me see why my own content isn’t viral”, “content coach”, “viral strategy”, “deconstruct an account”, “break down this video account / spoken‑word blogger”, “the blogger has new content, help me update the strategy sheet (re‑cook)”. Even if the user just drops a bunch of screenshots or links of a blogger’s notes and says “help me see how he writes”, it should still trigger.

Do not use for: crawling content that requires login to view, bypassing platform risk controls, bulk data collection for resale; do not use to generate content impersonating the blogger themselves (the distillation is about the strategy, not the identity); ordinary rewriting or polishing of a single article (that does not require deconstruction).
