Use before implementing UI/design from an artifact supplied as a desired visual reference, such as a screenshot, CodePen, Dribbble shot, website, or “make it feel like this” request. Screenshots used only to point out a defect stay in the normal review path. Prevents cargo-culting mechanics, fake UI chrome, and code-first misses by forcing reference breakdown, user-question calibration, visual target approval, and side-by-side review before implementation.
