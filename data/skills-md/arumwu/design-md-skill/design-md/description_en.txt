Reference library of 74 real-world brand design systems (Claude, Linear, Stripe, Vercel, Figma, Apple, Tesla, Spotify, Notion, Supabase, and more) captured as DESIGN.md files — colors, typography, components, layout, elevation. Use when the user wants UI, a landing page, or a component that matches a specific brand's look; wants to pick a design direction from real design systems; says "like <brand>", "make it feel like Linear / Stripe / Apple"; or needs a concrete design-token starting point before frontend work. Pairs with frontend-design and tailwind-design-system.
