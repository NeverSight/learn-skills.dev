Deploy Google Stitch screens to a CanAI WordPress site and bring the copy up to CanAI conventions, through the CanAI MCP server and the CanAI Stitch add-on (wpcanai-stitch-* tools). Use when the user wants to deploy, import, publish or update a Stitch design, screen or project on WordPress, re-deploy after the design changed, or clean a deployed screen up (menus instead of hard-coded nav, t() strings, header/footer templates). Companion to canai-mcp.
