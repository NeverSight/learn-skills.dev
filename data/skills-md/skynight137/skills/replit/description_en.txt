Replit sandbox toolkit: platform facts ($HOME wiped on recreate, REPL_HOME/XDG/env channels, rc & git persistence), pulling libs from /nix/store (.replit [nix] packages vs replit.nix vs nix-env, transitive LD_LIBRARY_PATH closure), Playwright on Replit's bundled Chromium (no browser download), and the Camoufox anti-detection Firefox server (Cloudflare/Turnstile/WAF sites, cookie persistence, session keep-alive). Use for anything running ON a Replit/Nix workspace.
