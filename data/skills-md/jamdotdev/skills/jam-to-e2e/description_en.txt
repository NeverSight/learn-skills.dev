Turn a Jam (a jam.dev screen recording with the console, network and user actions attached) into steps and an end-to-end test in the project's own runner and language, with first-class guidance for Playwright and tester-army/e2e, grounded in the current code. A Jam of a flow or demo becomes a test that guards it; a Jam of a bug becomes a failing test the fix turns green. Use when the user pastes a jam.dev link and asks for an e2e test, a regression test, a repro, or to "write a test for this".
