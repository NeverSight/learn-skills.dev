Top-level orchestrator that turns a one-line video idea into a finished short (story short, product spot, music-video snippet) on ComfyUI (local, Google Colab or Comfy Cloud) using only open-weights models. It does brief intake with defaults, a preflight and budget check that picks a draft, standard or max tier, then story bible and timed shot plan, stills first with a QA judge loop, image-to-video, continuity checks, voiceover, music and SFX, assembly with captions and exports, and a self-critique report. It keeps one resumable JSON manifest of every prompt, seed, model and cost. Use it when the user asks to "make a video", a short film, an ad or a music video, end to end.
