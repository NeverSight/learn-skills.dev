Turns Paper Shaders (30 WebGL shaders: mesh and grain gradients, god rays, liquid metal, smoke ring, dithering, halftone, paper texture and more) into frame-exact backgrounds, textures and logo stings for HyperFrames or Remotion videos, in brand colors, with nine tested presets and 11 researched looks (liquid metal field, god rays, mesh and grain gradients, dithered gradient, marbled ink, paper, contour lines, hand-drawn burst, gradient flow, datamosh rain) with numbers from Inigo Quilez, Paper's designers and Lucas Pope. Every frame is drawn from timeline time, so renders are seekable, parallel and repeatable, and spend no varg credits. Use when a video needs an animated shader background, a grain, dither, halftone or paper look on graphics or stills, or a logo reveal or sting. NOT for generating footage (use varg-ai), grading or film grain on real footage (HyperFrames media treatments), warping existing layers (HyperFrames data-vfx-chain), 3D scenes (a Three.js skill) or shaders for websites.
