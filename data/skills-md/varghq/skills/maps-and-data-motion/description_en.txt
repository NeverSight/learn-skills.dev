Eight map and data animations with exact presets: globe-to-country dive, satellite flyover between two places, vector route map, watercolor travel route, self-drawing bar and line chart, count-up statistic, versus timeline (two numbers diverging over the years) and an audio visualizer (silk ribbons, wireframe terrain, ASCII stream). Presets give camera paths on the van Wijk zoom-pan curve, flight durations, line widths, chart rules (bars from zero, one accent series, direct labels), number formats and count-up easing, and FFT sizes, log bands and attack/release for audio, from Heer & Robertson's animation study, MapLibre and Mapbox, Datawrapper, John Burn-Murdoch (FT), the W3C Web Audio spec and MilkDrop. Map credits and tile licences are part of every preset. Use when a video needs an animated travel map, a route between cities, a flyover, an animated chart or statistic, or a music visualizer. NOT for interactive web maps or dashboards.
