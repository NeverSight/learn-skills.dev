Use when a seller says "clone this winning ad", "what are my competitors running", "find winning ads for my product", "spy on TikTok Shop ads", "give me new angles", or pastes Ad Library or Creative Center links. Not for re-uploading or re-cutting someone else's footage (never), and not for one ad's 20 variants (use varg-ad-variants).
