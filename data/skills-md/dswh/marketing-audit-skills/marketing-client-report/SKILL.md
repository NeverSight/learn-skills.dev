# Marketing client report

## Inputs

The actual reports, sources, decisions and outstanding questions. Supply actual file contents, accessible files or pasted material. Relevant prior reports can be replaced by equivalent raw context. Keep supplied facts, historical results and authorized planning assumptions separate.


Read the actual section reports and their sources. A list of filenames without accessible contents is not the research itself.
Combine discovery notes, the working marketing brief, customer research, competitor research, customer journey audit, selected playbook and action plan. Preserve their evidence, unknowns and proposed status.
Open with the recommendation, why it fits, the first action and decisions needed. Put detailed evidence after the decision summary.
Read [the blueprint standard](references/blueprint-standard.md) before assembling the report. Use [the visual style](assets/blueprint.css) or an equally readable equivalent. The first screen shows Approve, Hold, amount at risk, proposed accountable owner and the evidence that releases the next decision. Do not lead with hypothetical revenue.
Synthesize the working reports into a compact founder view rather than concatenating eight reports. Use an actual-versus-modeled funnel, contribution economics, one annotated handoff and evidence-gated actions. Preserve the full working outputs and original captures in a linked appendix.
Check that the offer, buyer, primary paid outcome, earlier conversion steps, chosen channel, budget, first test and measurement agree across sections. Verify funnel arithmetic and denominators; label order value separately from cash collected and profit. Surface contradictions and missing material rather than inventing a bridge.
Add an approval list for scope, policy/copy, owner time, access, budget and next review. Keep service scope distinct from promised marketing results.
Use readable responsive HTML, short bullets, labeled diagrams and compact comparisons. Make any references available in a small source section. Never embed private customer data in a public report.
Preserve the audit's annotated screenshots, numbered findings, source metadata and original/annotation toggle. Embed required images or package their actual files; confirm that the final report opens without broken visual references. A proposed redesign must remain separate from observed evidence.

## Outputs

Create Marketing-Strategy-and-Action-Plan.html and Client-Decision-Summary.html. The summary becomes section client-decisions in the master. Preserve each stage's evidence and working output in its section or a clearly linked appendix. Reorganize repeated narrative into the founder view without discarding findings.

## Working rules

Use the materials the user supplies. Read .agents/marketing-business-context.md and the actual existing reports when available. A filename alone is not the report contents.
Ask once for missing information that could change the result. The user may proceed with unknowns.
Research permitted public pages when browsing is available. Record the URL and date checked. A supplied URL is not evidence that it was opened. If access fails, ask for page text or record a research task.
Keep observed facts, customer statements, provider claims, assumptions and proposals distinct. Preserve product labels and sample limits. Do not invent historical metrics, customer quotes, people, sources or actual performance. When the user authorizes planning assumptions, label each as an assumption, show its source and calculations, and keep it separate from actuals. Never call an assumed rate a market benchmark.
Use plain words, short bullets, a useful diagram or compact comparison, and a small source list. No decorative scores or unsupported earnings claims.
Make the output a decision blueprint, not an essay. Give each section one useful visual artifact and one decision. Use short labels and content-fit cards. Put detailed evidence behind the main reading view. Never replace missing actuals with modeled numbers or use an invented score to make the report look complete.
When evidence cannot locate the constraint, recommend the smallest investigation that can. A supplied budget or an attractive screenshot does not justify a paid pilot. Name the proposed owner, missing evidence and release condition instead.
Create local draft files only. Sharing, publishing, account access, messages and spending require their own authorization.

## Report continuity

Keep each working output understandable with its own context. Working notes are not automatically separate client deliverables or paid services. Add or update its matching section in Marketing-Strategy-and-Action-Plan.html while preserving all other sections and sources. Start the master if absent. Missing sections remain missing, not invented.
If files cannot be written, return the report content and intended filename. Mark owners, targets, budgets and copy as proposed until agreed.
