# Marketing playbook selection

## Inputs

Business, customer and competitor research, audit, budget and capacity. Supply actual file contents, accessible files or pasted material. Relevant prior reports can be replaced by equivalent raw context. Keep supplied facts, historical results and authorized planning assumptions separate.


Name the primary conversion and a quality or retained-value measure.
Identify the constraint supported by evidence: reaching the audience, message and proof, conversion, follow-up, retention, or missing measurement.
Choose the intervention from reconciled cohorts, not from the supplied budget or a preferred channel. Low attendance, weak offer exposure and low buying after exposure require different investigations. If the constraint is unknown, the first playbook is diagnosis; do not force an ad, creator or channel test.
Compare plausible routes using buyer behavior, audience access, current assets, time to useful evidence, cash, margin or lead value, team capacity and permissions. Explain tradeoffs without made-up scores.
Recommend the next decision and state what would change it. If evidence cannot distinguish channels, give a specific investigation rather than an arbitrary channel choice. For a new offer without historical cohorts, a small learning test can be appropriate when its question, downside cap and approval are explicit.
Draw channel -> message -> asset -> customer action -> quality check. Mark this as a proposed approach, not observed results.
List the research tasks, minimum tools, access needs, costs to confirm and first useful asset. Include a manual public-research route when paid tools are optional.
For creator partnerships, verify a small shortlist from actual profiles: URL, relevant content, audience-fit evidence, date checked and unresolved rate/media-kit questions. If access is missing, provide a research queue. Include a brief, content rights, disclosure check, tracked destination and qualified outcome. Do not invent creators or demographics.
For ads, agree budget and measurement before spending. For email, establish a permitted audience. For search, distinguish a production plan from ranking promises. For local services, check geography and booking capacity. For software, distinguish demos from activation and payment.
Define one test: audience, change, event, matching denominator/window, quality check, review condition and decision owner. Missing baseline and sample-size inputs stay pending.
For acquisition tests, a treatment can change who clicks. Do not rank ads on buyers per visit alone. Compare mature unique buyer volume, cost and contribution at comparable spend, using randomized assignment where feasible. Retain visit conversion as a diagnostic. Separate the better of two creatives from evidence of incremental advertising value, which needs an appropriate holdout.

## Output

Create Marketing-Playbook.html and update section marketing-playbook.

## Working rules

Use the materials the user supplies. Read .agents/marketing-business-context.md and the actual existing reports when available. A filename alone is not the report contents.
Ask once for missing information that could change the result. The user may proceed with unknowns.
Research permitted public pages when browsing is available. Record the URL and date checked. A supplied URL is not evidence that it was opened. If access fails, ask for page text or record a research task.
Keep observed facts, customer statements, provider claims, assumptions and proposals distinct. Preserve product labels and sample limits. Do not invent historical metrics, customer quotes, people, sources or actual performance. When the user authorizes planning assumptions, label each as an assumption, show its source and calculations, and keep it separate from actuals. Never call an assumed rate a market benchmark.
Use plain words, short bullets, a useful diagram or compact comparison, and a small source list. No decorative scores or unsupported earnings claims.
Make the output a decision blueprint, not an essay. Give each section one useful visual artifact and one decision. Use short labels and content-fit cards. Put detailed evidence behind the main reading view. Never replace missing actuals with modeled numbers or use an invented score to make the report look complete.
When evidence cannot locate the constraint, recommend the smallest investigation that can. A supplied budget or an attractive screenshot does not justify a paid pilot. Name the proposed owner, missing evidence and release condition instead.
Create local draft files only. Sharing, publishing, account access, messages and spending require their own authorization.

## Report continuity

Keep each working output understandable with its own context. Working notes are not automatically separate client deliverables or paid services. Add or update its matching section in Marketing-Strategy-and-Action-Plan.html while preserving all other sections and sources. Start the master if absent. Missing sections remain missing, not invented.
If files cannot be written, return the report content and intended filename. Mark owners, targets, budgets and copy as proposed until agreed.
