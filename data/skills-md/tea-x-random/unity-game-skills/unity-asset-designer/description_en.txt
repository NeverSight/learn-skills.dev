Create the reference artifacts that keep Unity game assets on-model before production generation. Use for recurring characters/mascots, model turnarounds, canon sheets, icon/item/prop families, style guides, art bibles, palette or silhouette drift, mismatched character tokens/app icons, or making assets look like one world. Defines palette, shape language, shading, lighting and anti-style; produces multi-view/expression/color-callout sheets and coherent family sheets; conditions downstream image/3D generation on those references; and runs silhouette, at-size, palette, line-weight and shading consistency QA. Feeds unity-image-generator, unity-pixel-art, unity-3d-generator, unity-ui-designer and the art-spec.
