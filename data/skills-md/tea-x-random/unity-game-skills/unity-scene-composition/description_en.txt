Define and enforce Unity scene composition before asset generation or assembly. Use for composition.yaml, camera projection/yaw/pitch/lens/framing, visual layers, focal path, negative space, big-medium-small rhythm, prop density/distribution, color zones, occlusion budgets, contact shadows, BeautyCell/golden screens, registry asset placement, or scenes that are mechanically correct but visually weak. Covers sprite-on-3D/2.5D pixel density, Y-axis billboarding, feet pivots, blob shadows and quarter-view framing; diagnoses pasted-on, floating or mismatched-chunkiness sprites; and runs deterministic scene measurements plus visual critique. Pair with unity-game-layout, unity-asset-pipeline, unity-art-direction, unity-graphics and unity-ui-designer.
