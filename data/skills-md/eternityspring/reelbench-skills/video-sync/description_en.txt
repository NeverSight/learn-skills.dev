Combine the clip‑analysis data with the original footage into a **viewable video**: one side shows the picture, the other side shows the storyboard information for that shot (shot number, start/end, duration, shot type, category, camera movement, visual description, dialogue), and **when the shot cuts, the information cuts together**; the shot list automatically scrolls and highlights the current shot. The layout depends only on the aspect ratio of the original footage: **landscape / square → picture on top, information below; portrait → picture on the left, information on the right**, and the picture is always scaled as‑is, without cropping or stretching. The shot list **scrolls with playback**: at each cut point it scrolls a short distance to bring the current shot to an anchor point, then stops, and the highlight bar slides synchronously past it. The implementation captures only three images (background + two unfolded long images); scrolling and highlighting are performed by ffmpeg using time‑based cropping, **the layout is entirely in scripts/panel.css, change it to modify the layout** without touching any scripts. It consumes the shots.json produced by video‑shots (if there are frames/, use keyframes as thumbnails). Zero npm dependencies, using ffmpeg for composition and a headless browser to render the panel. Use when asked to export video, compose video, storyboard video, video with shot information, narrated version, video with shot info, annotated shot video.
