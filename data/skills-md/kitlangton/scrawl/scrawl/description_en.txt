Read and dig into Scrawl screen captures, where someone recorded their screen while talking, clicking and drawing red marks on it. Use whenever a message contains a Scrawl capture (a line like `[scrawl 0:12 · App “Window” · /path/to/….scrawl]`, a `.scrawl` bundle path, or a contact-sheet.png from one), when the user says "look at my scrawl" or refers to something they just recorded or drew, or when you need the `scrawl` CLI to zoom into a moment, pull frames, send a capture to a session, or export one as video.
