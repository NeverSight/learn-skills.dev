# Risk Reversal

## The Big Idea

Buyers don't fear the price.
They fear paying and getting nothing.

Everyone says "trust me".
Almost nobody says "pay only if it works".
The second one wins, because you can't fake it cheaply.

## The Moves

1. **Pay after result beats any guarantee.**
   Money-back is words. Pay-later is structure.
   "Nothing upfront. You see it done, then you pay."

2. **Say it out loud.**
   "You'd judge me by results anyway. I just put it in writing."

3. **One free demo at your cost.**
   "First one is free. If it works, you pay for that one plus the rest."
   Nothing converts a skeptic faster.

4. **Small steps for big doubt.**
   "Batches of five. Each batch paid after you check it."
   Trust grows in checked steps.

5. **Even a NO leaves them ahead.**
   Give something they keep: an audit, a checklist, real info.
   "Wasted my time" becomes "still got value".

6. **Only promise what you can carry.**
   A risk reversal you can't honor is failure number one.

## When It Backfires

- Fine print. Conditions kill the effect. Keep it short enough to screenshot.
- Using it on buyers who already said yes. Save it for the scared ones.

## One-Line Memory

Take their risk, and the deal closes itself.
