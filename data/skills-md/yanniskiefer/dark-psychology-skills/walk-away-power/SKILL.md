# Walk-Away Power

## The Big Idea

Being able to leave beats any argument.

Buyers smell a bluff. Real walk power comes from having other deals.

Never walk as punishment. Insulted people take revenge.

## The Moves

1. **Check before you leave.**
   Deadlock or just fear, timing, money trouble?
   Try ONE question first: "What would need to change for this to work?"

2. **The soft exit** (decent person, bad fit):
   "No stress, this month is full anyway. If it matters again, I'm easy
   to find."
   One message. Then stop writing first.

3. **The strong walk** (terms impossible):
   "That number doesn't work for what this takes. I'd rather tell you
   straight than do it half-way."
   This one converts weeks later. You become the vendor who said no nicely.

4. **The hard exit** (abuse, scams, illegal asks):
   "Not something I can help with. Good luck."
   No lectures. The exit IS the boundary.

5. **Two lines max on any goodbye.**
   Long goodbyes read as hurt.

6. **Your goodbye gets screenshots too.**
   It must look clean forwarded by someone who dislikes you.

## When It Backfires

- Threatening to walk for discounts. Called bluffs end deals and status.
- Walking angry. Wait ten minutes, then decide.
- Coming back later at a discount. Full price or not at all.

## One-Line Memory

One warm no teaches everyone your terms are real.
