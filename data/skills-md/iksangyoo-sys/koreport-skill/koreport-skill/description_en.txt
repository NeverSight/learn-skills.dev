Using the methodology from Yu Ik-sang’s “Writing Reports That AI Can’t Write” Lesson 12, Korean business reports are planned, written, evaluated, and revised. It is applied to executive and decision‑making reports, project planning and result reports, status reports, writing or editing work e‑mails, as well as organizing reader needs, extracting messages, crafting titles, tables of contents, storylines, headlines and body text (bundling–unbundling), logic and evidence, slide layouts, sentence proofreading, visualisation, and when polishing or reviewing is requested. It is preferably not applied to creative or academic texts or code documents that are unrelated to reports.
