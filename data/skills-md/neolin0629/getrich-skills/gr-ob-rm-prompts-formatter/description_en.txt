Batch delete the YAML frontmatter at the beginning of Obsidian notes (the Obsidian attribute area, the section wrapped with `---`), keeping only the body text. By default it processes .md files in the `prompts/` directory, but other directories can be specified. Works on macOS / Linux. Trigger methods: /gr-ob-rm-prompts-formatter, /删除 frontmatter, /清理属性, 「remove frontmatter」
