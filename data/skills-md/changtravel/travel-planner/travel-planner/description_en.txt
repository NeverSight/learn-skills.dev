Users need to explore travel preferences, plan routes and budgets, calculate travel expenses, generate travel budget Excel sheets, or create an itinerary when confirming a plan. For simple local 1–2 day trips, start with specific experiences to discover preferences with low friction; for complex intercity, multi‑day trips, gradually fill in transportation, accommodation, companions, budget, and risk constraints. Quotes, service fees, and profit calculations for travel agency clients are not part of this skill. Only modify an existing itinerary or query a single ticket price, handling the task locally without restarting a full plan. Trigger methods: /travel-planner, “Help me plan a trip,” “Customize an itinerary based on budget,” “Generate a travel budget sheet,” “Make the trip expenses into Excel.”
