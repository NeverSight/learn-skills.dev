【Start‑Work Diagnosis】Execution‑ability diagnosis. When users know what they should do (post the first video, add the first batch of contacts, sell the first box of product, launch the first broadcast) but keep hesitating, help them identify which of the six “won’t‑start” states they’re stuck in, and give the smallest actionable step for that type—not just pep talk. Use this when users say “I know what to do but I just don’t do it,” “I’m always preparing,” “I want to change direction,” “I’ll start after finishing this course,” “Let me fine‑tune a bit more,” or “My current conditions don’t allow it.” ‖ Produced by Xiao Ye.
