[Start-Up Diagnosis] Execution capability diagnosis. When a user knows what they should do (post the first video, add the first batch of people, sell the first box of goods, launch the first broadcast) but hesitates to act, help them identify which of the six “not starting” types they are stuck in, and provide the smallest actionable step for that type, not just pep talk. Use this when the user says “I know what to do but I just don’t do it,” “I’m always preparing,” “I want to change direction,” “I’ll start after I finish this course,” “Let me polish a bit more,” “My conditions aren’t right right now.” | Produced by XiaoYe
