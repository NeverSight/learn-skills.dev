[Opening Diagnosis] Short video opening diagnosis and generation. First, pass the gate—determine whether the content qualifies for a good opening; then conduct a six-dimensional diagnosis of the existing opening; finally, using three methods—digging up scripts, supplementing facts, and concealing answers—produce 10–15 candidate openings and recommend the top three. Triggered when the user says “How to write the opening,” “Can’t keep viewers in the first 3 seconds,” “The 5‑second jump is too high,” “What should the first sentence be,” “Help me revise the opening,” or “The opening is too plain.” | Produced by Xiao Ye
