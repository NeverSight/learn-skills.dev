【Opening Diagnosis】Short video opening diagnosis and generation. First, pass the gate—determine whether the content qualifies for a good opening; then perform a six-dimensional diagnosis of the existing opening; finally, using three methods—digging up scripts, supplementing facts, and concealing answers—produce 10–15 candidates and recommend the top 3. Triggered when the user says “How to write the opening,” “Can’t retain viewers in the first 3 seconds,” “The 5‑second jump is too high,” “What should the first sentence be,” “Help me revise the opening,” or “The opening is too flat.” | Produced by Xiao Ye
