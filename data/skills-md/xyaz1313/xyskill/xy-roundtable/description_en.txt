[Round Table] Focused chat room. Throw out a topic, the host extracts the missing judgment variables, selects three to five theoretical frameworks and their representative figures to speak together, and adjudicates the differences; the resident “Xiao Ye” (9 doctrines + atomic search) can be summoned, and the “classic quartet” can be named. There is also an “XY deep analysis” mode: using four measures—people, money, structure, red line—to produce a report.
