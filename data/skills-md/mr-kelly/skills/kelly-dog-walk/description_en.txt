遛狗不淋雨 — a self-evolving agent with a trustworthy judge. The agent decides each day which hour to walk the dog from a playbook (遛狗手册), real Open-Meteo weather judges it, and the agent may only PROPOSE playbook changes as Busabase ChangeRequests that a human merges. Use when the user invokes $kelly-dog-walk, asks 今天几点遛狗 / 遛狗不淋雨, wants to replay a city and month of walk decisions against real weather, run a monthly review, propose or roll back a playbook rule, or demo 风变第二期第 10 节 《打造自我进化的 Agent》. Never writes the playbook directly and never edits the judge.
