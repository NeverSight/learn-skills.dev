Use when someone wants to design their own Video.js player skin: a custom look, layout, or set of controls for a Video.js 10 video or audio player, including one that replicates another player's look, such as YouTube's or Netflix's, built and previewed in a standalone workbench and published to share. Sets up the project with `npx @videojs/cli skin create`, then guides the design, motion, and accessibility work on the skin source. Also use in any project with a `skin.json` from `videojs skin create`.
