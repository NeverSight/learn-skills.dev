HubSpot Work, the project and work-management product inside HubSpot — workspaces, projects, tables of items and subitems, project documents, record comments, and the AI agents that automate a table. Use this skill whenever someone wants to look at, report on, or change work their team tracks in HubSpot Work — find a tracker or board, see what's overdue, blocked or assigned to someone, add tasks, update or bulk-update statuses, mark things done, comment on or @mention someone about an item, read a project's documents, or explain what a project's agents do — even when they only say "my tracker", "the launch board", "our onboarding table" or "my tasks in Work". Not for CRM records (contacts, companies, deals, tickets, CRM tasks), CRM workflows, or HubSpot developer projects, which have their own skills.
