Build small animated 3D worlds for the web (isometric camera, blocks on a rounded base, soft shadows, little vehicles and people that move) in one of six themes you can tune: toy (pastel blocks, warm sun), soft, mono (one accent, the rest tints of ink), line (a hairline drawing, white fills and ink edges, by hand if asked), print (flat tones and ink, a printed illustration) and night. A hero scene for a landing page, a card-sized illustration for docs or a blog, or a mini figure for any small spot (an empty state, a feature in a list, a 404). Use this whenever someone asks for a 3D illustration, an isometric scene, a low-poly or "toy town" animation, a monochrome or line-art 3D figure, a hand-drawn or sketched figure of an object with a bit of life in it, a blank/empty-state illustration, an illustration that answers a hover or a tap, a little game-like SVG world, even if they never say "3D" or "isometric". Ships one self-contained HTML file that draws SVG with no dependency (no three.js, no WebGL, no canvas), checked in a headless browser, and can be dropped into React. For textured terrain, articulated narrative scenes or a pixel finish, use the separate diorama-worlds skill instead; for dithered pixel-art miniatures without three.js, use diorama-pixel.
