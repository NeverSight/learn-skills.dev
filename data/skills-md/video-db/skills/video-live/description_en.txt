Live video and screen recording. Use when the user wants to connect a camera or live stream (RTSP, RTMP, HLS), watch it for an event and get alerted, search or play back what a stream showed, join and record a Zoom, Google Meet or Teams meeting that is happening now, or record their desktop screen and audio and get a summary or searchable timeline of that session. A recording that already exists as a file is a normal video: use video-search to ask about it.
