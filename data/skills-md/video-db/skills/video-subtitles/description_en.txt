Subtitles, captions and transcripts for a video or audio file. Use when the user wants a transcript (plain or timestamped) of a video, podcast or call recording, static subtitles burned in with a chosen font, colour and position, a transcript or subtitles translated into another language, or dubbing. Animated or karaoke captions belong to video-edit. Works on YouTube links, public video URLs, audio files and media already in the user's VideoDB account.
