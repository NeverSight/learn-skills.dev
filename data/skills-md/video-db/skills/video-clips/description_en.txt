Cut clips and highlights from a video. Use when the user wants a clip of a time range, a highlight reel of the moments that match a prompt ("every product demo", "the goals in this match"), a supercut across several videos, a vertical or square cut for Shorts, Reels or TikTok, a thumbnail, or an MP4 to download. One moment found by what was said is video-search, which also cuts that clip; joining whole videos with transitions is video-edit. Works on YouTube links, public URLs and the user's VideoDB videos.
