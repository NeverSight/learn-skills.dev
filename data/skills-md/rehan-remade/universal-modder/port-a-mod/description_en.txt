Port an existing mod to a newer game version or another mod loader while keeping its behaviour, values and quirks. Typical cases are an old Forge 1.7.10 or 1.12 Minecraft mod moving to modern Fabric or NeoForge, a Skyrim LE plugin moving to SE/AE, or a tModLoader 1.3 mod moving to 1.4. It covers pinning one source version, a private decompiled reference, an adaptation matrix, extracting the mod's data with scripts instead of retyping it, porting unit by unit with tests that catch drift, real-client checks, audits against the original, and keeping a long port resumable across sessions and agents. Use when the user wants to update, port, revive or re-implement a mod that already exists, or to continue such a port.
