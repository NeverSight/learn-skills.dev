Draft the governance papers a client's legal, privacy and security reviewers ask for about a Google ADK agent, from the project's evidence. It first screens which privacy and AI laws apply (Australia with NSW and Commonwealth agencies, United States, EU, UK, New Zealand, Singapore, Canada, in that order), then drafts a regulatory memo, an impact assessment (ISO/IEC 42005, NIST AI RMF), privacy impact inputs (PIA or DPIA), transfer facts, a system card, processing records, security questionnaire answers and DTA and NSW AIAF form answers. Each statement cites evidence or is flagged for counsel; not legal advice. Use before a pilot or production decision, or when a DPO, counsel or CISO asks. Do not activate to build redaction, screening or logging controls (protect-adk-sensitive-data), to threat-model or harden the agent (adk-agent-security), for release gates (adk-release-engineering), or to present results (adk-presenter).
