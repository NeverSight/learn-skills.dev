Find where AI would pay off in a business and pick the first use case, before any system is designed: with a rough area in mind, locate where the current journey breaks, choose the first workflow to improve and the business outcome to measure with its baseline; with no use case at all, go from what the business does and the KPIs it watches to the processes that drive them and the opportunities in them. Every opportunity gets a technology fit (agent, single LLM call, classical machine learning, rules or scripting, or a process change) and the portfolio is ranked. Ends with a discovery brief and the prompt for adk-system-designer. Do not activate when the use case and its measure are already chosen (adk-system-designer), for a change to an existing agent (adk-engineer), for market or competitor research, or to present results (adk-presenter).
