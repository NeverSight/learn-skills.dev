Turn a recording into MIDI — pick the right transcription model, run it locally, and check the result before trusting it. Use when the user says "сними партию", "вытащи миди из трека", "audio to MIDI", "какие там ноты", "переиграй это нашими плагинами", "сделай ноты из записи", "тема из того, что я сыграл", or drops audio expecting notes rather than a mix. Covers all three models we run locally — MuScriptor (full-mix multi-instrument), Basic Pitch (fast, licensable, velocity), SwiftF0 (monophonic line, ours, 190x realtime) — measured speed and accuracy on Apple Silicon, what each gets wrong, the known-answer control that catches a silently broken run, and how the MIDI lands in the SuperDuper instruments or in a film score.
