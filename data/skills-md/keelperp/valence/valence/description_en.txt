Give an agent a reward/punishment readout derived from the Drosophila mushroom-body connectome. Turns any state — a text snippet, an embedding, a telemetry or market snapshot — into a 16-dimensional compartment signature plus a signed appetitive-minus-aversive scalar. The 16 compartments and their valence signs are not hand-assigned: they fall out of the PAM:PPL1 dopaminergic synapse ratio in a real fly brain. Use when an agent needs a stable, cheap, deterministic affect channel to compare states, detect regime changes, or rank situations. This layer has no update rule: nothing in it changes with use.
