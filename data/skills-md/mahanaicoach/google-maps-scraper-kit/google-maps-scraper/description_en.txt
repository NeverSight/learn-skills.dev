Google Maps scraper for lead generation. Turns a business type and a place into a clean CSV of local businesses by driving the gosom/google-maps-scraper engine, running locally in Docker as a REST API on localhost:8080. Returns 8 lead fields by default (name, phone, email, website, category, address, rating and review count), plus optional Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn profiles or all ~34 raw fields. Use when the user wants a lead list, "a list of [businesses] in [place]", local business data, contact enrichment for places (emails, phones, socials), or a whole-city run past Google's ~120 results per search. Do not use for scraping Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X or any other social media platform directly.
