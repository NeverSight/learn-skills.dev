Find the published business contacts of companies with Firecrawl, whether the user names them or describes them by type and place: press and media, sales, partnerships and support inboxes, shared business phone numbers and official contact forms, taken from the companies' own websites. Use when the user asks how to contact a company or wants published business emails, phone numbers or press, sales, support or partnership contacts, since contact pages change and an address must come from the page, not memory. Not for named people's details (use firecrawl-lead-research) or finding people to reach out to (use firecrawl-lead-gen).
