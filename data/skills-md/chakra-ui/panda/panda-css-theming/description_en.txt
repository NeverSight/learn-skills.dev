Use when setting up theming, adding brand colors, wiring dark mode or a color-mode toggle, shipping more than one theme with theme.themes, staticCss.themes, data-panda-theme or injectTheme, naming or restructuring design tokens, deciding what belongs in tokens vs semanticTokens vs textStyles vs a recipe, designing a component's variant API, or deciding whether something should be a css() call, a cva, a config recipe, or a slot recipe, including converting a recipe into a slot recipe.
