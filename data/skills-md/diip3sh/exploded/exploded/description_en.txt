Turn a real, rendered UI component (button, input, switch, card, menu, dialog, any React/Vue/HTML component) into a live, interactive exploded spec sheet. The component is pulled apart in isometric into its paint layers (elevation, surface, stroke, inner surfaces, content, focus ring), every state (hover, focus, pressed, disabled, invalid, on/off) can be switched, and every measurement (padding, radius, gap, type, shadow, ring) plus the design token the author actually wrote is read from the browser, never typed. Use this whenever someone wants component documentation, a design-system or Storybook page, a "spec sheet", redlines, an anatomy or breakdown of a component, an exploded or layered view of UI, a visual handoff doc, or an interactive explainer of how a component is built, even if they never say "exploded". Works framework-free (one script and one stylesheet) and in React/Next.js through a wrapper component.
