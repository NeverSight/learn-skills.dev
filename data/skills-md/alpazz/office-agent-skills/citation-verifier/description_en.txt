Verify citations and references in scientific documents to detect hallucinated, invalid, or mismatched sources. Extracts DOIs, arXiv IDs, PubMed IDs, ISBNs, and URLs from Word (.docx), BibTeX (.bib), Markdown (.md), LaTeX (.tex), and plain text, then validates them using CrossRef, arXiv, PubMed, and Open Library APIs. Detects AI hallucinations where real identifiers are paired with fabricated paper titles.
