Directs and builds product demo videos (walkthroughs of a real-looking app and motion-design pieces that are not a walkthrough) as frame-accurate, scripted films rendered from code with Playwright and ffmpeg. Use when someone asks to make a demo video, product walkthrough, launch or promo film, social clip, explainer, app-screen recreation, motion design, or "a video of the product", including when they want a storyboard first. Plans the film beat by beat for approval, then builds it. Covers app-screen fidelity, camera and cursor motion, easing and transitions, captions, honest claims, and a frame-by-frame review.
