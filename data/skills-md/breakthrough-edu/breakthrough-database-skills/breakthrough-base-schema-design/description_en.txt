Design a Lark Base for the user's business through a guided interview: find the one unit of work the business repeats, settle where every kind of record is born (the Base itself, or software they already use such as accounting, a CRM, HR or an online shop) and which way it flows, then derive the tables, links and formulas from the numbers the owner wants to see. Ends with a Base Blueprint, an acceptance test, and a Plan Sheet a fresh session follows to build the Base. Use when the user wants a database or data hub designed ("design my Lark Base", "what tables do I need", "帮我设计多维表格", "我的业务要怎么建表", "data hub", "把会计软件的资料打通进 Base", "哪个系统为准"), when one giant table needs restructuring, or when unsure whether something is a field, a select, or its own table. NOT the build itself (a fresh session follows the Plan Sheet), NOT reports or dashboards from a filled database (breakthrough-database-to-dashboard), NOT a second-brain vault (my-second-brain).
