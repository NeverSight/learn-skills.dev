Make printable technical documents in the style of Apollo-era NASA paperwork. The model is an Apollo Operations Handbook "Systems Data" page with a typed running head, numbered paragraphs, Leroy-lettered line drawings and schematics, a performance data table, NOTE/CAUTION/WARNING notices, a Flight Data File checklist card, a black thumb tab and a dated footer, rendered to an A4 or US Letter PDF. Use this whenever someone asks for NASA-style, Apollo-style, retro aerospace or "mission manual" documentation. Also use it for a technical explainer, spec sheet or one-pager that should look like vintage engineering paperwork, for a handbook page explaining a product, codebase, system or fictional gadget, or for "another one of those NASA pages", even if they never say Apollo or handbook.
