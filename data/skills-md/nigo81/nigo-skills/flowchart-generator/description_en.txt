Internal control and audit flowchart generation tool. Use it when users need to draw swimlane flowcharts, approval processes, reimbursement processes, internal control processes, fund processes, procurement processes, or any business flowchart. Supports generating .drawio/PNG/SVG/VSDX formats from natural language descriptions. Even if "flowchart" isn’t explicitly mentioned, as long as it involves "drawing," "visualizing," or "creating" a business process, it should be triggered. Also applicable to scenarios where users say “help me draw an XX process” or “visualize this process,” etc.
