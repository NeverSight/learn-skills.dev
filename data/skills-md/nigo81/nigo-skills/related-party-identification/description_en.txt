An audit verification tool that automatically identifies “hidden related parties” based on business registration data. It reads the full set of business dimensions (52 Excel files) for the audited entity and its customers and suppliers, then automatically compares eight major dimensions—including address, phone, email, website, legal representative, board and senior executives, equity penetration, abnormal customer‑supplier profiles, shared intangible assets, guarantee chains, and historical关联 (relationship) traces. The tool generates a multi‑sheet Excel verification report (a summary judgment sheet plus evidence sheets for each dimension) to uncover undisclosed related‑party relationships.

Use this tool whenever the user mentions terms such as related party, related‑party verification, related‑party identification, hidden related party, implicit related party, undisclosed related party, de‑linking of related‑party transactions, relationship ties, customer‑supplier relationship, whether a customer is a related party, whether a supplier has any tricks, equity penetration, ultimate controller verification, related‑party due diligence, audit related parties, “related party,” or related‑party verification working papers. It should also be triggered by expressions like “check whether these companies are related,” “help me review the business registration data of these customers and suppliers,” “is this company a shell,” or “the audit needs to verify related parties.” The tool works together with the cicpa‑company‑query skill to retrieve data, and its rule set aligns with recent enforcement cases from the China Securities Regulatory Commission and the Ministry of Finance.
