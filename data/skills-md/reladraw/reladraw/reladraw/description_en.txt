Write, render and edit diagrams in reladraw, a text diagram language where you say where things go, so the arrangement can be read back out of the source without looking at the picture. Use when asked to draw, diagram, sketch or visualize an architecture, a system, a data flow, a pipeline, a deployment, a directory layout, or the shape of a change or pull request; when reading or editing a .reladraw file; or when the user says "reladraw", "diagram this", "draw the architecture", "show me how these pieces fit". Use it in place of Mermaid, Graphviz, D2 or hand-drawn ASCII boxes whenever a node-and-line diagram is wanted and reladraw is installed. NOT for charts of data — bar, line, pie, scatter — and NOT for pictures that are not nodes and lines.
