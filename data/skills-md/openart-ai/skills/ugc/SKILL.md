# OpenArt UGC

Before interpreting any brief, script or timecoded draft, read
[intent and script development](references/intent-and-script-development.md). Preserve the intended message,
required facts and exact keywords; actively improve expression, reasoning, actions and flexible beat timing
in this skill's style. Input detail is not an instruction to execute verbatim. Explicit exact-execution
constraints and an approved version requested unchanged remain locked.

When the Script Plan revises supplied creative input, END that response by briefly explaining what changed
and why, then ask whether to use the revised script or follow the original input exactly. Resolve that
version choice before generation under [generation approval](references/generation-approval.md);
a Draft/full-quality choice alone is not acceptance of the rewrite.

Before authoring, read [shared video experience](references/video-experience.md) and
[product understanding](references/product-understanding.md). Before a generation decision, read
[generation approval](references/generation-approval.md), including native question UI and wording examples.
Complete the full prompt and its matching readable Script Plan before showing either for approval; keep
all spoken and on-screen text in that same version. Default to Seedance 2.5 Draft preview; explicit
high-quality/full-quality delivery defaults directly to Seedance 2.5. Reuse clear approval or an explicit
approval waiver. These shared rules own the experience flow across every video and variant.
For presenter-led promotions, also read [opening hooks](references/opening-hooks.md): establish relevance
in the first 3–5 seconds and show the product from the first image without forcing a spoken brand pitch.

Read the entrypoint and these shared workflow guides, then follow the relevant references linked below.
Supporting Markdown is loaded on demand, not automatically in full. Read reference-analysis guides
for uploaded reference videos, product research for product URLs, direction before authoring, OpenArt
execution before fixing references, duration or request counts, and review before acceptance. Follow nested
links when that decision requires them.

Create a persuasive UGC ad that feels like a close, relatable person enthusiastically sharing a useful
discovery and recommending it. Everyday intimacy, tailored personality and contagious positive energy
are the defining priorities, not a professional presenter reading product facts in a casual room.
Understand the user's intent and develop sparse or detailed drafts into a stronger UGC recommendation.
Preserve required facts, exact phrases and explicit locks; detail alone does not make a script immutable.
See [README](README.md) for installation and deliverables.

Before authoring or revising prompts, read [creative preflight](references/creative-preflight.md).
Product images and a short brief are a valid starting point; actively design progression and
performance within this skill's boundary. Audit the final prompt for unjustified restrictions before submission.

## Establish intent and evidence

Use [intake](references/intake.md) to identify the task, reference roles, creator, situation, product and output.
Resume existing work with [project memory](references/project-memory.md), preserving accepted choices and jobs.
For a video reference, read [reference understanding](references/reference-understanding.md) and inspect its
complete visual and spoken argument. Do not infer natural performance from a transcript or first frame alone.
Read [product and claims](references/product-and-claims.md) before writing experiences or demonstrations.

UGC is a creative treatment here. A generated creator is not proof that a customer purchased, tested or
endorsed the product. A casual home scene alone does not make a cinematic commercial or staged PGC take UGC.
PGC emphasizes deliberately arranged camera/lighting and structured presentation; this mode emphasizes an
everyday situation and a personal conversational relationship. Respect explicit hybrid briefs.

## Direct the creator and sequence

Read [creative direction](references/creative-direction.md) to tailor cast, everyday setting,
recommendation energy and observable performance to the product and audience.
Before developing or improving copy, read [recommendation writing](references/recommendation-writing.md) and
[latte reference script](references/recommendation-examples.md). Establish why this person would share the product
with this listener, connect supported details to everyday consequences, and let the demonstration help explain.
Read [ad structure and pacing](references/ad-structure-and-pacing.md) to organize that reasoning and fit duration.
Establish relevance early; do not default to a claim/offer/CTA recital. Integrate commercial requirements into
the conversation. Explicit verbatim instructions, approved unchanged scripts and non-ad/raw-take scope take priority.
Use [sequence and prompts](references/sequence-and-prompts.md) for exact dialogue, actions, shots and sound.
Read [music and sound](references/music-and-sound.md) before choosing the audio treatment. Normally include
light, product-appropriate background music; deliberately omit it when the format or user calls for unscored sound.
Fit duration to meaningful speech and action rather than stretching the performance.

Use [continuity and variants](references/continuity-and-variants.md) for identity, voice, product state and
changes across requests. Distinguish designed shots, generation requests and final deliverables.
One complete model-generated video is preferred when feasible. Separate clips are a different output contract.

## Generate, review and deliver

Apply [OpenArt execution](references/openart-execution.md), read while planning. Use its live models and exact
schemas, bind real reference assets, save receipts, recover outstanding jobs and stay within authorized scope.
Use the shared Draft/full-quality selection; honor an explicitly specified model. A prompt-only request ends with prompts, not media jobs.

The model generates any requested shot changes, speech, ambience, music or captions inside its output.
No Hypit, local stitching, external dubbing or promised later graphics repair is part of this skill.
Carry the chosen music treatment into the generation prompt and listening review. Captions follow the brief;
do not remove required speech or planned music after a generation error without resolving the mismatch.

Use [review and delivery](references/review-and-delivery.md) to check full playback, natural behavior, actual
words, lip sync, product fidelity, handling and identity. Fix the failed request within scope and inspect again.
Deliver the selected video or explicitly agreed clip set, complete prompts, task/settings/reference records
and accepted/rejected/unverified QA. Never equate successful submission with a convincing creator performance.
