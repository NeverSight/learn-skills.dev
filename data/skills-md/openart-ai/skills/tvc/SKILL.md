# OpenArt TVC

Before interpreting any brief, script or timecoded draft, read
[intent and script development](references/intent-and-script-development.md). Preserve the intended message,
required facts and exact keywords; actively improve expression, reasoning, actions and flexible beat timing
in this skill's style. Input detail is not an instruction to execute verbatim. Explicit exact-execution
constraints and an approved version requested unchanged remain locked.

When the Script Plan revises supplied creative input, END that response by briefly explaining what changed
and why, then ask whether to use the revised script or follow the original input exactly. Resolve that
version choice before generation under [generation approval](references/generation-approval.md);
a Draft/full-quality choice alone is not acceptance of the rewrite.

Before authoring, read [shared video experience](references/video-experience.md) and
[product understanding](references/product-understanding.md). Before a generation decision, read
[generation approval](references/generation-approval.md), including native question UI and wording examples.
Complete the full prompt and its matching readable Script Plan before showing either for approval; keep
all spoken and on-screen text in that same version. Default to Seedance 2.5 Draft preview; explicit
high-quality/full-quality delivery defaults directly to Seedance 2.5. Reuse clear approval or an explicit
approval waiver. These shared rules own the experience flow across every video and variant.
For presenter-led promotions, also read [opening hooks](references/opening-hooks.md): establish relevance
in the first 3–5 seconds and show the product from the first image without forcing a spoken brand pitch.

Read the entrypoint and these shared workflow guides, then follow the relevant references linked below.
Supporting Markdown is loaded on demand, not automatically in full. Read reference-analysis guides
for uploaded reference videos, product research for product URLs, direction before authoring, OpenArt
execution before fixing references, duration or request counts, and review before acceptance. Follow nested
links when that decision requires them.

Direct a brand commercial whose images, product presence and sound express a specific idea. The brand may be
playful, accessible, technical, warm or premium. Luxury imagery, a human story and a presenter are not mandatory.
Honor the user's brief and accepted choices before applying defaults.

For installation and deliverables, see [README](README.md). Match the user's conversation language and requested
speech/text languages. Default to English creative directions when unspecified; infer commissioned speech/text
language from the brief and state material assumptions.

Before authoring or revising prompts, read [creative preflight](references/creative-preflight.md).
Product images and a short brief are a valid starting point; actively design progression and
performance within this skill's boundary. Audit the final prompt for unjustified restrictions before submission.

## Understand the commission

Read [intake](references/intake.md). Identify whether the user needs a new concept, reference adaptation,
execution of an approved plan, variants, or correction of an existing result. Do not restart settled creative work.
Use [project memory](references/project-memory.md) to resume decisions, active jobs and accepted outputs.

For supplied films, read [reference understanding](references/reference-understanding.md) and inspect the whole
experience, including the ending and sound. Use [product and claims](references/product-and-claims.md) to establish
what the product and brand references actually support. Separate style transfer from exact source editing.

## Design the brand experience

Use [creative direction](references/creative-direction.md) to choose a brand idea, visual relationships and
an appropriate form. Treat the product as part of the idea rather than an unrelated closing insert.
Develop only the treatment needed to make the next decision.
Read [music and sound](references/music-and-sound.md). Normally design a brand-level musical identity and
emotional progression suited to this product and film, including its ending; do not leave music unspecified.

Use [sequence and prompts](references/sequence-and-prompts.md) to turn the idea into concrete framing, action,
camera, material/light, transition, text and sound direction. Plan the closing image within the duration.
Use [continuity and variants](references/continuity-and-variants.md) for product/world consistency and revisions.
Designed shots and generation requests are different units.

## Generate, inspect and deliver

Apply [OpenArt execution](references/openart-execution.md), read while planning. Verify live model capabilities,
bind actual references, submit within authorized scope and preserve receipts. A request for a video calls for
execution when feasible; a prompt-only or analysis request stops at that requested deliverable.

The intended output is a complete model-generated commercial when supported. Internal cuts, graphics,
music and multiple scenes may be directed in the model prompt. Hypit, local stitching and an external
post-production fallback are not part of this workflow. Resolve a fixed-duration or fidelity mismatch before
presenting an alternative as equivalent.

Use [review and delivery](references/review-and-delivery.md) to check the whole output against the brief,
product truth and creative intent. Correct the affected request within scope, preserving accepted assets.
Return the video, prompt/settings/reference records, selected version and honest QA status.
A planned treatment, successful task or collection of unassembled shots is not proof of a finished commercial.
