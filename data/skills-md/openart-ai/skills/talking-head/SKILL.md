# Build Talking Head Video

Before interpreting any brief, script or timecoded draft, read
[intent and script development](references/intent-and-script-development.md). Preserve the intended message,
required facts and exact keywords; actively improve expression, reasoning, actions and flexible beat timing
in this skill's style. Input detail is not an instruction to execute verbatim. Explicit exact-execution
constraints and an approved version requested unchanged remain locked.

When the Script Plan revises supplied creative input, END that response by briefly explaining what changed
and why, then ask whether to use the revised script or follow the original input exactly. Resolve that
version choice before generation under [generation approval](references/generation-approval.md);
a Draft/full-quality choice alone is not acceptance of the rewrite.

Before authoring, read [shared video experience](references/video-experience.md) and
[product understanding](references/product-understanding.md). Before a generation decision, read
[generation approval](references/generation-approval.md), including native question UI and wording examples.
Complete the full prompt and matching readable Script Plan before presenting the plan; include every
spoken and added on-screen word. Default to Seedance 2.5 Draft preview. Explicit high-quality/full-quality
delivery defaults directly to Seedance 2.5, not another model. Reuse explicit approval and approval waivers.

The person addressing the viewer is the organizing form. A continuous shot can contain a question,
demonstration, closer look, reaction and useful conclusion. Do not reduce the video to a motionless face
reading short slogans. Choose the setup from the message and audience rather than automatically imposing
either UGC informality or PGC studio polish. High-quality delivery describes the render choice, not a genre.
An explicitly requested Talking Head video stays here even when it shares craft with UGC or PGC.

Read supporting guides when their decision arises; [README](README.md) explains installation and outputs.
Use the user's conversation language and commissioned speech/text languages. Honor analysis-only and
prompt-only requests without generating media.

## Establish what the person is helping the viewer understand

Read [intake and script](references/intake-and-script.md). Establish the message, audience, presenter,
setting, tone, language, ratio, desired duration and actual generation scope. Reuse supplied information;
ask only about meaningful gaps. For non-product explanation, do not demand product images or promotional copy.

For uploaded reference videos, read [reference understanding](references/reference-understanding.md).
Inspect complete picture and sound, including phrasing, pauses, emotional emphasis, product handling and
the ending. Distinguish observation from inference and identity from creative inspiration. If the user
supplies only a social-video URL, request the file; there is no link downloader in this skill.

A real presenter's recording can guide identity/performance within the live model's supported capabilities.
Photos establish appearance, not voice. Direct a fictional presenter when no real identity is requested;
do not force personal uploads or invent real testimony, credentials, product trials or use history.

## Author the opening and the useful conversation

Read [opening hooks](references/opening-hooks.md). The first 3–5 seconds should establish a relevant reason
to watch: a specific question, familiar situation, worthwhile curiosity or visible operation. In product
promotions, the product is visible from the first image, naturally held or used. It need not be named in
the first sentence. Non-product videos start with their relevant subject rather than a fabricated product.

Before developing or improving recommendation copy, read [recommendation writing](references/recommendation-writing.md)
and the [latte reference](references/recommendation-examples.md). Transfer the connection between supported
details, visible use and everyday relevance; do not copy a creator's biography or catchphrases. Teaching
and opinion videos use the same connected reasoning without a forced offer or CTA.

Read [creative preflight](references/creative-preflight.md) and
[performance and prompts](references/performance-and-prompts.md). Write the complete native prompt first
or alongside its matching Script Plan. Direct meaningful shifts in intention, gaze, selective emphasis,
natural pace and physically plausible handling. Give useful actions enough time without stalling the thought.
Show timed plan rows even for a single take so the user can review speech, action and text together.

Keep the presenter as the visual anchor throughout each clip. Default to a continuous shot with same-shot
product demonstration when relevant. Native captions or short labels can be planned when requested or
useful to the brief, but all exact text belongs in both prompt and Script Plan. Explicit clean-footage
requests omit added text. No B-roll montage, external dubbing, local editing or hidden later composition
is included. Resolve a request requiring those capabilities rather than promising an unfinished pipeline.

Read [music and sound](references/music-and-sound.md). Promotional takes normally include fitting light
BGM beneath intelligible speech. Podcasts, interview-like delivery, sound-led demonstrations and explicit
dialogue-only requests may remain unscored. Put the actual decision in the prompt and readable plan.

Read [OpenArt execution](references/openart-execution.md) before fixing references, duration or clip count.
Resolve actual Seedance 2.5 Draft or full-quality capabilities, references, audio, duration and pricing
through live tools. Read [segmentation and continuity](references/segmentation-and-continuity.md) when the
speech exceeds the live request limit or variants are requested. Prefer one take when it fits; otherwise split
at complete thoughts, preserve voice/identity/product continuity and explain the separate-clip delivery.
Do not label unassembled clips as one uninterrupted video or rush dialogue to fit.

## Generate the selected version

Submit the stored authorized prompt version with its selected settings through
[OpenArt execution](references/openart-execution.md); save the receipt and recover outstanding jobs before
creating replacements.

Follow the shared experience for preview review, revisions and an authorized same-video HD upgrade.
Do not substitute a fresh random generation for an upgrade or silently change the video model.

## Review and deliver

Use [review and delivery](references/review-and-delivery.md). Check the full performance and opening,
speech including brand names/prices, product appearance/actions, lip sync, exact planned text, music and
ending. A screenshot or successful job is not proof of human delivery. Mark unavailable checks unverified.

Deliver the selected preview or full-quality original video, or the explicitly agreed ordered clip set,
with its Script Plan/prompt version, settings, receipts and honest review status. Show a preview as a
preview, and offer only the upgrade that the actual selected result supports.
