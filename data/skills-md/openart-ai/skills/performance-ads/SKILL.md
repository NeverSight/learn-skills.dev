# OpenArt Performance Ads

Before interpreting any brief, script or timecoded draft, read
[intent and script development](references/intent-and-script-development.md). Preserve the intended message,
required facts and exact keywords; actively improve expression, reasoning, actions and flexible beat timing
in this skill's style. Input detail is not an instruction to execute verbatim. Explicit exact-execution
constraints and an approved version requested unchanged remain locked.

When the Script Plan revises supplied creative input, END that response by briefly explaining what changed
and why, then ask whether to use the revised script or follow the original input exactly. Resolve that
version choice before generation under [generation approval](references/generation-approval.md);
a Draft/full-quality choice alone is not acceptance of the rewrite.

Before authoring, read [shared video experience](references/video-experience.md) and
[product understanding](references/product-understanding.md). Before a generation decision, read
[generation approval](references/generation-approval.md), including native question UI and wording examples.
Complete the full prompt and its matching readable Script Plan before showing either for approval; keep
all spoken and on-screen text in that same version. Default to Seedance 2.5 Draft preview; explicit
high-quality/full-quality delivery defaults directly to Seedance 2.5. Reuse clear approval or an explicit
approval waiver. These shared rules own the experience flow across every video and variant.
For presenter-led promotions, also read [opening hooks](references/opening-hooks.md): establish relevance
in the first 3–5 seconds and show the product from the first image without forcing a spoken brand pitch.

Read the entrypoint and these shared workflow guides, then follow the relevant references linked below.
Supporting Markdown is loaded on demand, not automatically in full. Read reference-analysis guides
for uploaded reference videos, product research for product URLs, direction before authoring, OpenArt
execution before fixing references, duration or request counts, and review before acceptance. Follow nested
links when that decision requires them.

Make a feed ad that gives the intended audience a reason to stop, understand the product and take the requested
next step. These are different decisions; do not optimize only the opening. Follow the user's brief and accepted
choices before defaults. Presenter footage, stickers, large text, fast cuts and a CTA are not universal requirements.

See [README](README.md) for installation and output. Match the user's conversation language. Use the requested
speech/text languages; when unspecified, infer them from the brief and record material assumptions.
Creative directions may default to English without changing the commissioned dialogue.

Before authoring or revising prompts, read [creative preflight](references/creative-preflight.md).
Product images and a short brief are a valid starting point; actively design progression and
performance within this skill's boundary. Audit the final prompt for unjustified restrictions before submission.

## Locate the task and evidence

Use [intake](references/intake.md) to identify new creation, approved-plan execution, reference adaptation,
output repair, or variants. Resume prior work through [project memory](references/project-memory.md).
Do not replace an approved concept merely because a different hook occurs to you.

For supplied ads, use [reference understanding](references/reference-understanding.md) to examine the entire
piece and sound, including product recognition, evidence and payoff. Use
[product and claims](references/product-and-claims.md) for appearance, offers, testimonials and supported claims.

## Direct the ad

Use [creative direction](references/creative-direction.md) to connect attention to one main communication goal.
Choose action, demonstration, situation, comparison, humor, sensory detail or graphics according to the task.
Use [sequence and prompts](references/sequence-and-prompts.md) to assign a role to each shot, word and sound.
Read [music and sound](references/music-and-sound.md). Normally include product-appropriate BGM, with energy
suited to the creative treatment and clear speech; deliberately omit it when the format or user calls for it.

For alternative hooks, concepts, messages, formats or CTAs, use
[continuity and variants](references/continuity-and-variants.md). Identify the variable and intended constants.
A revision to a complete generation can change more than the intended passage; preserve scope in direction
and verify the actual result.

## Execute and review

Apply [OpenArt execution](references/openart-execution.md), read while planning. Resolve live capabilities, real
reference bindings and authorized generation scope. Complete video requests with generation when feasible;
honor prompt-only and analysis requests without submitting jobs.

This workflow generates the ad's scenes, cuts, audio and any graphics inside the video model's output.
It has no Hypit, local stitching or hidden post-production stage. Do not remove essential text or audio just
to turn a failed generation into a nominal success.

Use [review and delivery](references/review-and-delivery.md) for product/message clarity, actual text and sound,
creative intent and variant integrity. Deliver selected videos with prompts, task/settings/reference records
and honest QA. A creative hypothesis is not a verified conversion improvement.
