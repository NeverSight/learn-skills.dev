# OpenArt Product Listing Video

Before interpreting any brief, script or timecoded draft, read
[intent and script development](references/intent-and-script-development.md). Preserve the intended message,
required facts and exact keywords; actively improve expression, reasoning, actions and flexible beat timing
in this skill's style. Input detail is not an instruction to execute verbatim. Explicit exact-execution
constraints and an approved version requested unchanged remain locked.

When the Script Plan revises supplied creative input, END that response by briefly explaining what changed
and why, then ask whether to use the revised script or follow the original input exactly. Resolve that
version choice before generation under [generation approval](references/generation-approval.md);
a Draft/full-quality choice alone is not acceptance of the rewrite.

Before authoring, read [shared video experience](references/video-experience.md) and
[product understanding](references/product-understanding.md). Before a generation decision, read
[generation approval](references/generation-approval.md), including native question UI and wording examples.
Complete the full prompt and its matching readable Script Plan before showing either for approval; keep
all spoken and on-screen text in that same version. Default to Seedance 2.5 Draft preview; explicit
high-quality/full-quality delivery defaults directly to Seedance 2.5. Reuse clear approval or an explicit
approval waiver. These shared rules own the experience flow across every video and variant.
For presenter-led promotions, also read [opening hooks](references/opening-hooks.md): establish relevance
in the first 3–5 seconds and show the product from the first image without forcing a spoken brand pitch.

Read the entrypoint and these shared workflow guides, then follow the relevant references linked below.
Supporting Markdown is loaded on demand, not automatically in full. Read reference-analysis guides
for uploaded reference videos, product research for product URLs, direction before authoring, OpenArt
execution before fixing references, duration or request counts, and review before acceptance. Follow nested
links when that decision requires them.

Focus on the product-detail page media gallery: a video that sits alongside item photos and helps a shopper
inspect the exact product. Amazon-style listings are the primary use case; other storefront galleries can
use the same method. A gallery video, its cover image and a marketplace's main product image are distinct
assets and placements. Do not assume a video can replace the primary image or control its gallery position.

Help the viewer understand the actual product and make the intended purchase judgment. Product fidelity,
useful evidence and clear information govern the creative choices. A clean showcase, a usage scene and an
annotated feature demonstration can all serve this purpose.

Follow the user's requirements and accepted decisions before defaults. See [README](README.md) for installation
and deliverables. Match the user's conversation language and commissioned speech/text languages; infer missing
language choices from the brief. English creative directions do not require English product copy or speech.

Before authoring or revising prompts, read [creative preflight](references/creative-preflight.md).
Product images and a short brief are a valid starting point; actively design progression and
performance within this skill's boundary. Audit the final prompt for unjustified restrictions before submission.

## Establish the product and task

Read [intake](references/intake.md) to distinguish a new video, approved-plan execution, reference adaptation,
output repair, or SKU/format variants. Use [project memory](references/project-memory.md) to resume existing work.
Read [product and claims](references/product-and-claims.md) before selecting features or designing demonstrations.

For a supplied reference, use [reference understanding](references/reference-understanding.md) to inspect the
whole piece, including what each image proves, label behavior, physical operations and sound.
The reference's features and accessories do not transfer to the target product automatically.

## Choose and direct the demonstration

Use [creative direction](references/creative-direction.md) to choose among showcase, detail, usage, specification,
accessory, setting or annotated-feature approaches. White background plus feature callouts is a useful default
when appropriate, not a requirement imposed on every listing video. Lead with a recognizable item and
prioritize useful buying information over a disruptive feed hook or an unsolicited promotional CTA.

Use [sequence and prompts](references/sequence-and-prompts.md) to connect each message to a visible detail or
supported operation. Specify exact label copy and annotation behavior when used.
Read [music and sound](references/music-and-sound.md). Normally use a light, fitting music bed that supports
inspection; preserve product sounds as evidence and choose unscored passages or videos when appropriate.
Use [continuity and variants](references/continuity-and-variants.md) to lock SKU, appearance, object state,
quantity and variant ownership.

## Generate and verify

Apply [OpenArt execution](references/openart-execution.md), read while planning. Resolve live capabilities and real
references; generate the requested video within scope, or honor an explicit prompt-only/analysis request.

The output is a model-generated product video with requested shots, sound and graphics inside the result.
No Hypit, local compositing, stitching or promised later typography repair is part of the workflow.
If exact dimensions, spelling, mechanics or source preservation cannot be achieved, identify the gap rather
than quietly removing the requirement.

Use [review and delivery](references/review-and-delivery.md) to inspect the actual product, information,
demonstration and technical result. Return selected videos, prompts, reference/settings/task records and
per-version QA. A plausible render is not evidence of an unverified product feature.
