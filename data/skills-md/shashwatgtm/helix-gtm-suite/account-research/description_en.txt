Researches B2B target accounts for ABM, sales and demand generation: builds ICP fit account lists, audits and enriches lists in fixed fields, and writes a one account brief in TOPS order (technographics, organisation, person, situation) with a probe question for every finding. Every field has a source and a verified or unverified status: a data connector first, then two to three web sources; every account on the list covered; banned sources removed; it stops when the named source fails. The person block uses only public professional statements with link and date; personality notes are inferences. Use when someone says "research this account", "account brief", "brief me before the call", "account intelligence", "enrich these accounts", "build a target account list", "find companies that fit our ICP", "audit my account list", "ABM list", "ABM pilot ideas", "technographics", "tech stack", "dedupe", "probe questions" or pastes companies to research. Not for market sizing, private data or outreach emails.
