Check whether AI agents doing buyer research can evaluate, shortlist and recommend a B2B webpage, and get the fixes ranked by impact. Fetches the URL, analyzes the rendered HTML, and returns a 4-signal scorecard (0 to 100), the top 5 fixes, a "not ideal for" template if that signal is weak, and a before and after for the highest-priority fix. Use when the user asks for a RACE audit, agent-readiness audit, AI agent evaluation check, or wants to know if AI agents will recommend their page. The FITq audit scores AI search citation instead. Method: the Optise RACE™ framework (Requirements, Actions, Constraints, Evidence). Authored by Optise + Helix GTM Consulting under the Optise EU AEO Playbook methodology.
