Judges another model's or a colleague's critique of a draft, or a draft another model wrote, point by point, and ends with one final version, not a list. Each point goes through three filters in order (factual accuracy, the stated objectives, tone and machine sounding text) and gets a verdict: accept, accept with edit, modify or deny, with a reason. Facts are checked against an opened source before they are applied; a second model's draft is judged first on its purpose; the review challenges the user's view too and says which review is sharper. When the purpose is unclear it asks first, then offers options. Use when someone says "another AI assistant reviewed my draft", "another AI gave this feedback", "which suggestions should I take", "second opinion", "is this critique right", "take the logical ones and reject the rest", "give me the final output", "another AI assistant wrote this", "critique my plan" or "why are you agreeing with me". Not for a plain fact check or a research asset.
