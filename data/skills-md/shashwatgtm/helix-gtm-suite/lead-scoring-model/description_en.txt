Builds a lead or account fit scoring model from a B2B team's ICP: criteria, weights, values best first with graded points, the maximum and tiers A to D by script, with the user's weights or example defaults (critical 25, important 15, nice to have 10) labelled as starting examples, never a benchmark; scores leads, where a missing value means incomplete, never 0; and says how to validate on closed deals. Fit only: event lead engagement scoring stays with the events plugin, which has the user set every weight. Use when someone says "lead scoring model", "account fit score", "ICP scoring", "fit score", "score our leads", "score these accounts", "build a scoring model from our ICP", "tier our leads A B C D", "which accounts are tier A", "qualification scorecard" or pastes criteria or leads. Not for event engagement scoring, MQL rules or account lists.
