Writes outbound and deal stage email sequences for B2B sellers: cold outreach, warm follow up, post demo, proposal follow up, re-engagement of a deal that went quiet, and referral requests. Send days come from a script (example defaults, or the seller's own days and number of emails, which always win), weekend dates moved to working days, subject and body counted by script, every claim once with its source, one ask per email, and a "before you send" list. For account executives, SDRs, founders who sell and sales leaders. Use when someone says "email sequence", "cold outreach sequence", "cadence", "outbound emails", "follow up sequence after the demo", "post demo emails", "proposal follow up emails", "re-engage a dead deal", "the deal went quiet", "referral request emails" or "write my follow ups". Not for event follow up or nurture series, a single email after one meeting, or emails that need facts the seller does not have.
