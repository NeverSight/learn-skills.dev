Reviews and revises the ICP a B2B team already has: compares today's customers with the ICP part by part, computes metric gaps by script (contract value, sales cycle, win rate, churn, NPS) with labelled example bands, and turns recent wins, losses and market changes into proposed ICP changes, each a hypothesis with the CRM check that would confirm it, ending in a marked up draft. For founders, product marketers, RevOps and demand generation. Use when someone says "review our ICP", "ICP review", "ICP gap analysis", "are we selling to the right customers", "how should our ICP change", "update our ICP from wins and losses", "ICP evolution", "quarterly ICP review", "revise our ideal customer profile" or pastes an ICP with customer or deal data. Not for PMF scorecards against targets (pmf-check), an ICP from scratch or account lists, competitor win and loss analysis, or account saves.
