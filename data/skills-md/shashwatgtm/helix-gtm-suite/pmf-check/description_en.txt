Checks a B2B team's product market fit metrics only against the team's own targets: churn, NPS in points, LTV to CAC, retention, activation (not applicable for services, connectivity or investment businesses, which use an adoption measure) and any measure the team tracks, compared by script, with the dimensions behind target ranked, missing values and targets reported as not scored, and no published range, benchmark or stage label; pasted customer feedback is kept word for word and sorted into story, result, claim or friction. For founders, product marketers and RevOps. Use when someone says "PMF check", "product market fit check", "are we at product market fit", "PMF scorecard", "check our metrics against our targets", "how far are we from our targets" or pastes metrics with targets. Not for account churn risk (b2b-customer-success), revising the ICP (icp-review) or market sizing.
