Records a demo of the real running product: seeds its state, writes the flow as a script file, drives the actual web app or desktop app, and hands back a short clean take (mp4 or webm, plus a GIF for a README) in which one thing happens and every pointer move has a cause. Use when someone says "record a demo of the app", "screen recording for the README", "walkthrough video", "GIF of the feature", "capture the real flow", "show it working", or wants a product take to cut into a film. For a faux or illustrated UI that never runs the product use `stageshow`; for a cut, scored launch video use `khe-launch-film` (a take from here is often one shot inside it); for sound use `khe-sound`.
