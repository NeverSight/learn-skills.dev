Designs, animates and packs stickers of every kind: flat die-cut, lettering, character, badge, isometric slab and reaction, drawn as one set with one outline and one palette, each with a single motion that ends on a still, then rendered to static and animated SVG, PNG and the WhatsApp and Telegram formats. Use when someone asks to "make a sticker", wants a "sticker pack", "WhatsApp stickers", "Telegram stickers", an "isometric sticker", a mascot or reaction sticker, or says "animate this sticker". For icons, logos and general SVG use `khe-svg`; for the palette contract use `khe-design-tokens`; when a sticker needs a sound use `khe-sound`.
