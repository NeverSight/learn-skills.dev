Decides whether a diagram earns its place over a sentence or a table, picks the surface by who touches the picture next, then draws a finished editorial figure or edits a live tldraw canvas, with connector, accent and budget rules that also serve as the review. Use when someone says "can you diagram this", "draw the architecture", "make a flowchart", "sketch this on a board", "my diagram looks like Mermaid", or names tldraw, a .tldraw file or diagram-design. For SVG markup and animation use `khe-svg`; for a live chart inside a product use `khe-ui`; for a leader line on a product shot use `stageshow`.
