Packages something you built for people who have never heard of it: picks the format (an explainer rendered from HTML, a click-through of one live component, a hover reel, a recording page), sends three ideas as stills before any render, renders one frame at a time on a paused clock at 60 fps with the short side at 2000 px or more, crops to the work with 40 to 60 px of page around it, moves your system's real cursor on a slight curve and holds it still while the motion plays, then writes the post's first line for a stranger, not for you. Use when someone says "package this", "make a video of this for X", "render it at 4k", "a clip for the launch post", "how should I show this off", "give me ideas for the announcement video", or "package my work". To record a whole product flow use `khe-demo`; for a cut, scored film `khe-launch-film`; for faux UI `stageshow` (Benji Taylor); for the posts on each platform `khe-content-engine`. When unsure, `khe-auto`.
