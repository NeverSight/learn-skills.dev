Creates, cleans and animates SVG files: icons, illustrations, logo reveals, stroke draw-ons, path morphs, mascots, and flat video clips turned into one editable vector file, each on the token system and moved by the engine its home allows. Use when someone asks to make or fix an SVG, says "this icon won't recolor", "the export is 200 KB", "it rotates around the corner", "the morph jumps", or wants a logo, icon or mascot to move. For page transitions, gestures and timing beyond the file use `khe-ui`; for choosing an icon pack for a product use `khe-ui`; for stickers and sticker packs use `khe-sticker`; for diagrams use `khe-diagram`.
