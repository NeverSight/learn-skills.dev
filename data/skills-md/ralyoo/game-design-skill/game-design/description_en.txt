Design games with soul, from idea to shipped assets and micro-feedback. A gated workflow (idea → concept → story → mechanics → unity → progression → screen flow & transitions → assets → experience control & juice), a player-psychology toolkit, a GDD template, a platform-profile system so the same method works for any engine or platform, and a low-cost asset pipeline (one generated vector master per asset, variants edited by hand in SVG) plus audio direction and loudness mastering. Image and audio generation are optional and provider-agnostic; Recraft and ElevenLabs helpers are included as recommended defaults. Use before designing, revising, or reviewing a game design, GDD, art bible, or audio bible.
