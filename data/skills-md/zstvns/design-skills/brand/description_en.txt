The entry point for any brand work. Gathers the brand's existing assets itself from just a URL (crawling the site, following its links to social and press) — or, for a brand that doesn't exist yet, starts from the name and the idea — diagnoses how much should change, routes to the right path, and gets-or-makes the brand context document every other design skill reads. Use at the start of any design project, or when the user mentions 'brand context,' 'set up the brand,' 'brand guidelines,' 'our visual identity,' 'rebrand,' 'refresh our brand,' 'what's our brand,' 'starting a new brand,' 'we don't have a website yet,' or wants to stop re-explaining their colors, type, and visual direction on every task. Creates and maintains `.agents/brand.md`.
