Workshop that builds your own video-editing skills. Interviews you about a format (stream, lesson, vertical or your own), looks at frames of your footage, assembles a design system with you, edits a trial clip until you say yes, and installs a self-contained format skill that edits every next video the same way. Or, if you say «без вопросов», takes one message and a recording and returns the finished video and the installed skill. Also refines a format skill you already have. Starts only on /aiediting.
