Public Account Headline Growth Ranking Tool. Based on Honghu data, it calculates weekly growth of public account headline reads (this week vs. last week), sorted in descending order of headline growth rate. It supports filtering by 23 author categories and can display up to 5 pages (20 items per page, output page by page) to discover public account accounts that are rapidly increasing in volume.
