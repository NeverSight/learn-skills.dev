WeChat public account creator benchmarking tool, which matches accounts based on a three‑layer weighted system (core foundation 40% + operation monetization 35% + data features 25%), recommends benchmark accounts and top accounts. It is used when users need public account recommendations, benchmarking, account start‑up reference, or account placement selection.
