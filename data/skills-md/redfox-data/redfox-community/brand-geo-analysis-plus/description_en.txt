A unified tool for monitoring Chinese internet brand perception. It aggregates three complementary modules: whole‑network hot‑list tracking (hub), in‑depth discussion research over the past 30 days on Xiaohongshu/Douyin/public accounts (cn30), and brand visibility analysis in AI search engines like Doubao/Kimi/DeepSeek (geo). It helps brands, marketers, and content creators comprehensively perceive a brand from three perspectives—topic popularity, authentic reputation, and AI responses. It is used when users need to study Chinese social‑media hotspots, analyze brand sentiment, compare competitor reputation, examine a brand’s performance in AI search, conduct GEO optimization, or track network‑wide trends. Trigger words: brand perception, sentiment monitoring, social‑media research, hot‑list aggregation, AI search visibility, GEO analysis, cross‑platform sentiment, hotspot tracking, brand reputation, brand competitor comparison, social‑media hot lists.
