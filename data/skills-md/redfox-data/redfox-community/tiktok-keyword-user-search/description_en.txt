TikTok account search and following list query tool. Based on Red Fox data, it locates target accounts on TikTok via nickname or handle, allowing you to view profiles such as followers, posts, likes, and verification status; it can also retrieve the list of accounts that a given account follows, analyzing the characteristics of those followed accounts. The two capabilities can be combined—search for an account first, then check its followings. It is suitable for scenarios like searching TikTok users, seeing who a particular account follows, and researching the profiles of follow targets. Trigger words: TikTok search user, TikTok find account, TikTok following list, TikTok who they follow.
