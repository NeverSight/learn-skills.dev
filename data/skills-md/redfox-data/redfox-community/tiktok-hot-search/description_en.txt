TikTok hot search allows you to input a keyword once and simultaneously obtain three types of data: popular videos, related users, and related topics. Based on Honghu data, videos support sorting and filtering by release time and region; users support filtering by follower count and verification status; topics include view counts, usage frequency, and direct links. The output provides ranking tables, viral video breakdowns, influencer recommendations, and topic trend analyses, with support for pagination and HTML report export. It is suitable for TikTok overseas topic research, competitor scanning, and influencer discovery.
