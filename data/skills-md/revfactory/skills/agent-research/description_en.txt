Skill for assembling an agent team to produce a comprehensive research report. It analyzes the research subject and dynamically selects and configures the optimal agents from a pool of experts. (1) When requested with “investigate for me,” “research for me,” or “write a report for me.” (2) When requested with “analyze ~company,” “investigate ~company.” (3) When requested with “investigate ~person,” “analyze ~person,” or “tell me about ~.” (4) When it is necessary to comprehensively understand media reactions, community opinions, competitive environment, etc. (5) When requested with “research with an agent team” or “team research.” (6) When background research is needed based on video scripts, articles, press releases, and the like.
