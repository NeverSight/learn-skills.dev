Framework-independent decision process for instrumenting a GenAI/LLM app (Python or TypeScript, any provider/agent SDK) so it emits OTel GenAI semantic-convention (gen_ai.*) spans queryable in Dynatrace. Four steps: detect, instrument, enrich, validate. Decides which approach a given SDK needs; does not replace the concrete per-framework recipes in dynatrace-oss/dynatrace-ai-agent-instrumentation-examples. Trigger: "instrument this GenAI app", "add tracing to this LLM app", "how do I get gen_ai spans into Dynatrace", "onboard this agent app to AI Observability". Do NOT use for analyzing telemetry from an already-instrumented app (use dt-obs-genai) or for languages other than Python/TypeScript.
