Research, decide, and record a complete design definition for a product: brand concept, principles, tokens, typography, color, motion, components, content rules, ethical engagement patterns, platform adaptations, and a verifiable checklist. Use before a redesign or a new product's first screens, when the current look feels generic or inconsistent, when a previous redesign failed, or when future work needs one documented visual direction. Covers web, mobile, desktop, and terminal (TUI) interfaces. Research and audit come before visual decisions; record the directives in the repository's own documentation.
