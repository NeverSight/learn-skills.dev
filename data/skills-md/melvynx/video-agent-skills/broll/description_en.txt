The creator's own B-roll (their clips, screen recordings, photos) turned into a library any agent can search in one grep - scanned once with ffprobe and ffmpeg (length, orientation, date, GPS, one contact sheet per clip), tagged by cheap Haiku sub-agents (title, description, mood, keywords, creator on screen), then placed full screen in a short by short-editing. Use for /broll setup, /broll add, /broll use, /broll search, or when the user says "my B-roll", "add these clips", "put my B-roll on this short", "find me a sunset shot".
