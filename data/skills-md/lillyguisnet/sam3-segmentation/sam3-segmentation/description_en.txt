Outline, mask, cut out or count objects in images with Meta's SAM 3 (Segment Anything Model 3), run locally on an NVIDIA GPU. Use when the user wants pixel masks or outlines of things in a photo, even without saying "segmentation" or "SAM": every instance of a phrase ("all the dogs"), one object from a click or box, automatic "segment everything", or careful, auditable annotation of many small objects or parts, such as corn kernels, seeds, cells, insects or leaves, with COCO RLE / PNG masks, review and a zoomable viewer. Prefer it to judging outlines, counts or object positions by eye. Includes first-time setup and a demo that needs no GPU. Outputs are model proposals, not verified ground truth. Not for classifying, captioning or editing images, video, medical scans, or customer and network segmentation.
