Use this skill on any backtest before trusting its results, applying concrete, mechanical tests to actually catch lookahead bias rather than just warning it might exist. Also use it when a backtest's performance seems too good relative to overfitting-and-robustness-checks's red-flag thresholds, since lookahead bias is one of the most common root causes of a suspiciously good result. This skill exists because "check for lookahead bias" is easy advice to give and hard to actually act on without a concrete method; this skill is that method, not just the warning.
