Use this skill when building, backtesting, or reviewing a strategy that trades crypto spot or perpetual futures, in addition to the general checks elsewhere in this repo. Also use it when a user is porting a strategy originally designed for traditional markets over to crypto without having considered what's actually different about crypto market structure. This skill exists because crypto markets have specific structural features, funding rates, exchange fragmentation, wash-trading-inflated volume figures, that don't exist in traditional markets and that a strategy or backtest built without accounting for them will get quietly wrong.
