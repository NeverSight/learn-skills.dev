Base44 workflows: trigger-driven automated processes defined as CNCF Serverless Workflow files under base44/workflows/ (scheduled, entity, connector, in-app agent, auth, publish and payment triggers; call, wait and switch steps; jq expressions), with the full authoring guide, plus the legacy automations pipeline for apps that predate workflows. Use for any 'when X happens, do Y' or 'every N hours, do Y'.
