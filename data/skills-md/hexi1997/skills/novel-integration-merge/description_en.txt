Use Novel’s `fix/niudieyi-merge-issue` transitional branch to handle conflicts when merging development or feature branches into dev/test. It is used for “going through the transitional branch to merge dev/test,” “resetting to origin/dev or origin/test then merging the source branch and force‑pushing,” and other complete integration requests, and it also supports reporting only the conflict range. It is not intended for directly publishing dev/test/master or for regular feature‑branch synchronization.
