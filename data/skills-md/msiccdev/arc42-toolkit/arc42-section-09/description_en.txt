Interactively guides the documentation of arc42 Section 9 (Architecture Decisions) using Nygard ADR format extended with toolkit cross-references. Asks about significant decisions, alternatives considered, and consequences before generating structured ADRs. Official arc42 only requires that significant decisions are documented; the Nygard ADR base format and the toolkit cross-reference additions are conventions, not arc42 requirements. Iterates until the user is satisfied.
