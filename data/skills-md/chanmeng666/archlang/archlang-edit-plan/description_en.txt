Use when the user wants to change an existing ArchLang .arch floor plan, e.g. "make the bedroom 1 m wider", "add a bathroom next to the hall", "move the front door to the south wall", "merge the kitchen into the living room". Reads the plan's facts with the archlang CLI, makes the smallest edit that does the job, and re-verifies. Not for writing a plan from a brief (archlang-brief-to-plan).
