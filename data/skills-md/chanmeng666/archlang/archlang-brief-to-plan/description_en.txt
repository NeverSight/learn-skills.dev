Use when the user gives a written brief for a new floor plan (rooms, areas, how rooms connect, a plot size) and wants the drawing, e.g. "design a two-bedroom flat of about 75 m²" or "lay out a small office with four rooms". Writes an ArchLang .arch file from the brief, checks it against the brief with the archlang CLI, and finishes it as a sheet. Not for changing an existing plan (archlang-edit-plan) or for exporting one (archlang-export).
