IP addressing on MikroTik RouterOS 7. Covers IPv4 addresses and the connected routes they create, secondary addresses and unnumbered links, IP pools and used addresses, ARP (static entries, proxy ARP, reply-only, published entries), system-wide IP settings (forwarding, rp-filter, redirects, neighbor table size, ECMP hashing), IPv6 addresses (global, link-local, from a pool, EUI-64), router advertisements and SLAAC, neighbor discovery and RA proxying, and IPv6 settings. Use when assigning or fixing addresses, planning subnets, building IP pools, tuning ARP, enabling IPv6 on a LAN, or when hosts cannot reach the router or each other at Layer 3.
