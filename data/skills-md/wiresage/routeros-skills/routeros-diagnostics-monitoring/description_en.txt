Diagnostics and monitoring on MikroTik RouterOS 7. Covers the system log (topics, logging rules and actions, debug buffers, disk, syslog over UDP, TCP and TLS, CEF, email and script actions), ping and path MTU discovery, traceroute, torch, the packet sniffer with TZSP streaming and pcapng files, interface counters and monitor-traffic, Netwatch probes and scripts, SNMP agent, communities, SNMPv3 and traps, Traffic Flow (NetFlow and IPFIX), graphing, resource and CPU views, profiler, health sensors, watchdog, Detect Internet, bandwidth test and speed test, IP scan, and the traffic generator. Use when a link, host or service misbehaves and you need to find where, when collecting logs or metrics from a router, setting up alerts, or measuring throughput.
