Writing and reviewing the MikroTik RouterOS firewall. Covers the filter, NAT, mangle and raw tables and their chains, packet flow, connection tracking and states, FastTrack, address lists, IPv6 filtering, port forwarding, masquerade versus src-nat, safe rule ordering for the input chain, brute-force and DoS protection recipes, and UPnP, NAT-PMP and Kid Control. Use when adding, ordering, debugging or auditing firewall or NAT rules, publishing a service, blocking addresses, protecting the router or LAN, or when traffic is unexpectedly dropped or not translated.
