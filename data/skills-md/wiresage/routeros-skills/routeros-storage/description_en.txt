Storage on MikroTik RouterOS 7. Covers drive detection and slot names, formatting (ext4, fat32, exfat, btrfs, xfs, wipe), mount points and templates, partitions, swap, tmpfs and ramdisk, mounting images, safe removal, check and repair, S.M.A.R.T., the Storage package (rose-storage) with RAID levels 0, 1, 4, 5, 6 and nested, Btrfs RAID, subvolumes and snapshots, dm-crypt and self-encrypting drives, network storage (SMB server and client, NFS v4, iSCSI, NVMe over TCP, SSHFS, rsync) and the DLNA media server. Use when adding or sharing a USB, SATA or NVMe disk, moving logs or caches off flash, building a RAID, or exporting storage to other devices.
