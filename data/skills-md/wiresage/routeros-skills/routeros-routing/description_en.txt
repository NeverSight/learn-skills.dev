IP routing on MikroTik RouterOS 7. Covers the routing table and route selection, static routes and default gateways, gateway checks and failover by distance, ECMP, blackhole routes, recursive gateways and scope and target-scope, custom routing tables, routing rules and mangle-based policy routing, VRF and VRF-lite, script-style route filters, router ID, BFD, RIP, IS-IS, IGMP proxy and PIM-SM multicast, routing multi-core behaviour, and migrating routing configuration from v6 to v7. Use when adding or debugging routes, steering traffic over several uplinks, isolating networks with VRFs, writing route filters, or when a destination is unreachable although a route exists.
