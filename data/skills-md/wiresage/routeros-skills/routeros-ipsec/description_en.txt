Configuring IPsec on MikroTik RouterOS 7. Covers IKE phases, profiles, proposals, peers, identities, policies and templates, mode-config, IKEv1 and IKEv2, pre-shared keys, certificates and EAP, site-to-site tunnels, GRE over IPsec, road warrior setups with Windows, macOS, iOS, Android and strongSwan clients, L2TP over IPsec, NAT and FastTrack bypass, hardware acceleration, and reading IPsec logs. Use when building, debugging or hardening an IPsec tunnel or remote-access server, when phase 1 or phase 2 fails, or when tunnel traffic is unencrypted, dropped or very slow.
