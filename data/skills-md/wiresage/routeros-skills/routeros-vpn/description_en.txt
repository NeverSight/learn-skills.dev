Building VPNs and tunnels on MikroTik RouterOS 7. Covers WireGuard (site to site, road warrior, client configuration export, multi-WAN), L2TP, SSTP, OpenVPN and PPTP servers and clients with PPP secrets and profiles, PPPoE, Layer 2 tunnels (EoIP, L2TP-Ether), GRE, IPIP, 6to4 and ZeroTier, with the firewall, routing and MTU steps each one needs. Use when creating or debugging a site-to-site or remote-access tunnel, choosing a VPN protocol, issuing client configurations, or when a tunnel is up but no traffic passes.
