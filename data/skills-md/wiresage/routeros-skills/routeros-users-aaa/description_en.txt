Authentication, authorization and accounting on MikroTik RouterOS. Covers router administrator accounts, groups and policies, password rules and expiry, SSH public keys, inactivity timeouts, RADIUS client setup (including RadSec) for management logins, PPP, HotSpot and wireless, PPP profiles and secrets, PPP AAA and 802.1X (Dot1X) authenticator and supplicant. Use when creating or hardening admin accounts, giving someone limited rights, moving logins to RADIUS, setting up PPP users for VPN servers, or configuring port-based access control.
