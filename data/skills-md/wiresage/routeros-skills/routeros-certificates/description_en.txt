Managing X.509 certificates on MikroTik RouterOS 7. Covers certificate templates, creating a private CA and signing server and client certificates, importing and exporting PEM, DER and PKCS12, Let's Encrypt and other ACME certificates, SCEP, revocation lists, key usage and trust stores, and assigning a certificate to a service (HTTPS, SSTP, OpenVPN, IPsec, API-SSL). Use when a service needs a certificate, when issuing or renewing one, when a browser, VPN client or fetch complains about trust, or when exporting keys safely.
