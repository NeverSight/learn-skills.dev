Ethernet, SFP and wired interfaces on MikroTik RouterOS 7. Covers link speed, auto-negotiation and advertised modes, forced mode for DAC cables and optical modules that fail auto-initialization, FEC, flow control, PoE-out switches on ports, combo ports, cable test, interface statistics and monitoring, MTU, L2MTU and jumbo frames, interface lists (LAN, WAN and custom lists), loop protection on ports, and PWR-Line. Use when a link does not come up or runs at the wrong speed, when setting MTU or jumbo frames, when grouping interfaces in lists for firewall or bridge rules, or when reading port counters and optical module data.
