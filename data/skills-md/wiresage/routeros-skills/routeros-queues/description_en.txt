Bandwidth limiting and quality of service on MikroTik RouterOS. Covers simple queues, queue trees, the HTB model (limit-at, max-limit, priority, parent), queue types (PFIFO, BFIFO, SFQ, RED, PCQ, CoDel, FQ-CoDel, CAKE), bursts, queue size, interface queues, and how queues interact with FastTrack and mangle packet marks. Use when limiting or guaranteeing bandwidth for hosts or networks, sharing a link fairly, fighting bufferbloat, prioritising traffic, or when a queue shows no traffic or limits the wrong direction.
