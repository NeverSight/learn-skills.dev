Troubleshooting and support workflow for MikroTik RouterOS 7. Covers a symptom-to-skill routing table, collecting evidence safely (read-only triage bundle script, supout.rif and autosupout.rif), recovery when access is lost (reset button sequences, backup bootloader, Netinstall, lost password), reboot loops, unclassified CPU load, default passwords and what to send to MikroTik support. Use when something on a MikroTik router is broken and the cause is unknown, when a device is unreachable or boot-looping, when a password is lost, or when preparing a support request.
