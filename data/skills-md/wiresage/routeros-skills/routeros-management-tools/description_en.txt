Ways to reach and manage MikroTik RouterOS devices. Covers WinBox (v4 and legacy v3), WebFig and its HTTPS certificate, the SSH server and client (host keys, key login, ssh-exec in scripts, known hosts), the serial console, MAC Telnet and the MAC server, RoMON overlay management, Quick Set, the MikroTik mobile app, TR-069, CMR fleet management, The Dude, FlashFig and the branding package. Use when choosing or securing a management path, recovering access to a router with a broken IP configuration, running commands on another device from a script, managing a fleet, or enabling HTTPS for the web interface.
