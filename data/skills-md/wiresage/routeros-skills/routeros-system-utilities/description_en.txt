Everyday system features of MikroTik RouterOS 7. Covers the system clock, time zone and NTP client and server, the scheduler (nightly backups, startup scripts), the /ip/service management listeners and what the router listens on, device mode restrictions and their physical confirmation, files and the flash directory, fetch (downloads, uploads, webhooks, certificate checks), email through SMTP, system identity and login note, neighbor discovery (MNDP, CDP, LLDP), TFTP, Wake on LAN, UPS monitoring, IP packing, partitions and PTP. Use when time or time zone is wrong, scheduling a task, restricting or auditing management services, a feature is blocked by device mode, moving files or calling web endpoints from a router, or sending alerts by email.
