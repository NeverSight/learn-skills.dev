Configuring and troubleshooting OSPF (v2 for IPv4, v3 for IPv6) on MikroTik RouterOS 7. Covers instances, areas and area types (standard, stub, totally stubby, NSSA), interface templates, network types (broadcast, point-to-point, NBMA, point-to-multipoint), cost, passive interfaces, authentication, redistribution through routing filters, default route origination, BFD, virtual links, route summarization, and neighbours stuck in Init or flapping. Use when bringing up OSPF between routers, redistributing routes, designing areas, migrating OSPF from v6, or when adjacencies or routes are missing.
