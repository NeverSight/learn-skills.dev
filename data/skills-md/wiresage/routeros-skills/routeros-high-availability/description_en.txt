High availability and redundancy on MikroTik RouterOS 7. Covers VRRP (virtual IP, IPv4 and IPv6, priority, preemption, authentication, connection tracking sync, group authority for LAN and WAN pairs), bonding (802.3ad LACP, balance-xor, active-backup and other modes, ARP and MII link monitoring, transmit hash policy, hardware offload), MLAG across two switches with ICCP, WAN failover with check-gateway and recursive routes, per-connection load balancing with PCC, ECMP and Nth. Use when two routers must share a gateway address, when a link or uplink must fail over automatically, when aggregating ports, or when spreading connections over several WANs.
