Build a strategy-driven time-slot generator with availability in a Next.js App Router app: daily ranges, Nth-weekday-of-month rules, one-off slots and whole-day slots, authored in local wall-clock time and stored as UTC instants, with seats, bookings, blackouts, host busy time, shared resources and working hours subtracted, a listing route and a booking route that re-checks capacity in the write transaction. Use when: (1) an app needs appointment, class, rental or reservation slots generated from rules rather than stored one by one, (2) slot code gives wrong days around DST, wrong weekdays far from UTC, a week view with one day, or times that change with the server's time zone, (3) strategies must be validated, pinned to a zone, or moved off wall-clock-as-UTC storage, (4) two guests can book the last seat at once, (5) the user mentions: time slots, slot generator, availability, booking slots, opening hours, slot strategy, RANGE, PERIODICITY, DAY_RANGE, ONCE, slotInterval, slotDuration, slotsLimit, daysLimit, first Monday of the month, last Friday, overnight slots, lead time, booking window, timeOffset, IANA time zone, DST, fromZonedTime, storageVersion, slot ID, md5, overbooking, capacity check, book_slots, slot-no-longer-available, slot-has-not-enough-capacity. Carries a dependency-free engine on Intl with a 23-test suite run under four process time zones, slot IDs that stay stable for existing bookings, one availability map for listing and booking, and a capacity re-check inside the booking transaction on both backends. Next.js App Router route handlers on the Node.js runtime; Firestore or Supabase/Postgres behind the SlotStore seam. Not a calendar UI, not RRULE or iCalendar recurrence, not payments or booking-flow screens.
