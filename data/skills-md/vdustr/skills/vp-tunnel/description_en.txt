Use when something outside this machine must reach a local service: a deployed or public page whose requests to localhost, a local proxy, or a local WebSocket hang, spin, or fail with net::ERR_BLOCKED_BY_CLIENT (Chrome Local Network Access); a webhook; an OAuth provider that rejects localhost redirects; a phone off the LAN; a remote or cloud browser; or a teammate or client viewing a demo. Also use when asked to expose or share localhost, get a public URL, or compare ngrok, cloudflared, trycloudflare, tmole, or their alternatives. Decides whether a tunnel is needed, gates the exposure, and chooses the tool. Boundary: not for local clients reaching localhost, starting dev servers, or running a tunnel as production infrastructure; each tool's docs cover its commands, and vp-long-running-processes owns the process.
