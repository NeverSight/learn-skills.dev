Use when writing or debugging tokio task and future mechanics, including cancel safety in tokio::select!, timeout, JoinSet, or FuturesUnordered; task ownership and shutdown with JoinHandle, TaskTracker, or CancellationToken; blocking work via spawn_blocking, block_in_place, or block_on from a foreign thread; Waker::noop polling from a synchronous loop; and Send bounds on an async closure, AsyncFn, or async fn in traits.
