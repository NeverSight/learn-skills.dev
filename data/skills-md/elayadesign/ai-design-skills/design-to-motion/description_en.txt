Turns a static design (a Figma frame, dashboard, card set, app screen, or a UI on a photo background) into a pixel exact HTML replica that loads in from an empty state, then has every visual act out what it does (a chart being read, a list receiving items, a prompt being typed) in a seamless loop, and exports an MP4 for X, a website GIF, and an 800×500 GIF. Use this skill whenever the user asks to animate a design, a Figma link, a screenshot, UI cards, a dashboard, or a hero image, to make a loading or appear animation, a showcase loop, a motion mockup, or a video or GIF of a design, even when they only say "animate this". Also use it on landing pages and websites for bento and feature cards that pair text with a visual, and for product or dashboard mockups. Always consult it before exporting layers, choosing what moves, building the intro, writing the visual story loops, or encoding video and GIFs.
