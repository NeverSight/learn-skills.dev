Text to speech with Cartesia (Sonic 3.6, the latest model) from the `cartesia` command: list and download voice samples, audition a script's first lines in a few voices, and read a whole script into one WAV with Cartesia's own word timestamps (OUT.words.json, the layout `transcribe` uses) and each line's start and end. Always asks the user first: the tool, the voice, the speed and the emotion are the user's choice. Use for any TTS, voiceover, scratch read, voice sample or "read this out", and before planning a video so its shots are timed from a real read.
