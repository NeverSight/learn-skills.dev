The house editing style for every short-form ad edited in FilmCraft, whatever is on screen. A talking head with inserts, 150 Flow B-roll shots under a voiceover, a sung ad, a stills slideshow. It holds the principles clients approved across Aleo, Mysa and CloudAlign (a new picture only on a caption start or a cut, cuts on the first word, something new every 2–4 s, every still moving, dead air cut to a breath, captions on the words, music well under the voice, hooks 2–3 cloned from the user's hook 1), the user's working rules (never move their clips, back up, no exports unless asked), the checks before every hand-back, recipes for change requests, and a new-machine checklist. Use for any FilmCraft edit, fix, re-cut, caption pass or export, for a client's edit feedback (Loom, notes), or when starting the edit of a new video. Then load the sub-skill for the format, talking-head-edit (a speaker carries the script with full-screen inserts) or voiceover-edit (shots or stills under a voiceover or song), plus filmcraft and captions-filmcraft.
