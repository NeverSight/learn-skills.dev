Speech to text with word timestamps for any audio or video file. Fully local: Parakeet on the Intel GPU first, then Parakeet on the CPU (two runtimes), then faster-whisper. Writes a transcript, a words.json and optionally an SRT. Use when the user wants a transcript, speech-to-text, word timestamps, subtitles from audio, "what does he say", to time a voiceover, or to find swears, bleeps or mutes. The captions, video-plan and song-ad scripts use its helper.
