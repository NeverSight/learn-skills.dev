Guidance for step-by-step forms and wizard workflow entry based on Ant Design Vue Steps and multiple YFormily instances, covering independent validation for each step, persistent data retention across steps, final confirmation preview, and aggregated submission closure; use it when the business requires stepwise entry, phased processes, or multi-step wizards. For single-page regular forms, use formily-foundation; for simple field linkage, use formily-linkage-effects.
