In Volcano Engine or BytePlus Continuous Delivery (CP), manage the full CI/CD lifecycle in the production environment, including workspaces, CI pipelines and execution logs, triggers, custom steps, service connections, deployment resources, and CD hosted applications. Supports the production regions cn-beijing and ap-southeast-1. Use when the user explicitly requests operations on online, production, or prod CP CI/CD, pipelines, builds, deployments, workspaces, Webhooks, triggers, custom steps, service connections, deployment resources, hosted applications, runtime status, or logs. Only supports the production environment; must not be used for pre, pre-release, or testing environments.
