Converts audio objects stored in Volcengine TOS into PCM-oriented WAV output through TOS data-process async jobs. Use this skill when the user needs to convert audio to PCM or WAV, resample audio to 16k, convert to mono, change sample format, normalize audio for ASR pipelines, or prepare TOS audio files for speech recognition — even if they don't explicitly mention 'PCM' or 'audio conversion'.
