Use this skill when the user wants a comprehensive quality check of a paper before submission. Triggers include "review my paper before I submit", "is this paper ready to submit", "check my paper for issues", "pre-submission check", "find weaknesses in my paper", or "what would a reviewer complain about". Acts as an honest internal reviewer covering completeness, logical consistency, experimental rigour, and writing quality. For venue-specific criteria after this general check, use venue-reviewer.
