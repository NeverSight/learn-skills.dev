Use this skill when the user is generating, evaluating, or auditing synthetic medical images using generative models. Triggers include "evaluate my diffusion model for chest X-rays", "check if my model memorises training data", "generate counterfactual images", "concept erasure in my generative model", "benchmark my synthetic data quality", "does my synthetic data preserve clinical realism", or "evaluate generated MRI/X-ray quality". Handles generation pipeline design, clinical realism evaluation, memorisation detection, counterfactual generation, and bias in synthetic data. Do not use for fairness analysis of discriminative classifiers — use fairness-medical-imaging instead.
