Use this skill when the user wants to draft, structure, or improve an academic paper or a specific section of one. Triggers include "write my paper", "help me write the introduction", "structure my methods section", "draft my abstract", "improve my related work", "write up my results", "help me write contributions", or "make this section clearer". Handles full drafts and section-by-section writing for conference and journal formats. Do not use for rebuttal writing — use paper-rebuttal-helper; do not use for literature synthesis alone — use literature-review.
