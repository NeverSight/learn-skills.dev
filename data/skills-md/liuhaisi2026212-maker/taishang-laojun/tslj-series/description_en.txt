Recipe: Series planning. Help the user design a series that can be filmed episode by episode: decide what stays constant each episode, what changes, where the material comes from, how long it can sustain, what kind of audience it will attract, how to shoot the first episode, and after how many episodes to look for signals to decide whether to continue or switch. Use this when the user wants to keep updating but doesn't know what to film, the account’s content is all over the place, they stop after a few posts, or they want to create a fixed segment that viewers can watch just once and be satisfied.
