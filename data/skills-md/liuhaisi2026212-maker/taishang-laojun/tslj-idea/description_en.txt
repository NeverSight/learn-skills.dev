Watch the fire: topic selection judgment. Based on the user’s experiences, scenarios, references, or trending topics, determine what can be filmed, provide a few personalized, reason‑based directions, and recommend one. It also serves to evaluate whether an existing topic is worth filming and what audience it will attract. Use it when the user doesn’t know what to film, has a trending topic or reference and wants to find an entry point, or wants to know if a particular topic will work.
