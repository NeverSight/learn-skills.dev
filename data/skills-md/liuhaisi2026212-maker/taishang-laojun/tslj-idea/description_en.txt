Kan Huo: Topic selection judgment. Determine what can be filmed based on the user’s experience, scenario, references, or hot topics, offering a few personalized, reason‑based directions and recommending one; it also serves to assess whether an existing topic is worth filming and what kind of audience it will attract. Use this when the user doesn’t know what to film, has a hot topic or reference and wants to find an entry point, or wants to know if a particular topic is viable.
