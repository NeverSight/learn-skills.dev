Material selection: find benchmarks, dissect hit products. The ingredient stage of the alchemy furnace: identify benchmarks based on the user's criteria, break down a hit product to understand why it succeeded, then use three checkpoints—repetition, customers, and substitution—to assess whether it would still work in the user's hands, decide which mechanism to borrow, what to replace, and which of the user's own elements to capture. It is also used to determine which AI tool to use. This approach applies when a user comes across a hit product they want to make their own, is looking for benchmarks, and doesn't know whom to learn from.
