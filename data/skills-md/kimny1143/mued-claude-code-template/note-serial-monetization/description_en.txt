Skill for designing the paid/free segmentation strategy for note serial articles. Handles the placement of free and paid episodes throughout the series, the design of the paid line within a single article, pricing, and magazine composition. Usage timing: (1) when you want to write a paid article on note (2) design of monetization for a serial (3) positioning the paid line (4) considering magazine structure (5) consulting on pricing. Trigger examples: “I want to make it paid on note,” “Serial monetization design,” “From where should it be paid?” “I want to earn on note,” “How to structure the magazine,” “Paid line,” “Pricing.” Can be used together with the ai-interview-article skill.
