Build interactive customer journey maps as standalone HTML files with D3.js. Use this skill whenever the user wants to create a journey map, experience map, service blueprint, or customer experience visualization showing phases, touchpoints, emotions, pain points, and opportunities. Also trigger when the user says 'journey map', 'customer journey', 'experience map', 'CX map', 'service blueprint', 'touchpoint map', or asks to visualize a user's end-to-end experience across stages. This is different from flow diagrams — journey maps focus on the emotional and experiential layer of a user's relationship with a product, not just the screen-by-screen navigation.
