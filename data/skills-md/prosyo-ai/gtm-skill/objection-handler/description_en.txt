Use this skill when a prospect raises an objection in a reply, on a call or in a deal: "how do I respond to 'too expensive'", "handle this objection", "build an objection handling guide", "they said they're happy with their current vendor". Diagnoses what the objection really means, picks the response pattern (clarify, reframe, prove, defer or walk away), writes responses for email, call and LinkedIn, and builds a reusable objection library with proof for each.
