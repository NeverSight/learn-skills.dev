Use this skill when you want more accounts like your best customers: "find companies like our top 10", "build a lookalike list", "who else looks like Acme". Profiles your best customers on the traits that predicted the win, searches for companies that share them, scores each match on similarity and timing, and explains why each one made the list. Produces a ranked lookalike account list with match reasons and a suggested reference customer for each, ready for outbound or ABM.
