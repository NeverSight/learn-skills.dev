Use this skill when you need sales voicemails and LinkedIn voice notes that get remembered: "write a voicemail script", "what should I say in a voice note", "voicemail for a cold call follow-up". Writes 15-25 second scripts that tie to an email or message, give one reason to listen, and tell the prospect exactly what happens next. Includes versions for cold calls, post-email follow-ups, referrals, no-shows and LinkedIn voice notes.
