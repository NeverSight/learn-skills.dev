Use this skill when you want to test what works in outbound instead of guessing: "A/B test my cold emails", "design an outbound experiment", "which variant won", "testing plan for our sequences". Picks the variable worth testing (list, angle, offer, first line, CTA, channel, timing), writes the hypothesis, splits the audience fairly, sets the sample size and decision rule, and reads the result honestly, including when it is a tie. Produces a test plan, a log entry and the decision.
