Use this for Tier 1 accounts where a short video can show, not tell. A personalised video works when the first seconds prove it was made for this person; it fails when it is a generic demo with their name in the title.

## Inputs

- Research on the person and account.
- What to show on screen: their website, a post, a job listing, a dashboard example, a before and after.
- Offer and one proof point.

## The play

### 1. Plan the first five seconds
Open with their name and something of theirs on screen. The thumbnail shows it too: their homepage, their post, a whiteboard with their name. The first five seconds decide whether they keep watching.

### 2. Script it in four beats (45-75 seconds)
| Beat | Seconds | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0-8 | "Hi Maya, made this for you. This is Lumen's careers page..." |
| Observation | 8-25 | What you noticed and why it matters for them |
| Insight | 25-50 | One idea or a peer example, shown on screen |
| Ask | 50-65 | Easy next step, said and repeated in the email |

### 3. Write the carrier email
Short, with the thumbnail as an image linking to the video (a GIF or image of the first frame works; embedded players do not render in most inboxes). Subject: "made this for you, Maya" or the topic.

### 4. Record fast, not perfect
One or two takes. Natural beats polished. Look at the camera during the hook and the ask.

## Example script

> [Screen: Lumen careers page, four SDR listings highlighted]
>
> "Hi Dana, I made this quick video for you. This is your careers page: four SDR roles, all saying outbound is being built from scratch.
>
> [Screen: simple timeline of a new SDR's first month]
>
> Here's what usually happens to new SDR teams: weeks one to four go to building lists and researching accounts, so first meetings land in week six.
>
> [Screen: a peer's anonymised before and after]
>
> Northwind changed one thing: each rep got a researched list on day one. First meetings moved to week two.
>
> If that's useful before your new hires start, reply 'yes' and I'll send you how they set it up. Thanks Dana."

## What good looks like

- Their world is on screen in the first frame.
- Under 75 seconds; one idea.
- The ask is a one-word reply, not a meeting.
- The common mistake is a two-minute product demo with "Hi <name>" stitched on the front.

## Rules

- MUST show something specific to the prospect in the first seconds.
- MUST keep videos under 90 seconds for cold outreach.
- NEVER show other customers' confidential data on screen; anonymise or use approved examples.

## Example prompts

- "Write a 60-second video script for the CRO at Fabrikam. They just opened an EMEA office."
- "Turn this cold email into a video script with screen directions."
- "Write a video recap to send after today's discovery call."
