Use this skill when you want to use short personalised videos in outbound: "write a prospecting video script", "personalised video for this account", "how do I use video in my sequence". Writes 45-75 second scripts that open on the prospect's own world (their site, their post, their listing), show one insight, and end with an easy ask, plus the thumbnail idea, subject line and the email that carries the video. Covers first touches, follow-ups and post-meeting recaps.
