Use this skill when you need a B2B customer case study or success story that sales can use: "write a case study", "turn this customer interview into a story", "customer success story for our website", "one-pager for sales". Plans the interview, structures the story (before, trigger, decision, rollout, results, what's next), writes it with real numbers and quotes, and produces versions for the website, a sales one-pager, a cold email paragraph and a LinkedIn post. Every claim is approved by the customer.
