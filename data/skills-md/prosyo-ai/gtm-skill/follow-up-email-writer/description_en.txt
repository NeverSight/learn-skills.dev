Use this skill when you need to follow up after no reply, after a meeting, after a proposal or after a prospect went quiet: "write a follow-up email", "they ghosted me", "follow up after the demo". Diagnoses the situation, picks the right follow-up type (add value, new angle, recap, nudge on a decision, re-engage), and writes a short follow-up that gives the reader a reason to respond without guilt-tripping or saying "just checking in".
