Use this skill when you need to turn a revenue or pipeline target into outbound activity: "how many emails to hit quota", "reverse funnel calculator", "how many SDRs do we need", "what activity does our pipeline target require". Works backwards from revenue through win rate, deal size, opportunity rate, meeting rate and reply rate to contacts, touches and daily activity per rep, shows the levers that change the answer most, and checks it against team capacity and market size.
