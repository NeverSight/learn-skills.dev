Use this skill when you need a B2B cold call script or talk track: "write a cold call script", "what do I say when they pick up", "help me prep cold calls for this list", "handle objections on the phone". Builds an opener that earns 30 seconds, a problem statement in the buyer's words, two or three discovery questions, a meeting ask and the responses to the most common brush-offs. Written to be said out loud, with a one-page version for the call and a practice drill.
