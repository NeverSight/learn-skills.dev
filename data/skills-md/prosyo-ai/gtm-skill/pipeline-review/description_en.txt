Use this skill when a manager or founder needs to review the sales pipeline and forecast: "review my pipeline", "prep for the forecast call", "which deals are at risk", "is our quarter going to land". Checks pipeline coverage, stage health, deal aging, slipped close dates, missing next steps and single-threaded deals, scores every deal's risk with evidence, and produces a forecast (commit, best case, upside) with the actions that change the outcome this week.
