Use this skill when outbound replies need triage and answers: "sort these replies", "draft responses to my inbox", "what do I say to this reply", "handle replies from my campaign". Classifies each reply (interested, question, objection, referral, not now, not interested, out of office, wrong person, unsubscribe), sets priority and the next action, drafts a response for each that moves toward a meeting or closes cleanly, and updates what the CRM needs to know. Drafts only: a human approves before anything is sent.
