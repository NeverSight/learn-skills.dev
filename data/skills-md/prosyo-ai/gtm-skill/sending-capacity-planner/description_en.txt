Use this skill when you need to know how many mailboxes, domains and LinkedIn accounts a meeting target requires, or how much a team can safely send: "how many inboxes do I need", "plan our sending capacity", "can we hit 40 meetings a month". Works backwards from the meeting goal through reply and conversion rates to contacts, touches and daily sends, then sizes email and LinkedIn infrastructure inside safe limits, with cost, ramp time and the bottleneck to fix first.
