Use this skill when you need a written sales playbook a team can actually follow: "build our sales playbook", "document how we sell", "playbook for new reps", "our reps all sell differently". Captures the ICP, personas, the stages and exit criteria, the plays for each stage, messaging, objection answers, tools and the metrics that matter, from what top performers already do. Produces a structured playbook outline with the first version of every section, ready for the team to refine.
