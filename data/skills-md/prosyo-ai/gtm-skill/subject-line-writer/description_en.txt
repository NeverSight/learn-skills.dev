Use this skill when you need cold email subject lines that get opened and do not look like marketing: "write subject lines for this email", "my open rates are low", "A/B test subject lines". Writes short, lowercase, internal-looking subject lines matched to the email's angle, explains the pattern behind each, flags spam and clickbait risks, and sets up a fair subject-line test. For cold outreach, follow-ups and re-engagement emails.
