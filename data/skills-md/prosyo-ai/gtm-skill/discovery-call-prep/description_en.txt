Use this skill when you have a discovery or first sales call coming up and want to run it well: "prep me for this discovery call", "what questions should I ask", "build a discovery call agenda", "call plan for tomorrow". Combines account and person research with a call plan: hypothesis of their problem, agenda, discovery questions ordered from situation to impact to decision, qualification checks, likely objections and the next step to ask for. Produces a one-page call plan and a post-call notes template.
