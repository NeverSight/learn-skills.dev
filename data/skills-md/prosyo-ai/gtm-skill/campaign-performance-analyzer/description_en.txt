Use this skill when you need to understand how outbound campaigns are performing and what to change: "analyse my campaign results", "why is this sequence underperforming", "compare my campaigns", "weekly outbound report". Builds the funnel from contacts to meetings for each campaign, step, segment, sender and channel, benchmarks it, finds the stage where performance breaks, separates list, deliverability, copy and follow-up problems, and recommends the next test. Produces a short report a team can act on.
