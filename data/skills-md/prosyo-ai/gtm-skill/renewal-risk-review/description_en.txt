Use this skill when renewals are coming up and you need to know which customers might not renew: "renewal risk review", "which accounts will churn", "prepare for renewals next quarter", "customer health check before renewal". Scores each upcoming renewal on usage, outcomes, relationships, support and commercial signals, flags at-risk accounts with evidence, and writes a save plan and the value-review outreach for each. Produces a renewal risk table and a 90-day action plan.
