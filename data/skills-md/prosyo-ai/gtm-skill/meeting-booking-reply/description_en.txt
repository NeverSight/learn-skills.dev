Use this skill when a prospect shows interest and you need to turn it into a booked, confirmed meeting: "they said yes, what do I send", "book the meeting", "write the meeting confirmation", "reduce back and forth scheduling". Writes the booking reply with specific times or a link, the calendar invite title and agenda, the confirmation and the day-before reminder, so the meeting gets booked fast and actually happens. Handles time zones, assistants and multiple attendees.
