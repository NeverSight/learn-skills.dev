Use this skill when you need an outbound cadence that combines email, LinkedIn, phone and video so each touch builds on the last: "build a multichannel sequence", "combine email and LinkedIn outreach", "plan a 3-week cadence for Tier 1 accounts". Chooses channels by persona and tier, orders and spaces the touches, writes the connecting thread across channels, sets daily capacity and exit rules, and produces a day-by-day cadence ready to load into any sequencing tool.
