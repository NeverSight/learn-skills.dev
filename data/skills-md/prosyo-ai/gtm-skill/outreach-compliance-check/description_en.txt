Use this skill when you need to check that cold email or LinkedIn outreach follows privacy and anti-spam rules before it goes out: "is this campaign GDPR compliant", "CAN-SPAM checklist", "can we email people in the EU", "check our cold outreach for compliance". Checks the audience, lawful basis, data source, sender identity, opt-out, frequency and record keeping by region, flags what must change, and produces a pass/fix report. A practical checklist, not legal advice.
