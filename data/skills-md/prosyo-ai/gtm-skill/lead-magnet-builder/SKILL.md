Use this when you need a reason for buyers to raise their hand. The best lead magnets solve one small, urgent part of the buyer's problem and lead naturally to the bigger part your product solves.

## Inputs

- Persona, their problem and the job they are trying to do.
- The product and where it fits in that job.
- Where the magnet will be promoted (LinkedIn, ads, SEO, outbound).

## The play

### 1. Pick the format by intent
| Format | Works when | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Template | The buyer has to produce something | "Mutual action plan template" |
| Checklist | The buyer fears missing something | "Cold email deliverability checklist" |
| Calculator or free tool | The buyer needs a number or a quick result | "Sending capacity calculator" |
| Benchmark report | The buyer wants to compare themselves | "Outbound reply rates by industry" |
| Swipe file | The buyer wants examples to copy | "50 cold emails that got replies" |
| Mini course | The buyer needs a skill, over days | "5-day LinkedIn outreach course" |

### 2. Pass the bridge test
Using the magnet should make the buyer more likely to need your product. A deliverability checklist for a sending tool passes; a generic productivity ebook does not.

### 3. Draft it
Specific and usable in under 30 minutes. One problem, solved well. Include real examples and numbers.

### 4. Write the landing page
Headline with the outcome, three bullets of what is inside, a preview image or sample, a short form (email, and maybe company), and a thank-you page with a useful next step.

### 5. Write the follow-up
Three to five emails: deliver the asset, a tip for using it, a related story, then a soft invitation to the product. Route leads by fit: ICP leads with high-intent actions go to sales; the rest stay in nurture.

### 6. Decide gating
Gate when the asset is substantial and the audience is qualified; leave ungated (and capture with a softer ask) when reach and SEO matter more. Many teams gate the editable version and publish the preview openly.

## What good looks like

- Downloaders are mostly ICP buyers.
- The magnet is used, not just downloaded: templates get filled in, calculators get run.
- The follow-up gets replies, not only unsubscribes.
- The common mistake is a 40-page ebook nobody finishes, gated behind a 9-field form.

## Rules

- MUST make the asset genuinely useful on its own.
- MUST tell people what they will receive and how often they will hear from you, and honour unsubscribes.
- NEVER pass downloaders to sales as "hot leads" without fit and intent.
- NEVER use other companies' data or content without permission.

## Example prompts

- "Give me 10 lead magnet ideas for selling to heads of RevOps, ranked."
- "Build a 'cold email deliverability checklist' lead magnet with landing page and follow-ups."
- "Turn our benchmark data into a gated report."
