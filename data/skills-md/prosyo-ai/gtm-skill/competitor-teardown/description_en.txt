Use this skill when you need to understand a competitor well enough to beat them: a deal is going head to head, a new competitor appeared, positioning needs a refresh, or reps keep losing on the same objection. Tears down the competitor's positioning, ideal customer, pricing and packaging, product strengths and gaps, customer complaints, sales motion and recent moves, and produces a teardown with where to compete, where to avoid, and the talk track that wins. Built from public sources and your own win-loss notes.
