Use this skill when a contact changes jobs: a champion or happy user joins a new company, a new leader starts at a target account, or a key contact leaves a customer. Runs the three job-change plays: follow the champion to their new company, welcome the new leader at a target account, and protect the customer account they left. Produces the message for each with timing, who to contact and what never to say. One of the highest-converting outbound plays in B2B.
