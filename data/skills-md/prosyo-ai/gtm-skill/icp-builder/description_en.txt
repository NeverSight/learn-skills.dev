Use this skill when you need to define or sharpen an ideal customer profile (ICP) for B2B sales: starting outbound, the reply rate is low, the pipeline is full of bad-fit deals, or leadership asks who we should really sell to. Analyses closed-won and closed-lost deals, finds the firmographic, technographic and situational traits that predict a win, and produces a one-page ICP with must-haves, nice-to-haves, disqualifiers and list-building filters. Works for founders with five customers and for teams with hundreds.
