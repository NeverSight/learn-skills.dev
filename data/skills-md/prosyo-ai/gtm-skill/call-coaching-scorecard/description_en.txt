Use this skill when a manager or rep wants feedback on sales calls: "score this call", "review my discovery call transcript", "coaching feedback for my SDR", "build a call scorecard". Scores a call transcript or notes on opening, discovery, listening, value, objection handling and next step, quotes the moments that mattered, picks the one skill to work on next, and gives a concrete drill. Also builds a team scorecard so coaching is consistent across managers.
