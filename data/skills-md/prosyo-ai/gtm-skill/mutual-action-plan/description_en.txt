Use this skill when a B2B deal needs a shared plan to get from evaluation to signature and go-live: "build a mutual action plan", "close plan for this deal", "what are the steps to sign", "deal keeps slipping". Works backwards from the buyer's go-live date, lists every step on both sides (evaluation, security, legal, procurement, approvals, onboarding) with owners and dates, flags the critical path, and writes the email that introduces the plan to the champion as a tool to help them, not to pressure them.
