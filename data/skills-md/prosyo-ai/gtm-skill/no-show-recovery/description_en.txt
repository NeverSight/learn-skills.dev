Use this skill when a prospect misses a booked meeting or keeps rescheduling: "they didn't show up", "no-show follow-up email", "how to rebook a no-show", "reduce no-shows". Writes the same-day no-show sequence (gracious message, rebooking options, value touch, close the loop), sets the timing across email, phone and LinkedIn, and diagnoses why no-shows happen so the booking process can be fixed.
