Use this skill when you set up new domains or mailboxes for cold email and need a warm-up plan: "how do I warm up a new domain", "warm-up schedule for new inboxes", "how fast can I ramp sending". Plans domain purchase and setup, a week-by-week warm-up and ramp schedule per mailbox, the health checks that gate each step up, and what to do when a mailbox shows trouble. Produces a ramp calendar and capacity forecast for the whole sending infrastructure.
