Use this skill when a seller, founder or SDR needs a LinkedIn profile that converts prospects instead of reading like a resume: "optimise my LinkedIn profile", "rewrite my headline", "make my profile buyer-focused", "why don't prospects accept my requests". Rewrites the headline, About section, featured items, banner message and experience entries around the buyer's problem and proof, and checks the profile from a prospect's point of view. Built for social selling and outbound.
