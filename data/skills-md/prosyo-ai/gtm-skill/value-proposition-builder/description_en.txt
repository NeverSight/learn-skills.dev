Use this skill when you need a sharp B2B value proposition: "write our value prop", "our messaging is generic", "how do we describe what we do", "homepage headline", "one-liner for outbound". Builds the value proposition from the customer's problem, the alternatives they use today and the outcome only you deliver, tests it against competitors and real buyer language, and outputs a one-liner, a short paragraph, three supporting pillars with proof, and versions for the homepage, cold email and sales calls.
