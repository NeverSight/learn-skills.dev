Use this skill when you need to write a B2B cold email that gets replies: "write a cold email to a VP of Sales", "draft outreach for this prospect", "my cold emails get no replies". Builds the email from the prospect's situation, one pain, one proof point and one low-friction ask, keeps it under 100 words, and returns three variants with different angles plus subject lines. Works for one-to-one emails and for segment templates with personalization slots.
