Use this skill when you need a B2B lead list for outbound: "build me a list of 500 heads of operations at logistics companies", "find leads for this campaign", "who should we target next". Turns an ICP into precise account and contact filters, builds the list in layers, verifies contacts, removes customers, open deals and opt-outs, and segments the result so each segment can get its own message. Produces a clean, segmented, campaign-ready list with a coverage report.
