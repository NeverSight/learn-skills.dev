Use this skill when you track buying signals and need to decide which ones deserve outreach today: "which accounts are in market", "prioritise these signals", "build a signal-based selling system". Catalogues the signals that matter for your offer (hiring, funding, leadership changes, intent, website visits, engagement, tech changes), scores them on strength and freshness, stacks multiple signals per account, and outputs a daily ranked action list with the play to run for each. The core of signal-based outbound.
