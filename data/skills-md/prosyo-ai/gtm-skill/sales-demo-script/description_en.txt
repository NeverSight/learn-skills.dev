Use this skill when you need to plan or run a B2B product demo that moves the deal forward: "build a demo script", "prep my demo for tomorrow", "our demos are feature tours", "tailor the demo to this prospect". Builds a discovery-led demo around the buyer's top two or three problems: a quick recap, the before-and-after story, the moments that matter, proof, and a clear next step. Produces a timed demo plan, talk track, the questions to ask during it and the follow-up email.
