Use this skill when you want former customers back: "win back churned customers", "re-engage cancelled accounts", "churn win-back campaign", "which lost customers should we call". Sorts churned accounts by why and when they left, picks the ones worth winning back, finds what changed since (product, pricing, their situation), and writes a short, honest win-back sequence with the right offer for each churn reason. Produces a prioritised win-back list, messages and the rules for when to leave an account alone.
