Use this skill when a B2B deal reaches pricing, discount or contract negotiation: "they want 30% off", "procurement is pushing back", "how do I negotiate this renewal", "prepare for the negotiation call". Sets your walk-away and target, maps what each side values, plans the trades (give only to get), scripts responses to the common pressure tactics, and keeps the deal value intact. Produces a negotiation plan, a give-get list and the talk track for the call.
