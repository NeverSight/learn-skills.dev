Use this skill when you want to learn from customers or prospects in a structured interview: "write customer interview questions", "voice of customer research", "why did customers buy", "jobs to be done interviews". Plans who to interview, writes a guide that uncovers the buying story (the trigger, the alternatives, the decision, the outcome) without leading questions, and turns the transcripts into themes, buyer language and changes for messaging, ICP and product. Produces an interview guide, a recruit message and a synthesis template.
