Use this skill when cold emails land in spam or reply rates suddenly drop: "audit my email deliverability", "why are my emails going to spam", "check my sending setup", "open and reply rates fell off a cliff". Checks domain setup (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, tracking domain), sending volume and patterns, bounce and complaint rates, list quality, content risks and inbox placement, ranks the problems by impact, and produces a fix plan with a recovery timeline.
