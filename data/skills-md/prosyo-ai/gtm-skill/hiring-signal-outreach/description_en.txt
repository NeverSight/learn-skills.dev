Use this skill when a target company is hiring for roles your product supports or replaces: "they are hiring SDRs", "use job postings as a buying signal", "write outreach based on their job listing". Reads the job listing for priorities, tools, team size and pain, decides whether the hire means need for your product, picks the right contact (usually the hiring manager), and writes outreach that helps the new hire succeed rather than threatening the role. Includes a scoring rule for which listings are worth acting on.
