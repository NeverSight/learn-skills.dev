Use this when a small number of accounts are worth a coordinated effort. ABM works when sales and marketing run one plan per account (or cluster) with the same message, not when marketing runs ads at a list.

## Inputs

- Target accounts and why each matters.
- Research per account (or run an account research brief).
- Budget, team and timeline.

## The play

### 1. Tier the program
| Tier | Accounts | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1:1 | 5-15 | Custom insight, content and plays per account |
| 1:few | 20-100 in clusters of 5-15 | Content per industry or shared problem |
| 1:many | 100-1,000 | Segment-level messaging, targeted ads and outbound |

### 2. Map the buying group
Per account: economic buyer, champion candidates, evaluators, users, blockers. Five to ten names for 1:1 accounts.

### 3. Write the account insight
What is happening at the account that makes your value urgent: strategy, triggers, pains, initiatives. Then the value hypothesis: "Because <insight>, <account> could <outcome> by <how>, worth about <impact>." Read [references/abm-plan-template.md](references/abm-plan-template.md) for the full one-page plan.

### 4. Plan plays by stage
| Stage | Goal | Plays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unaware | Get known by the buying group | Targeted ads to the group, executive content, events, insight-led outreach |
| Engaged | Start conversations | Personalised outreach to 3+ people, custom insight piece, invite to a roundtable |
| Opportunity | Win the evaluation | Business case built with the champion, executive sponsor, peer references |
| Customer | Expand | Value review, new-team plays |

Sales and marketing actions for each stage share one calendar.

### 5. Measure what matters
Engaged contacts per account, meetings with the buying group, opportunities created, pipeline and win rate versus non-ABM accounts. Impressions and clicks are inputs, not results.

## What good looks like

- Each Tier 1 account has a specific insight a rep could open a conversation with.
- Three or more people per account engaged before an opportunity is opened.
- Sales and marketing review accounts together every two weeks.
- The common mistake is "ABM" that is just ads to a target list, with no account insight and no sales follow-through.

## Rules

- MUST write an account-specific insight for every 1:1 account.
- MUST coordinate sales and marketing touches on one calendar.
- NEVER use personal data about individuals beyond their professional role and public work.

## Example prompts

- "Build a 1:1 ABM plan for our 10 enterprise targets in logistics."
- "Cluster these 60 accounts for a 1:few program and write the message per cluster."
- "How do we measure whether our ABM program is working?"
