Use this skill when one message does not fit every buyer and you need messaging by persona, industry or stage: "build a messaging matrix", "tailor our pitch for each persona", "different messages for CFO and CTO", "sales messaging framework". Maps each persona to its top pains, the outcome they care about, proof that persuades them, objections and the words to use and avoid, then writes ready-to-use lines for cold email, LinkedIn, calls and landing pages.
