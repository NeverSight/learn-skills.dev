Use this skill when a prospect or contact list needs cleaning before outreach or import: "clean this CSV", "dedupe my leads", "fix the names and titles", "why are my bounces high". Normalises names, titles, companies and domains, removes duplicates, invalid and risky emails, role addresses, customers and opt-outs, flags stale contacts, and outputs a send-ready list with a cleaning report. Protects sender reputation and stops embarrassing merge-field mistakes.
