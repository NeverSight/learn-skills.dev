Use this skill when you need personalised first lines (icebreakers) for cold emails or LinkedIn messages at scale: "write personalised openers for this list", "add a custom first line for each lead", "make my outreach feel 1:1". Researches each prospect for a real, relevant hook, writes a one or two sentence opener that bridges to the email's problem, grades each line, and falls back to an honest role-based line when nothing specific exists. Never invents details.
