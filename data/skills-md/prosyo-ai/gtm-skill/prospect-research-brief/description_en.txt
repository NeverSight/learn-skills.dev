Use this skill when you need to research a specific person before reaching out or meeting them: "look up this prospect", "what do I say to Jane", "find a personalised first line". Reads the person's role, tenure, public posts, talks, career moves and company context, and produces a short brief with what they care about, what they are measured on, conversation hooks with sources, and a personalised opener for email and LinkedIn. For SDRs, AEs, founders and recruiters doing B2B outreach.
