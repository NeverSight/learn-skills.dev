Use this skill when you want to grow revenue from existing customers: "find upsell and cross-sell opportunities", "which customers are ready to expand", "net revenue retention plan", "expansion playbook". Scores customer accounts on usage, health, growth signals and whitespace (teams, products and seats not yet bought), finds the strongest expansion opportunities with the evidence behind each, and writes the value-review outreach that opens the conversation without sounding like an upsell.
