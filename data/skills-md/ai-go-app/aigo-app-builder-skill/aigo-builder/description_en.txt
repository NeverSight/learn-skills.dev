Use when working on an AI GO Custom App (ai-go.app): develop the frontend (React + TypeScript) or Server‑Side Action (Python), handle deployment and verification, plan a new app from scratch (requirement inventory, determine Custom App vs. Hosted App product line), design the user authorization architecture (roles, app‑role whitelist, batch invitations for internal and external personnel), design the data architecture (custom tables in the data center / Data Reference), connect Webhooks or set up app scheduling, and migrate existing systems or entire projects (frontend + backend + DB; Supabase, Google Sheet, MySQL, etc.) into AI GO.
