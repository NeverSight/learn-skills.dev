Drill sample data interpretation (Geochemistry & Mineral Exploration). Based on lithology logs of multiple boreholes and surface coordinate data, draw continuous borehole geological cross‑section (drill section / cross section). Arrange multiple boreholes along the section line, and between boreholes through the ground. Use when the user asks about geochemistry and mineral exploration tasks such as: help me organize the data of this borehole, make a bar chart and delineate a Cu grade >0.3% ore body; plot the Au grade variation with depth; use these three boreholes to make a stratigraphic comparison section. Core tools: ogp_drill_section, ogp_drill_section.
