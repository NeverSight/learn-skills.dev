Validate and submit the DeepSeek Harness plugin to the imsai-sh/awesome-deepseek-harness-plugins community directory. It applies to plugin authors requesting inclusion, publishing, or submission of their own plugins, creating a directory JSON, fixing directory submission PRs, or initiating compliance PRs. Check the public repository, dsh-plugin topic, dsh.bundle.patch, author testing evidence, bilingual metadata, and single‑file diffs, and after obtaining authorization, perform the fork, push, and create a PR.
