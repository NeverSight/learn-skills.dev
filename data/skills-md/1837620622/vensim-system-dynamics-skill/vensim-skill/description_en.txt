Build, lay out, check and simulate Vensim system dynamics models (.mdl). Use when the user wants a stock-and-flow or causal loop diagram in Vensim, needs an existing MDL tidied (overlapping labels, crossing arrows, messy views), wants polarity and R/B loop checks, or needs reproducible simulation, scenario, sensitivity, calibration or policy runs with publication-quality Python plots. Chinese variable names by default; Windows, macOS and Linux.
