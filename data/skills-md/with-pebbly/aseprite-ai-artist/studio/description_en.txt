The front door for any Aseprite or pixel-art request. Works out what the user actually wants, picks which workflows to run and in what order — and whether to work in the user's open Aseprite window or a headless batch Aseprite (`--headless` / `--live` force it) — hands parts to the specialist agents where the harness has them, and does not stop until the result has been looked at and reviewed. Use when a request spans several steps ("make me an animated knight for Godot") or when you are unsure which workflow applies.
