Design a multi-channel isolated power supply system and verify the margin. Use it when the user wants to "design power supply/power source," "calculate current budget," "select regulator/DCDC," or "split power rails." Calculate the load on each rail based on the motor's stall current (not the rated current), generate design/power.yaml and power.md, and perform deterministic verification with tools/power_budget.py, which requires manual approval.
