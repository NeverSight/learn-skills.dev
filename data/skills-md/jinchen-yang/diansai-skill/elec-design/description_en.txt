The top-level orchestration entry point of the electric‑control design pipeline. Use it when the user wants to “start/advance the vehicle electric‑control design,” asks “what step should be done now,” needs to coordinate hardware, control, and algorithm teams in parallel, or is unsure which sub‑skill to invoke. Read STATUS.md and the current state of the design/ directory, determine the current stage, dispatch the next step, and arrange three lanes to run in parallel.
