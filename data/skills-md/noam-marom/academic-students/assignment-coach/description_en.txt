Break down, guide, check, split among group members, or solve a university assignment in any discipline — problem sets, coding homework, lab reports, legal problems, clinical cases, design briefs. Use when a student has an assignment with discrete questions and says things like "יש לי מטלה", "תעזור לי עם שאלה 3", "תפתור לי את התרגיל", "איך מתחילים את זה", "תבדוק לי את הפתרון", "רמז לסעיף ב", "break down this assignment", "where do I start on HW2", "check my solution", "hint for question 4", "איך נחלק את העבודה בינינו", "we're 3, how do we split this", or uploads an assignment PDF/DOCX/photo. Default is a breakdown; a full solution when the student asks for one. Do NOT use for continuous-prose work — seminar paper, essay, literature review, reflection — that is academic-writing even when it was assigned; nor for a conceptual question with no assignment behind it, exam practice (exam-prep) or summarising lectures (lecture-notes).
