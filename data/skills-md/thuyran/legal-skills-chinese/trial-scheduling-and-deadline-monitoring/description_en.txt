When users need to track and be reminded of various statutory deadlines for a case—such as hearing schedules, enforcement schedules, evidence submission deadlines, appeal deadlines, service deadlines, and preservation renewal deadlines—they should use this skill.

Typical trigger scenarios include, but are not limited to:
- Mentioning keywords like “appeal deadline,” “evidence submission deadline,” “service deadline,” “preservation deadline.”
- Adding, modifying, or inquiring about case deadlines.
