When it is necessary to systematically link multiple pieces of evidence in a case with legal argumentative claims, this skill is triggered. Typical trigger scenarios include: preparing litigation documents (complaint, answer, brief) and needing to construct a complete evidence‑to‑claim correspondence; organizing the evidence inventory before trial and clarifying the evidentiary purpose of each item; reorganizing the evidence chain to respond after the opposing party raises objections; the case involves multiple contentious issues, requiring grouping evidence under different argumentative claims; assessing whether the existing evidence is sufficient to support all claims, and identifying weak points and gaps in the evidence chain. The core of this skill is to establish a complete mapping of “claim → element → evidence → evidentiary‑strength assessment,” ensuring each legal claim is fully supported by evidence, each piece of evidence has a clear purpose, and the overall argument chain is logically rigorous and clearly structured.
