When an AI agent retrieves a specific legal provision during legal reasoning and needs to verify its validity, this capability is triggered.  
Trigger conditions include, but are not limited to:  
1. Before the agent cites any legal provision as a basis for reasoning;  
2. When a user provides a provision and requests analysis of its applicability;  
3. When multiple legal norms may conflict and priority must be determined;  
4. When the temporal validity of a legal document is in doubt (it may have been amended or repealed);  
5. When it is necessary to confirm whether a normative document has legal binding force;  
6. When questions arise about the application of legal norms across different levels or regions.  
This capability ensures that every legal norm cited by the agent is currently effective, correctly hierarchical, and free of conflict with higher‑level or peer laws, thereby safeguarding the reliability and authority of the legal reasoning conclusions.
