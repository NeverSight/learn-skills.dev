When comprehensive analysis of multiple related documents is needed—extracting shared viewpoints, identifying conflicts, and producing a unified overview—this capability is triggered. Typical scenarios include, but are not limited to:
- Users upload several judgments or rulings and request a summary of the key points of similar cases.
- Users provide multiple contract texts and ask for a synthesis of clause similarities, differences, and key distinctions.
- Users supply various laws, regulations, and judicial interpretations and request a review of normative evolution and essential application points.
- Users present multiple pieces of evidence or investigation transcripts and ask for an integrated factual narrative.
- Users offer several academic papers or research reports and request extraction of research consensus and divergences.
- Users request a comparison of viewpoint differences across multiple legal opinion letters.

The core value of this capability: it does not simply condense a single document, but builds cross‑document relational analysis, identifies commonalities and differences, and generates new, comprehensive insights.
