When users need to track and manage lawyer hours, expert fees, investigation costs, control litigation costs, and issue time sheets or expense statements to clients, this skill is used. Typical trigger scenarios include but are not limited to:  
- Mentioning keywords such as “working hours,” “expert fees,” “investigation costs,” “case handling cost,” “time sheet,” “expense statement,” “budget,” “cost control.”  
- Asking about a specific lawyer’s working hours, a particular case’s handling fees, or litigation costs.  
- The user requests a summary of lawyer work hours or a case time sheet/expense statement for delivery to the client or for reimbursement.  
- Needing to prepare a budget, monitor budget execution, or analyze budget variances for a specific case or routine legal service.  
- Needing to assess litigation costs versus expected benefits and conduct an economic analysis of the litigation.
