When a structured summary of legal documents such as judgments, rulings, mediation statements, arbitration awards, administrative penalty decisions, and similar is required, this capability is triggered.  
Typical trigger scenarios include, but are not limited to:  
- The user requests a summary, extraction of key points, or generation of the judgment’s gist for a legal document.  
- The user needs to convert multiple legal documents into standardized summaries that are comparable and searchable.  
- The user asks to extract case information, dispute focus, legal basis, or reasoning process.  
- The user needs summary material for case research, litigation analysis, or teaching preparation.  
- The user requests differentiation of summary focus for different document types (judgment/ruling/mediation/arbitration/administrative penalty).  

Core value of this capability: while remaining faithful to the original text, it transforms lengthy legal documents into concise, objective, structured summaries that highlight core content and avoid full-text repetition and subjective evaluation.  

Please always respond in the user’s language.
