- A reliability rating for the conclusion is required after completing legal analysis.  
- The user explicitly requests an assessment of the persuasiveness or reliability of a particular argument.  
- Multiple competing legal interpretations exist that need a comparison of argumentative strength.  
- The reasoning process involves uncertain factors (unclear facts, legal gaps, disputed viewpoints) that need to be flagged.  
- Providing risk warnings to decision‑makers, explaining the limitations of the conclusion.  
- Evaluating the offensive and defensive strength of one’s own or the opponent’s legal arguments.  
- When legal opinions, memoranda, and other documents need to include a confidence statement.
