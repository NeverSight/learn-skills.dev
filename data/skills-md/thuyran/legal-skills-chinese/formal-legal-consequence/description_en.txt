When it is necessary to derive specific legal consequences (including but not limited to the type and scope of civil liability, calculation of damages, determination of criminal sentencing, type and magnitude of administrative penalties, contract validity status, etc.) based on confirmed legal facts, identified legal relationships, and matched legal norms, this skill is triggered.  
Typical trigger scenarios include:  
- The user asks “How much compensation can I obtain?” or “What liability does the other party bear?”  
- The user asks “How many years will this conduct be sentenced to?” or “How much is the fine?”  
- A need to infer concrete legal effects from the satisfaction of legal constitutive elements.  
- A need to calculate specific amounts of compensation, sentencing ranges, breach‑of‑contract penalties, and other quantitative results.  
- A need to consider aggravating, mitigating, or exempting factors and arrive at the final legal consequence.  
This skill is the terminal link of the legal reasoning chain, converting abstract legal norms into concrete, actionable legal conclusions.
