Systemic interpretation refers to an interpretation that, in response to disputes over the meaning or scope of application arising after a literal interpretation of a legal norm, takes into account the norm’s position within the legal system (e.g., which law, code, title, chapter, or section it belongs to), the meaning of related legal norms, or the purpose that the law, as a system, is intended to serve, and provides the interpretation most consistent with the system. This clarifies the meaning and scope of the legal norm from a systemic perspective.
