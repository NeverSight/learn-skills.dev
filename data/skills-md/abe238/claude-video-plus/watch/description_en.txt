Watch, analyze, summarize, or answer questions about a video — a pasted link (YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Loom, X, Twitch, most sites) or a local video file (.mp4, .mov, .mkv, .webm, .avi). Use this whenever the user shares a video link or file and asks anything about its contents — what's in it, what happens, what's said, what's on screen, a summary, a transcript, or a specific moment — and whenever they say "watch this", "watch the video", "what's in this video", or similar, even if they never type /watch. Downloads with yt-dlp, extracts auto-scaled frames with ffmpeg, pulls the transcript from captions (or Whisper API fallback), and hands the result to Claude so it can answer questions about what's in the video. With a question, evidence mode retrieves only the relevant chapters, numeric facts, and on-screen moments instead of sampling the whole timeline.
