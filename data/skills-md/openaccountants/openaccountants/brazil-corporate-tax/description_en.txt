Brazil corporate income tax for calendar year 2026 for ordinary resident companies and advisers calculating IRPJ and CSLL. Covers lucro real, eligibility for lucro presumido, ordinary rates and the IRPJ surcharge, the 2026 increase in presumptive percentages under LC 224/2025, activity allocation, deductions, separate loss pools, quarterly payments, annual estimates, ECF and DCTFWeb/MIT. Includes the dividend changes under Lei 15.270/2025 and two recomputing cases, including a lucro real versus presumido decision. Trigger on IRPJ, CSLL, lucro presumido, lucro real, Brazil company tax, ECF and corporate tax Brazil. Financial-sector rates, incentives, reorganisations and multinational minimum tax need separate advice. Also the regime overview (regimes tributários) comparing Simples Nacional, lucro presumido and lucro real, with PIS/Cofins under each and the 2026 CBS and IBS test rates. Trigger on regime tributário, Simples ou lucro presumido, IBS, CBS and reforma tributária.
