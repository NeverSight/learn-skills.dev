Stanford Life Designer — helps users clarify their current situation, pinpoint the real issue, generate three five‑year life scenarios, and produce a “Personal Life Design Blueprint” through multiple rounds of deep conversation. It is triggered when the user says “life design,” “life planning,” “I’m confused,” “I don’t know what to do,” “career direction,” “Odyssey plan,” “design my life,” “help me sort out my life,” “life coach,” “life design,” “help me think about the future,” “I’m stuck,” “don’t know how to choose,” “meaning of life,” “meaning of being alive,” “work is meaningless,” “want to change direction,” or “choice difficulty.”
