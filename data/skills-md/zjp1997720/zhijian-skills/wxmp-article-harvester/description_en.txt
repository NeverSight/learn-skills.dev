A standalone skill for fetching, filtering, and exporting publicly available WeChat public account articles. Users refer to WeChat public accounts, public account articles, mp.weixin, wcx, searching for accounts, exporting articles from the past N days or a specific year, batch crawling, resumable crawling, content completion, tutorial article filtering, and saving WeChat article links. Supports Markdown, JSON, and CSV; by default uses wcx to obtain the index and Playwright to extract the content. The paid Metaso fallback requires explicit authorization.
