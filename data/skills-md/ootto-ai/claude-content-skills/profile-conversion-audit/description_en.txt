Use when an owned social profile needs an evidence-based audit of whether the right visitor can understand the account, trust it, and take the next approved step. Trigger phrases include "audit our Instagram profile", "fix our LinkedIn page", "why does this profile not convert?", "review this social bio", and "what should be clearer on our profile?". Do not use this to promise conversion lifts, change a platform account directly, or write a campaign message from scratch; use positioning-audit for the core message, social-proof-mining for approved proof, and campaign-brief for a launch-specific promise.
