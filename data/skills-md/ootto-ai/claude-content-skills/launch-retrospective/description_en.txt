Use after a social launch, campaign, creator collaboration, or distribution test has enough evidence to decide what should be repeated, changed, or stopped. Trigger phrases include "run a launch retro", "what did we learn from this campaign?", "postmortem this launch", "should we repeat this?", and "turn these results into next steps". Do not use this to assign blame, invent missing data, or write a live performance dashboard; use social-analytics-report for the in-flight metric read and campaign-brief to plan the next campaign.
