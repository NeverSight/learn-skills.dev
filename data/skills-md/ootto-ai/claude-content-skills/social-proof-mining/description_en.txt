Use when you need to locate and prepare already-approved customer evidence for a social campaign, profile, post, or sales-adjacent explanation. Trigger phrases include "find proof we can use", "what customer evidence supports this?", "mine testimonials", "turn these outcomes into social proof", and "which quote backs this claim?". Do not use this to fabricate testimonials, imply endorsement, expose private customer information, or turn a single anecdote into a performance promise; use social-listening for broad feedback patterns and positioning-audit for the message itself.
