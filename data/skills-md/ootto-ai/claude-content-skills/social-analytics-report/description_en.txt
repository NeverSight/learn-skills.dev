Use when you need to turn supplied social performance data into a decision about what to keep, test, change, or stop. Trigger phrases include "report on this campaign", "what worked on social?", "analyse these post results", "make a weekly social report", and "what should we do next from these metrics?". Do not use this to invent data access, claim causation from a dashboard, or replace a campaign brief; use campaign-brief to define success before launch and launch-retrospective for the broader after-action review.
