Use when an approved campaign, launch, research finding, or asset needs a deliberate plan across several social channels. Trigger phrases include "adapt this for LinkedIn and X", "where should this campaign go?", "make a distribution plan", "repurpose this without duplicating it", and "what is each platform's role?". Do not use this to invent a campaign strategy, bulk-post identical copy, or schedule a calendar; use campaign-brief for the strategic source, content-repurposer for asset transformations, and content-calendar for dates.
