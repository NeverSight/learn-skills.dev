Use when a social campaign needs a decision-ready brief before anyone writes posts, books creators, or spends budget. Trigger phrases include "brief this launch", "turn this into a campaign plan", "what should this campaign say?", "make a social brief", and "align the team on this promotion". Do not use this for a content calendar, a single caption, or an unapproved product claim; use content-calendar for scheduling, caption-and-hashtags for a post, and positioning-audit when the core message is still unclear.
