# Onboarding copy

## Principles

- **One job per screen** — do not explain the whole product at once.
- Lead with outcome: "Track invoices in one place" not "Welcome to our platform".
- Short sentences; active voice; no jargon on step 1.

## Patterns

| Moment | Copy pattern |
|--------|----------------|
| Welcome | Benefit + single CTA |
| Empty state | What's missing + why + CTA |
| Tooltip | ≤ 120 chars; optional "Learn more" link |
| Checklist onboarding | 3–5 steps max; celebrate completion |

## Progressive disclosure

- Advanced features introduced **after** first success (aha moment).
- Skippable tours; remember skip in localStorage or profile.

## Accessibility

- Tooltips keyboard-dismissible; don't block primary task.

## Deliverable

Provide copy per component/screen:

- Headline
- Body (optional)
- Primary CTA label
- Secondary (skip) label

## Avoid

- Lorem ipsum or "[Title here]".
- Blocking modal chains > 3 steps without skip.
- Identical copy on every empty state.
