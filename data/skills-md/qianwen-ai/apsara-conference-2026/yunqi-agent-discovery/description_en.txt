Yunqi Conference (Yunqi) Discovery and Planning Skill. Query agenda and exhibition information, recommend and compare conferences and exhibitors/booths based on Agent objectives, answer introductory questions about the conference's purpose, highlights, venue, etc., based on the program booklet; answer service-related questions about transportation, accommodation, dining, tickets, etc., based on the attendee guide. Applicable to scenarios such as “query conference agenda,” “query exhibition information,” “recommend conference,” “compare exhibitors/booths,” “plan attendance route,” “conference highlights,” “conference introduction,” “transportation/accommodation/dining,” “tickets/check‑in,” etc. The data source is the yunqi subcommand of the qianwen CLI (API retrieval preferred, with static program booklet and attendee guide as supplements), publicly read‑only, and performs no write operations.
