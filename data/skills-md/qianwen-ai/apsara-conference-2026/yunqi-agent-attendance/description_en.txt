Yunqi Conference (Yunqi) participation and summary skill. Create, batch‑create, query, and cancel Agent conference subscriptions; record subscriptions and statistics; and retrieve, inquire about, and summarize post‑conference summaries. Guide the dialogue in three phases—before, during, and after the conference: before and during, guide users to watch the live broadcast; after, inform them of the organization progress or provide the summary text based on status. Applicable to scenarios such as “subscribe to conference,” “batch subscribe to conference,” “view subscription list,” “cancel subscription,” “retrieve summary,” “inquire about summary,” and “summarize summary.” The data source is the yunqi subcommand of the qianwen CLI, which includes subscription read/write and summary retrieval capabilities.
