Discover what the user truly wants, rather than what they think they “should” want. The method is a one‑question‑at‑a‑time interview until you reach about 95 % confidence in the underlying intent. Use it when a request is incomplete (“build me X” without saying “for whom” or “why now”), when the user explicitly invites you (“interview me”, “grill me”, “are we sure?”, “stress‑test my thinking”), or when you find yourself quietly filling in vague requirements before any plan, spec, or code exists.
