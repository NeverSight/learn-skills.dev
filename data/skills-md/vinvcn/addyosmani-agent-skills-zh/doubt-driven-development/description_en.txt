Before any nontrivial decision is made, conduct an adversarial review using a completely new context. Use this when you want to cross‑examine every hypothesis before proceeding, stress‑test the plan to expose hidden failure modes, prioritize correctness over speed, are dealing with unfamiliar code, face high risk (production auth, security‑sensitive logic, high‑risk migrations, irreversible operations), or whenever you are confident that verifying the output now is cheaper than debugging it later.
