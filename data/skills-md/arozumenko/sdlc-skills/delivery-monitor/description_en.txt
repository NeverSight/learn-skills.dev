Delivery monitor for harness work items — the cycle-time and cadence sibling of tokenomics. Registers a plan (campaign → mission → task) with ranged, human-accepted estimates, records dispatch/merge/cancel transitions into a git-committed event ledger (.agents/telemetry/delivery/), backfills history from git, and reports cycle time, weekly throughput/velocity and estimate-vs-actual delta per level. Use when the user asks "how fast are we delivering", "register the plan / estimates", "record that TASK-023 merged", "delivery report / status", "enable delivery tracking", or "how good were our estimates". For cost per case use tokenomics; for sizing new work use automation-scoping.
