Write Celigo settings forms -- the form definition (settingsForm) the Celigo UI renders to collect a resource's settings, on integrations, flow groups, flows, exports, imports, connections, and iClients. Covers the seven scopes and how steps read their values, fields and types, options, conditions, layouts and containers, init scripts, and the dynamic exportSelect and staticMap fields. Use when adding or changing a settings form, giving users a setting that steps read at run time, building a dropdown from an export or a value-mapping table, or working out why a form or a setting doesn't show what you expect.
