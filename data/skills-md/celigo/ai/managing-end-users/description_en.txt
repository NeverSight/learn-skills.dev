Manage Celigo MCP end users -- people who use an account's MCP servers without workspace access -- and the groups, roles, capability sets, and grants that decide which tools, APIs, prompts, and resources each one can call. Covers inviting and revoking, granting per server, groups and roles, what removing a capability set does to grants, compiled effective access, and end users' own connections. Use when giving people or teams access to a Celigo OAuth MCP server, changing or removing that access, or working out why someone can or can't call a tool.
