Manage Celigo network policies -- named IP allow and deny lists that restrict where API tokens, MCP servers, and workspace-user or end-user sign-in may connect from. Covers how a policy decides, the four places one is assigned, patch versus full-replace edits, deleting a policy that is still assigned, and avoiding locking yourself out. Use when restricting an MCP server, a token, or sign-in to known networks, editing or deleting a policy, or checking whether a policy explains a refused caller.
