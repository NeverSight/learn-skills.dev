Configure Celigo Event Streams topics -- durable message channels that let one flow publish records and any number of other flows consume them, each from its own position. Covers creating and registering topics, retention and licensing, publisher and listener steps, publishing, reading and purging messages, listener positions, and delete rules. Use when creating or registering a topic, wiring a topic publisher or listener, publishing or inspecting messages, diagnosing a listener that never runs, or deleting a topic.
