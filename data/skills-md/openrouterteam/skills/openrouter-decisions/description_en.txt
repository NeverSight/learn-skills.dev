Use when replacing a prompt-and-parse LLM call, a keyword or similarity heuristic, a human review queue, or a misfiring router, filter, or classifier with a decision model, when locating something in an image with a Box question, when moving TypeSafe SDK or System One code onto OpenRouter, when asked to find or implement decision-model use cases in an app or agent, and whenever decision models, Jev, TypeSafe, or System One come up. Covers routing, classification, moderation, guardrails, grounding checks, scoring, ranking, dedupe, escalation and approval gates, bounded extraction, and thresholds. Not for chat completions, text generation, or general OpenRouter setup.
