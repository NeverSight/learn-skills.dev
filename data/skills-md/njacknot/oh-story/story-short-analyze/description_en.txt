Short online fiction analysis. Dissect the core story, structure, emotional line, twist design, writing techniques, and resonance levels of popular short novels (Tomato Short Stories / Story Collections / Zhihu Salt Selection / Wife Pursuit / World Affairs / Rebirth / Scumbag Abuse and other mainstream topics). A single comprehensive analysis pipeline: after completing stages 2–6, produce a complete analysis report and save it to `拆文库/{book title}/`. The downstream `story-short-write` simultaneously reads the analysis report, plot nodes, writing techniques, original text, and _meta.json to write the next piece. Trigger methods: /story-short-analyze, /短篇拆文, “拆短篇”, “拆这篇短文”, “短篇拆文”, “精细拆解短篇”, “8000字短篇拆解”, “番茄短篇拆文”, “故事会拆解”, “盐言故事拆解”, “分析这篇短篇” — all go into the same pipeline.
