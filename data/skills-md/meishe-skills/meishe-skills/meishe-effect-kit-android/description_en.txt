Integrate, extend, troubleshoot, or assist in the acceptance of NveEffectKit within an existing Android project, and record the final output texture of the project's existing NVE pipeline to the system photo gallery. This is used for the first integration or addition of complete or individual beauty skin, beauty shaping, micro‑plastic surgery, makeup, filters, facial props, custom effects, and recording of the screen after NVE processing, as well as handling AAR/authorization/models/assets, CameraX/Camera2/custom GL, lifecycle, black screen/crash/freeze, preview or effect not taking effect, orientation mirroring, audio‑video synchronization, and performance/UI verification issues; it is not intended for generic camera recording without an NVE final output pipeline.
