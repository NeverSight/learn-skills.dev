Use when validating a completed task or a colleague's deliverable for task acceptance, initially or after prior review and revisions: whether the work is correct, complete, and trustworthy, including review plus real acceptance/edge-case testing, re-reviewing fixes against earlier findings and current task requirements, or checking a research/security report and its evidence. Not for defect-only or merge-readiness review without task-completion validation even if a ticket is cited; test-only execution; reporting settled findings; explanation-only walkthroughs; implementing the task; or fixing findings.
