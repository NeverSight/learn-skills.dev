Use whenever a Multica issue asks to "work on the contribution", "implement <candidate> in <owner/repo>", "prepare the branch for <repo>", "turn the scouted contribution into a PR-ready diff", or to "address the review comments on <PR>" — even if the issue only names the target repo and the change to make.
