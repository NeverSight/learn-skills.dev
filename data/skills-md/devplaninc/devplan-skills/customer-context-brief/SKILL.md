# Customer Context Brief

For one named customer, identify current product needs, open concerns, and the most important follow-ups. Use the freshest customer-specific evidence available. Check whether recent meetings or product changes may be missing, and do not substitute evidence from other customers or business verticals.

Identify verified shipped updates worth sharing, plus relevant ongoing or planned work. Separate what is shareable now, ongoing or planned, and not safe to claim. Do not equate merged code with deployed or customer-visible behavior.

Apply the `devplan-product-context` foundation skill when available. Let it handle retrieval, graph interpretation, evidence quality, and delivery-state checks. If it is unavailable, say so. Do not imply Devplan was checked.

Use Devplan and connected sources as read-only unless the user asks for a specific change. Draft only; never send or schedule messages.
