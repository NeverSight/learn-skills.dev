# Pop Report and Scarcity Premium

Pop counts drive premium. PSA 10 pop < 100 = scarcity premium; pop > 10,000 = liquidity card.

## Pop count tiers

| PSA 10 pop | Pricing dynamic |
|---|---|
| 1-10 | Auction-only, scarcity premium 5-20× normal |
| 10-50 | Scarcity premium 2-5× |
| 50-200 | Mild scarcity premium |
| 200-1,000 | Standard premium |
| 1,000-5,000 | Liquid market, comp-driven |
| 5,000+ | Commodity, fee-floor pricing |
| 10,000+ | Diminishing returns, may compress |

## Pop growth signals

Track pop growth quarterly:
- Pop +5%/quarter = healthy
- Pop +15%/quarter = supply pressure (price may compress)
- Pop +30%/quarter = saturation incoming
- Pop flat = stable market

## Pop manipulation

Watch for:
1. Sudden pop spike after price ramp = submitters chasing → expect pullback
2. Pop 9 >> Pop 10 = most miss 10 by one axis → submission EV poor
3. Pop 10 = 1 'highest known' = liquidity vanishes; niche collector only
