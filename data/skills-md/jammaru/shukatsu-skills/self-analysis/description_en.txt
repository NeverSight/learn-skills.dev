A job‑search and career‑change support skill that uses a dialogue‑style interview to systematically elicit a user’s experiences, values, strengths, and weaknesses, and generates an objective self‑analysis report. It is employed when users say things like “self‑analysis,” “preparing for job hunting,” “I want to know my strengths and weaknesses,” “I want to create a self‑PR,” “I want to organize my motivation for applying,” or “I want to write my ‘gakuchika.’”
