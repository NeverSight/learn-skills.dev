Design-engineering rules and the decision process behind tactile, layered, quiet product UI. Use when building, restyling or reviewing product interfaces such as dashboards, settings pages, onboarding flows, forms, modals and bottom sheets, menus, tables, cards, toasts, buttons and motion; when deciding primary versus secondary actions, how much data to show, where controls go or how hierarchy should read; when making UI feel tactile and layered in both light and dark mode; or when adapting this system to any typeface, brand colours and data.
