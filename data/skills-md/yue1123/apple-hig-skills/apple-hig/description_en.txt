Apple Human Interface Guidelines distilled for React Native projects, organized by platform (iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, visionOS, watchOS) with concrete RN implementation mappings, exact metrics, and review checklists. Use this skill whenever building, reviewing, or restyling any UI that runs on an Apple platform — screens, navigation, sheets, modals, alerts, lists, forms, buttons, tab bars, toolbars, typography, color, dark mode, Liquid Glass, animation, haptics, safe areas, Dynamic Type, accessibility, VoiceOver, widgets, notifications, or App Store requirements like permissions and account deletion. Also use it when the user asks whether something "feels native", "looks like an Apple app", follows the HIG, or should be adapted for iPad/Mac/TV/Vision Pro — and when porting an Android or web design to iOS, where platform conventions are most often lost. Prefer this skill over general design intuition for any Apple-platform UI decision, even when Apple or the HIG isn't mentioned by name.
