Makes a task or prompt more ambitious using Felix Rieseberg's types of ambition, Altitude (aim at the outcome, the goal beyond the goal), Depth (work like the best expert in the field, from the raw data, checked by an outside judge) and Range (bring in other fields and do what their experts do). Writes the ambitious plan and a rewritten prompt, then does the work when it was handed a task. Use when the user types /be-ambitious, says "be more ambitious", "aim higher", "go deeper on this", "what's the goal beyond the goal", "what would a top expert do here", "raise the bar", or asks to make a prompt more ambitious.
