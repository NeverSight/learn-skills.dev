Rigorous QA of a finished implementation (human- or AI-written) against its spec, ticket or acceptance criteria. Writes and runs tests for correctness, edge and boundary cases (null/None/undefined, falsy, empty, limits, bad data), error handling and determinism, checks for common AI-generated-code pitfalls, and reports PASS/FAIL with exact failing inputs. Use after a feature or fix is implemented, before handover or merge, or when asked to "test this", "QA this", "verify", or "check the edge cases".
