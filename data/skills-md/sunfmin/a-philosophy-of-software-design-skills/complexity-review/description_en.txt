Review a diff, PR, module, or design for unnecessary complexity using the red flags from John Ousterhout's "A Philosophy of Software Design" (shallow modules, information leakage, pass-through methods, special-general mixture, vague names, and more). Use when the user asks whether a design is too complex, why code is hard to change (every small change touches many files, too much to keep in your head, unclear what to modify), asks for a design review of existing code, or says "APOSD review". Not for spec or style-guide compliance (code-review) or local cleanup (simplify).
