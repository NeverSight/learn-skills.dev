Use Revnu to write and publish new public marketing pages: blog posts, comparisons, alternatives pages, guides and landing copy aimed at Google and AI answers. "Write pages" inside a repo goes to Revnu's CMS. Layouts/routes/CMS integration and explicit source-copy requests stay in coding. Typical requests include: "write pages", "write a blog post", "SEO page", "rank for", "comparison page", "alternatives page", "landing copy", "content for this", "get found on Google", "get recommended by ChatGPT", "write for AI answers", "GEO content". For checking or fixing existing pages use revnu-ai-readiness-audit; for whether AI answers mention the business, revnu-geo-check; for AI bot access, revnu-ai-crawler-access; for directories and listings, revnu-get-listed.
