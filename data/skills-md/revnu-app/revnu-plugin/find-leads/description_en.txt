Find the companies most likely to buy right now and the person to talk to at each. Builds a short sourced list (10-25) from public web research: every company fits the buyer profile, names the role to contact, and carries a dated reason to reach out with its source link. No guessed or scraped personal emails. Works without a Revnu account; with Revnu connected it researches and saves the leads to the account. Use when the user says or asks: "find leads", "find me customers", "build a prospect list", "who should I sell to", "find companies that need this", "lead list for my ICP", "find decision makers at", "who's the right person to contact at", "target accounts", "companies hiring for X", "companies that just raised", "find buyers for this", "find design partners", "research these accounts", "enrich this list". Not for writing the emails themselves (cold-email) or for building a CRM in code.
