Write cold emails that get replies and reach the inbox. Writes a first email and 2-3 follow-ups from a real, dated reason to reach out (no invented personalization), checks the sending domain's SPF, DKIM, DMARC, volume, warm-up and unsubscribe setup, and sets stop rules as numbers before anything goes out. Works without a Revnu account; with Revnu connected it prepares and sends within the founder's approval settings and handles replies. Not for newsletters, onboarding or other email to people who signed up.
