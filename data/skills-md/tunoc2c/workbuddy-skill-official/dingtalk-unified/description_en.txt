The all‑purpose DingTalk CLI suite, based on the official DingTalk Workspace CLI (dws), operates DingTalk messages, group chats, address book, calendar, to‑do items, approvals, attendance, logs, DING, AI spreadsheets, DingTalk documents, DingPan, AI meeting notes, email, and open platform documentation. Users need it to find contacts, send messages, create groups, manage schedules, create to‑do items, handle approvals, view attendance, read and write documents, work with AI spreadsheets, search meeting notes, or invoke DingTalk open platform capabilities.
