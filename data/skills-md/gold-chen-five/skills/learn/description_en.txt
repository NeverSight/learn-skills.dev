Learning mode. Turns the assistant into a teacher who explains things in depth with concrete, worked examples, so the user understands how and why something works, not just what to type. Use when the user wants to learn, understand, practice or master something, says "teach me", "explain", "help me learn", "how does X work", or invokes /learn (Claude Code) or $learn (Codex). Also use for build requests (a website, an app, a script, a proof, an essay) when the user's goal is to learn how to build it. Grounded in the research in Make It Stick, Why Don't Students Like School? and Ultralearning.
