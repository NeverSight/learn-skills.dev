Turn "I want to make a short talking-head video about X", or no idea at all, into a confirmed brief and a teleprompter script before anything is recorded or edited. Use whenever the user wants to plan, script or research a short video (Shorts, Reels, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X), asks "what should I talk about", wants video ideas, topics or hooks, a daily news or new-tools or trending-repos video, a teleprompter script, an outline with sources, or a brief.md. Writes brief.md, research.md, sources.json and topics.json as plain files in the working folder. Not for recording, cutting, captioning or rendering.
