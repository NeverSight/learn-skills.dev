Build interactive presentation websites — slide decks projected to a room, controlled by one presenter. Use this skill WHENEVER the user wants to create, build, or design a presentation, slide deck, talk, or "bài thuyết trình / slide / trình chiếu", and also when they ask to add/edit slides, restyle a deck, scaffold a new presentation project, reuse their presentation setup in another repo, control slides from a phone (remote, QR code, "điều khiển slide bằng điện thoại"), or export slides to PDF — even if they don't say the word "slide" but clearly need projected talk visuals (e.g. "I'm giving a talk next week and need visuals", "dựng deck cho buổi chia sẻ", "làm mấy trang chiếu cho hội thảo"). Supports two tracks: a zero-build single HTML file, or a Vite + React project. Do NOT use it for printable documents, normal web apps, or dashboards meant for individual users to interact with.
