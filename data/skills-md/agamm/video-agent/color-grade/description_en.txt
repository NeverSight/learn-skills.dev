Color-grade a video — apply a cinematic look / LUT, or generate and compare candidate looks for the user to pick. Use whenever footage looks flat/muted/cold and needs grading, you want a named reusable look (warm, teal-orange, punchy, neutral), or you have a .cube LUT to apply. Covers applying .cube/HALD LUTs via ffmpeg, baking a look from a color-filter chain, and the "render a few looks, let me choose" workflow.
