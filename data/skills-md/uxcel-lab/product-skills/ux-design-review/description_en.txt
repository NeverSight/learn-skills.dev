Orchestrated, holistic UX review of a screen, flow, or component. Establishes context once, auto-runs the always-relevant lenses (usability, microcopy, dark patterns), offers the context-dependent ones (accessibility, aesthetics, IA, mobile, visual foundations), and returns one prioritized report. Trigger when the user asks for a full design review, "review my UI/screen/flow", "is this good UX", "audit this design", or general design feedback — but NOT when they name a single specific lens.
