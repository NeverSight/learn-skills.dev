Run a structured rigor audit on how a team validated (or plans to validate) its assumptions — for a discovery plan, experiment, test, or any "we validated it" claim. Produces a severity-rated issue list with concrete fixes — catches testing the comfortable assumption instead of the riskiest, untestable phrasing, method fidelity mismatched to decision risk, stated intent mistaken for behavior, success criteria set after the fact, goalpost-moving, and underpowered evidence. Use when reviewing a validation/discovery approach or as the validation step after generating an assumption-test plan.
