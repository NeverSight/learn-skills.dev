Orchestrated review of a PM artifact (spec, PRD, OKRs, roadmap, prioritization, strategy). Routes by artifact type to the right audits, auto-runs the always-relevant assumption check, and returns one prioritized report. Trigger when the user asks for a full review of a PM document, "review my spec/roadmap/OKRs", "is this PRD solid", or general PM feedback — but NOT when they name a single specific audit.
