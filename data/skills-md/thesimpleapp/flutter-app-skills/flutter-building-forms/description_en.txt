Build Flutter forms with Form, TextFormField, DropdownMenuFormField, and custom FormField. Covers validation, AutovalidateMode, forceErrorText, theme-driven InputDecoration, autofillHints, keyboards, and focus traversal on Flutter 3.47+. Use when adding or fixing a login, signup, checkout, or any validate/save form; when fields jump, lose text, skip autofill, or ignore FormState.validate(); or when the user runs /flutter-building-forms.
