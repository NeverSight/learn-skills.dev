Generate, poll and inspect individual image, video, speech or music assets through openmotion_assets_use after openmotion_media_use discovery (configured Atlas Cloud, fal.ai, WaveSpeedAI or OpenDesign Cloud BYOK providers, or a signed-in OpenDesign account), either when delegated by an OpenMotion film workflow or for a standalone asset-generation request. Native-only tasks with no new speech or music stay outside generation unless explicitly requested. Not a whole-film entry point: creating, recreating, templating, editing or exporting a whole video belongs to the OpenMotion video entries.
