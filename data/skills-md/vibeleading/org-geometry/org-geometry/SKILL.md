# Organizational Geometry

Organizational Geometry is the shape of a Vibe Leading team: one Pilot, four human Specialists, and a swarm of AI agents doing the grind.

> Source: "Vibe Leading The AI: The Corporate Race Against Machines" (Jean Machuca). Framework concepts: the 5-Person Racing Squad, the Double Tenaza, killing the Bridge, NPC Agents.

## The 5-Person Racing Squad

**1 Pilot + 4 Specialist Crew + 100+ NPC Agents.**

- **1 Pilot** — you. Sets the Vibe, monitors the HUD, runs the Laps.
- **4 Specialist Crew** — human experts:

| Specialist | Duty |
|------------|------|
| Ops Mechanic | Maintains the "Garage": AI tools, data pipelines, MCP servers. |
| Logic Navigator | Maps mission goals into the HUD (metrics, thresholds, triggers). |
| Creative Pilot | Crafts Mission Scripts that tell the AI how to speak. |
| Risk Spotter | Watches for Hallucination Debt; owns the Privacy Shield compliance score. |

- **100+ NPC Agents** — AI agents doing the grind. A Specialist writes a 5-minute Mission Script and an NPC generates the report in 10 seconds.

Role details, old-role replacements, and success signals in `references/diamond-squad-roles.md`.

## The Double Tenaza (two-handed grip)

Two grips hold the airplane steady:

- **Grip 1 = Human Intuition.** Feel when a project "smells" wrong. The gut is a sensor.
- **Grip 2 = Machine Precision.** Trust the HUD telemetry more than a human's feeling. The scoreboard outranks the vibe-check.

**Quiet Grip:** at IRA >= 95%, hold steady, stay quiet, let the machine run. **The Snap:** the instant telemetry drifts, tighten the grip and pull the machine back to the Vibe.

## Killing the Bridge

The middle-management translator is drag. Replace it with Direct Intent: Mission Scripts handed straight from the Pilot to the machine.

- Stop "Reporting Up." Start "Displaying Out": give the boss View-Only HUD / Observer Access.
- Vertical Reporting is Performance Art. Current telemetry beats a rehearsed summary.

## NPC Agents

The three canonical agents, each driven by a Mission Script from a Specialist:

- **The Researcher NPC** — scours market data 24/7. Fed by the Real-Time Scout MCP connector.
- **The Auditor NPC** — checks every line for Hallucination Debt. Owns the debt tally on the HUD.
- **The Drafter NPC** — produces the first 80% of every document from the Pilot's Vibe.

Full catalog and orchestration patterns in `references/npc-agent-catalog.md`.

## ROI of Orchestration

- **Decouple growth from headcount.** Output scales with NPC count, not hires.
- **Management overhead ~0.** The HUD replaces status meetings; the Bridge is dead.
- **Reinvest the 30%.** Whatever portion of the week the old rituals consumed goes into Vibe calibration and Circuit Delta.

## How to use this skill

1. Assign the four Specialist seats first; every one is a human, and every one maps a mission goal into the machine's world.
2. Name the NPCs per workload and point each at the MCP feed it needs.
3. Kill the Bridge: Observer Access to the HUD, not summaries upward.
4. Test the Double Tenaza: can you Let Go (automation holding)? Can you Grip (snap triggers armed)?
