# Pit Stop Audit

Every Lap ends in a Pit Stop, and every month the Pit Stop deepens into a Vibe Audit. Both are calibration rituals, not status meetings: they answer "are the right things on the scoreboard?" far more than "how is it going?"

> Source: "Vibe Leading The AI: The Corporate Race Against Machines" (Jean Machuca). Framework concepts: the Pit Stop Ritual, the Vibe Audit, the Pilot's Final Checklist.

## The Pit Stop Ritual (per Lap)

Replace 2-week Sprints with Laps (4 hours to 4 days). Each Lap ends in a Pit Stop with three moves:

1. **Review** — did the NPCs hit the Vibe? Grade the Lap against the Mission Script.
2. **Refuel** — more data or better prompts? The Vibe was the usual suspect in any drift.
3. **Restart** — launch the next Lap immediately. The job is to keep Lap Time decreasing without losing Traction.

## The Monthly Pit Stop / Vibe Audit

The monthly ritual has three checks:

1. **Check the HUD** — are the metrics still measuring the right things? A metric that stopped predicting value is theater.
2. **Check the Squad** — is the Double Tenaza too tight? Are the humans burning out? Pace is a dial, not a wall.
3. **Check the Script** — has the market moved? Does the Mission Script need a rewrite? Write it fresh or it drifts.

## Traction vs Velocity sanity check

Velocity is "Our AI generated 1,000 lead-gen emails in 60 seconds." Traction is "Did those emails result in a 5% increase in booked meetings, or did they just get our domain blacklisted for spam?"

Velocity without Traction is a spectacular way to crash. The audit checks for system drift: muscle memory shipping output that no longer moves the metric. Volume is not a strategy; it is a speedometer. Traction is the compass.

## The Pilot's Final Checklist

Five checkpoints, each with sub-checkboxes. Run them at kickoffs, quarter starts, and after any significant drift.

1. **The Vibe Calibration** — Is the Mission Script high-fidelity: poetic clarity a human feels plus engineering precision an AI can execute? Is the Frequency set — Stealth/Precision or High-Velocity/Market-Capture?
2. **The Garage Inspection** — Are MCP Servers live? Is the Privacy Shield active? Is the HUD visible?
3. **The Diamond Squad Roll Call** — Are my 4 Specialists in position covering Logic / Ops / Creative / Risk? Is the "Bridge" dead?
4. **The Double Tenaza Grip** — Ready to Let Go: is the system automated enough? Ready to Grip: are telemetry triggers set to alert the moment AI drifts?
5. **The Circuit Delta Target** — What is our Lap Time baseline? What is our Target to decrease Circuit Delta?

The full printable checklist is in `references/pilot-final-checklist.md`.

## The Pilot's Oath

> "I am no longer a facilitator of meetings. I am an orchestrator of intelligence. I do not manage by the clock; I lead by the Scoreboard. My technical authority is my engine; my poetic vision is my compass. I will not be a fossil. I am the Pilot."

## How to use this skill

1. At the end of every Lap, run the three Pit Stop moves: Review, Refuel, Restart.
2. Once a month (or at project kickoff / quarter start), run the Vibe Audit's three checks.
3. Then run the Pilot's Final Checklist end to end, ticking every sub-checkbox.
4. Fix whatever fails the checklist first, before re-arming the next Lap.
