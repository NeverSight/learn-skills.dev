Find and pick B-roll (stock clips for the lines of a voice-over), background music, photos and sound effects that fit the video, and check each before it goes into the edit. Use it before calling search_videos, search_music, search_photos or search_sfx, or downloading from Pexels or Pixabay, whenever a video needs B-roll, cutaway shots or music, and when someone says a pick does not fit. Writes what each slot needs, gathers 3-5 candidates per slot, measures each with measure.py (camera shake, cuts inside a clip, what a 9:16 crop keeps, repeats; for music the tempo, intro, energy, length), looks at every frame sheet, drops what does not show the line, and has the person hear two or three music options first.
