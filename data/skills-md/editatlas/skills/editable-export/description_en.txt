Give the person an editable copy of a video you made, built from its elements (footage clips, text, graphics, sounds, music), which opens in the EditAtlas studio, a web page with a timeline. There they fix small things themselves and export the MP4 again in their browser, without asking you to edit and re-render. Typical fixes are a sound a little late or too loud, text slightly off, captions too small, a typo, a clip a few frames long. The copy is a folder on their computer (kept in Google Drive from the studio, it also gets a link). Each save adds a version, so you can take turns. It is optional. Offer it when you deliver a video, and build it only when they want it. Also use it when someone asks for "an editable version", "a project file", to "tweak it myself", pastes a message from EditAtlas Studio, "chỉnh lại chút", "tự sửa", "file để sửa", "chỉnh cỡ chữ", or "âm thanh bị trễ" after an edit. It is not a full editor; effects burned into the clips cannot be changed there.
