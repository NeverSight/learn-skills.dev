When a lawyer needs to archive files in the inbox with a single sentence, or to present the facts and options of a case in a conversation (e.g., “note this line,” “I confirm this date,” “proceed with the second option”), they use it; it is also used at the beginning and end of drafting an opening to first clear the inbox and turn the facts given in the conversation into a statement. It provides two zero‑dependency CLIs: archival transfer (moving the relative paths inside the inbox as‑is to materials, guides, templates/official, templates/generated, with destination determined by a model) and statement filing (one entry per file, placed into materials/lawyer‑statements/, read‑only after being written to disk). It does not create diagrams: modifying diagram composition and adding entries belong to the skill “loo0ng‑graph”; generating a document version and finalizing it are driven by the skill “loo0ng‑doit”; domain directories and the prototype derived from guides belong to the skill “loo0ng‑domain”; asking where and what to do next is the skill “ask‑loo0ng”. It does not register, compute fingerprints, deduplicate, decompress, recognize images, nor maintain a current statement list.
