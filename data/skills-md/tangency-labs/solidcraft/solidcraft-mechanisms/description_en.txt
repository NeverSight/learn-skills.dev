Use when building, driving or animating an assembly or mechanism in SolidCraft through its MCP server - components, grounding, joints and joint origins, rigid groups, motion links, closed loops such as an actuator on a hinged panel or a linkage, contact sets, motion studies, degrees of freedom - or when a drive moves nothing, stops early or a part jumps when it is joined. To model the parts themselves, use solidcraft-modeling.
