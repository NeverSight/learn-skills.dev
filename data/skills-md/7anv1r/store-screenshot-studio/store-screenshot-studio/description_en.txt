Designs eye-catching, art-directed App Store and Google Play screenshots for any app or game from its raw screen captures. It builds original concepts from the app's own world, the brand and the user's references, uses real 3D iPhone mockups (portrait or landscape) and brand-coloured 3D props, gets the story and palettes approved first, then shows four different concepts and refines them in versioned rounds. Finally it renders every store size (iPhone 6.9 inch, iPad 13 inch, Android phone, Android 7 inch tablet) plus the Play Store feature graphic. Use when the user asks to make, design, redo or resize app store screenshots, Play Store or App Store listing images, ASO screenshots, a feature graphic, or marketing phone mockups for an app or game. Do not use for designing the app's own UI or website hero images.
