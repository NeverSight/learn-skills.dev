Convert documents and URLs to clean Markdown using Microsoft MarkItDown. Use whenever you need to READ or extract content from Office and structured formats — .docx, .pptx, .xlsx/.xls, .csv, .html, .epub, .msg (Outlook email), .json, .xml, ZIP archives, or web/YouTube URLs — instead of writing ad-hoc extraction scripts. NOT for creating or editing Office files, and NOT for PDFs or images (read those natively).
