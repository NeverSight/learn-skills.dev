General online video and music download, media extraction skill. When a user expresses a download intent such as “download this/save this/download this” and provides an HTTPS URL, the skill will detect and download it even if they do not explicitly mention “video” or “music.” It includes built‑in adapters for WeChat Channels and Spotify tracks, and supports yt‑dlp extractors for YouTube, Bilibili, X/Twitter, Douyin/TikTok, Xiaohongshu, Instagram, Facebook, Vimeo, Twitch, Reddit, Weibo, AcFun, etc.; it also handles MP3s, subtitles, media info, and yt‑dlp updates. If a URL is pasted without a download intent, it will not automatically download. It never uses UI automation to operate WeChat, Xiaohongshu, or risk‑control verification pages.
