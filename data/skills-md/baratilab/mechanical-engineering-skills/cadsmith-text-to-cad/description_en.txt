Generates dimensionally exact CadQuery CAD from natural language with the CADSmith multi-agent method — Planner, RAG-assisted Coder, sandboxed Executor, kernel-metric + three-view vision Validator, and Refiner in two nested loops (≤3 error repairs, ≤5 geometric refinements). Use when the user wants a part generated from a text description with verified dimensions, a STEP/STL from a spec, or wants to benchmark text-to-CAD output (Chamfer distance, F1, IoU).
