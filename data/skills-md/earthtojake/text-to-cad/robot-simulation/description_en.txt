Robot and world descriptions (URDF, SRDF, SDF) for ROS, MoveIt and Gazebo. Use for ROS robot_description files (links, joints, limits, inertials, meshes), MoveIt planning groups, end effectors, group states and disabled collision pairs, Gazebo/gz models and worlds (sensors, plugins, physics), and opening or reviewing a `.urdf`, `.srdf` or `.sdf`. Not the 3D CAD part itself, which the `cad` skill makes, nor a part's structural or physics analysis, nor signed-distance fields.
