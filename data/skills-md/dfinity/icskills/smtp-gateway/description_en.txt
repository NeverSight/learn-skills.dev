Receive email in an Internet Computer canister through the SMTP Gateway Protocol. Covers implementing smtp_request and smtp_request_validate, addressing (user@<canister-id>.icp0.io, custom domains, handing mail to another canister with /.well-known/ic-smtp-canister-id), how the gateway turns reply codes into SMTP (5xx bounces, everything else retries, 550 rejects one recipient), what actually arrives (raw headers, raw MIME body, a per-attempt message_id, no sender authentication), the ic-smtp-gateway-protocol Rust crate, and the optional chunked upload for messages over 2 MiB. Use when a canister must receive or process inbound email, when implementing the SMTP gateway Candid interface, or when mail to a canister bounces, is retried, or never arrives. Do NOT use for sending email from a canister (the gateway is inbound only; call an email API with https-outcalls) or for serving HTTP from a canister.
