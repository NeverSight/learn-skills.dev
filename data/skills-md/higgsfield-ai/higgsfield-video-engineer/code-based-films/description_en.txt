Make a short animated or cinematic film (15–30 s) in which every frame is drawn by our own code — models, rigs, animation and simulations as JS programs, procedural textures in Python/numpy, rendered by the kit's own offline WebGPU path tracer on the local GPU (remote GPUs optional) and encoded with FFmpeg. Use for story-led character films, kids' craft-look films and cinematic shorts built with this kit, from story and beat sheet through assets, animatic, look, final render, sound, QA and delivery. Not for generated-video tools (text- or image-to-video), not for After Effects or Blender projects, and not for real-time or game-engine renders.
