yapyak, an i18n compiler with a small runtime: t() with the source string as the key, ICU plurals and selects, rich text, format.* for numbers, dates and lists, locale switching and persistence, yapyak.config.ts, translators, the yapyak CLI, and YAPnnnn diagnostics. Use when adding or editing user-facing text in a project that depends on yapyak, when setting up @yapyak/vite, @yapyak/react, @yapyak/vue, @yapyak/svelte, @yapyak/astro, @yapyak/nuxt, @yapyak/react-router, @yapyak/sveltekit or @yapyak/tanstack-start, or when a YAP diagnostic fires.
