Based on product images, retrieve visually similar or identical items from the Temu public marketplace, returning product, sales volume, price, reviews, inventory, supply price, store, and category data. It is triggered when the user explicitly requests Temu image search, Temu search for the same item, reverse image search on Temu, Temu visual product search, or provides an image/image URL and clearly wants to find similar products on Temu. Generalized image‑based product search requests for Temu that are not specified do not trigger automatically. This skill is not for cross‑platform sourcing from 1688, image recognition or editing, product detail history lookup, or keyword product search.
