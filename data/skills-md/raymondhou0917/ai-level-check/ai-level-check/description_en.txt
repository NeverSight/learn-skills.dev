Trigger conditions: the user says “help me do an AI capability assessment,” “analyze how I use AI,” “run this month’s AI usage report,” “what type of AI user am I,” or “help the team do a bi‑weekly AI usage inventory.”  

Do not trigger: pure teaching on how to write prompts, optimizing a single prompt, making performance‑evaluation decisions, or comparing two people to see who is stronger (this skill explicitly forbids ranking and percentile reporting).
