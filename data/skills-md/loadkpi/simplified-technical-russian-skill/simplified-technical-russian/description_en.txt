Simplifies and clarifies Russian technical text: one meaning per word, active voice, straight word order, no more than 20 words per sentence (UTR, GOST R 58049‑2017). Use it only if the text being simplified or created is written in Russian; the language of the request does not matter. Triggers: simplify, briefly, shorter, easier, clearer, in plain language, according to UTR. Do not apply to text in other languages.
