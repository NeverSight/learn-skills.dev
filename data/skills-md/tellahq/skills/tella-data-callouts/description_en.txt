Show the numbers a speaker says in a Tella video as animated graphics, through the Tella MCP, as big stats, before-and-after comparisons, bars, percentages and count-ups, each timed to the spoken number and checked against the transcript. Use when asked to visualize stats, metrics, results, growth, pricing or any numbers in a video, or for data callouts or animated charts. Builds on tella-motion-design. A single stat can be a text overlay; charts use the Tella Motion tools, which are in beta and not available in every workspace.
