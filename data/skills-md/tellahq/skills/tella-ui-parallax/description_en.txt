Turn a real product screenshot into a parallax shot in Tella Motion, through the Tella MCP, by cutting its pieces out with masks, stacking them as depth layers over the blurred, dimmed screen, and moving one camera through them so near layers travel further, with shadows, depth blur and an optional slight 3D turn. Nothing is redrawn, so it is quick and exactly the real UI. Use when asked for a parallax product shot, a 2.5D or 3D-feeling screenshot, to make a screenshot pop or pull a piece of the UI out, or for b-roll from a screenshot. To rebuild UI that animates its interaction, use tella-ui-redraw. Builds on tella-motion-design. Needs the Tella Motion tools, which are in beta and not available in every workspace.
