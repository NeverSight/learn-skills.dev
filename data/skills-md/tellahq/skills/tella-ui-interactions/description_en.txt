Recipes for animating product UI behaviour in Tella Motion through the Tella MCP, covering a button press, a menu or modal growing from what was clicked, a toggle, tabs, typing, a field that rejects or accepts, loading into content, a success check, notifications arriving, a sheet sliding up, a list filling and a number counting. Use with tella-ui-redraw when redrawn UI has to act, or when asked to animate a UI state change. Needs the Tella Motion tools, which are in beta and not available in every workspace.
