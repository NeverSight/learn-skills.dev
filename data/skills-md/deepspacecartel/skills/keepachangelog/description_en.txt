How to write and maintain a CHANGELOG.md following the Keep a Changelog 1.1.0 format (https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) - project-agnostic. Covers the six change categories, the Unreleased section, version/date headers, comparison links, and yanked releases. Use when creating a new CHANGELOG.md, adding an entry for a release, or reviewing one for format compliance.
