How to apply Semantic Versioning 2.0.0 (https://semver.org/) - project-agnostic. Covers the MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH rules, pre-release and build-metadata grammar, precedence rules for comparing versions, and the 0.y.z/1.0.0 FAQ. Use when deciding what the next version number should be, tagging a release, or reviewing whether a version bump matches what actually changed.
