Repairs AI-built screens that look generated. Measures them with the ai-design-check rules and checker, writes a short brief from the product, then works through usability, truth, copy, numbers, components, order and decoration before building the proof. Checks again and reports the counts before and after. Use when the user asks to fix, clean up or improve the design of a screen, or says it looks generated, vibecoded or sloppy. Keeps features, data and content.
