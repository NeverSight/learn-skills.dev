AI Engineering from Scratch provides AI-native mentors and onboarding processes for four independent Claude certification tracks. It is suitable for learners who wish to choose Claude certification, prepare for the CCAO‑F, CCDV‑F, CCAR‑F, or CCAR‑P exams, continue along certification pathways, learn the next lesson interactively, run and verify practical experiments, build and grade deliverables, participate in diagnostic or mock assessments, or bolster weak exam areas using Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT, Cursor, or other agents on GitHub.
