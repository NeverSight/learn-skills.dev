Shoot software UI like a film. A sequence of hero shots, each a steep, oblique, extreme close-up of the interface floating on a stage (a black void for dark products, a high-key studio for light ones), with a razor-thin focal plane, a slow motivated camera drift, and UI that acts on its own, hard-cut on the action, ending on one line of copy and a still logo. It also shoots stills, the same look frozen into single photographs. Use when asked for a launch video, product film, feature trailer, teaser, promo clip, hero shot, or any "cinematic" footage of an app, UI, or website, and also for a hero image, key visual, product photo, poster frame, launch-post image, Open Graph or social card, or any "cinematic" still of a UI. This skill covers camera, framing, light, and edit. It is stack-agnostic.
