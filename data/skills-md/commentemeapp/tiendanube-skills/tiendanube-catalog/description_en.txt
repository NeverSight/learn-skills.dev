Apply when writing code that reads or writes the catalog of a Tiendanube / Nuvemshop store through the Admin API: products, variants and their attributes, images, categories, stock and multi-location inventory (locations and inventory_levels), price tables, kits, custom fields and metafields, and localized fields such as name per language. Use for ERP or PIM catalog sync, bulk stock and price updates, variant reconciliation by SKU, or reviewing code that calls /products, /categories, /locations or /metafields.
