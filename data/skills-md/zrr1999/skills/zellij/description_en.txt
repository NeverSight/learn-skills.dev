Use Zellij to organize, restore, monitor, or automate persistent terminal workspaces. It is suitable for scenarios that require naming sessions, detaching/attaching, pane/tab orchestration, reusable layouts, parallel development of services and logs, querying runtime status, waiting for pane command results, restoring exited sessions, read‑only watch, or sharing the terminal via controlled web/remote attach. Focus on selecting Zellij capabilities according to your goals; precise commands, flags, and version differences must be obtained from the local `zellij --help` and the corresponding subcommand help. Do not use it for single ordinary commands, system service management, or CI orchestration.
