Review AI's progress and output without relying on the AI itself. When the AI says “it’s done / it’s correct,” verify whether it’s truly so; after a lengthy conversation, if it seems to drift away from the original intent, it’s no longer what you wanted; if the AI constantly praises your ideas, suspect it’s just agreeing; or when the AI gives a lot of suggestions and you don’t know which to trust, use this approach. Compare each point against the original goal and independent evidence, and if needed, compile the established requirements into a brief, start a new conversation, and begin again. When a problem can’t be articulated clearly, first use zp‑question; use zp‑judge for decisions on whether to proceed.
