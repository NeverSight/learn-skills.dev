Review AI’s progress and output without relying solely on the AI itself. When the AI says “it’s done/it’s correct,” verify whether that’s true; after a long conversation you may feel it’s drifting further away and no longer matches what you originally wanted; the AI keeps saying your ideas are good, and you suspect it’s just agreeing with you; or the AI gives a lot of suggestions and you don’t know which one to trust—use this approach. Compare each point with your original intent and independent evidence, checking them one by one; if necessary, compile the agreed‑upon requirements into a brief and start a new conversation. If the question is still unclear, first use zp‑question; use zp‑judge to decide whether to proceed.
