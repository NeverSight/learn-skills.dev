Use this skill when writing or revising explanatory long-form prose that should be read continuously, such as articles, essays, chapters, tutorials, and narrative technical explanations, especially when the content is correct but feels cognitively flat, uniformly dense, or difficult to keep reading. Shape the progression of claims, observations, qualifications, questions, and resolutions without changing meaning or manufacturing drama. Do not use it for reference documentation, specifications, transactional writing, terse notes, or text whose primary purpose is rapid lookup rather than continuous reading.
